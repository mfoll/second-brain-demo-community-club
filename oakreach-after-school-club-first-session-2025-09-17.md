# Oakreach After-School Club First Session 2025-09-17

The first Wednesday after-school athletics club run by club coaches at Oakreach School, Wednesday 17 September 2025, 15:30 to 17:00 on the school track.

- Run by Kwabena Osei with Sheila Mortimer helping for the first three weeks. Luke Garvey, head of PE, was there for the first session.
- 22 pupils came. Year totals are in [[oakreach-after-school-club-summary]].
- Session plan: running drills, relay games, standing long jump, a short timed run. Kept deliberately playful for the younger pupils.
- The school keeps the register and its own safeguarding procedures apply on its site; our coaches follow them as well as ours.

This is the club's side of the track swap: free Tuesday evening track use for our athletics section in return for this club in term time ([[oakreach-track-agreement]]).
