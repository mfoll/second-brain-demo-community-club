# Volunteer Rota Overhaul

Moving the volunteer rota off paper sheets in the pavilion and a sprawling group chat onto the Clubfold rota, so everyone can see two weeks ahead who is doing what.

## Outcome

The paper and group-chat rota replaced by the Clubfold rota, with every match-day and training slot visible two weeks ahead. Reached on 29 August 2025.

## Current State

- Completed. The Clubfold rota has run every week since the start of the 2025-26 season.
- About 140 slots a month across match days, training, tea hut, line marking and kit.
- Fill rate two weeks out averaged 81% in 2025-26, against a guessed 50-60% under the old system.
- Only [[nisha-raghavan]] and I can edit rota templates. That single-point risk is tracked in [[who-takes-over-the-clubfold-rota-admin]], not here.

## Scope and Boundaries

Covered the rota only: slot types, templates, reminders and swaps. Membership, payments and background-check records also live in Clubfold but were set up by [[ravinder-sahota]] and are not part of this project. The opt-out fee and pledge were a separate decision ([[family-volunteer-pledge]]).

## Working Context

The old system was a laminated sheet on the pavilion door, a spreadsheet only I could find and a group chat of 140 people where requests scrolled away within an hour ([[volunteer-rota-2024-25-group-chat]]). Coaches regularly turned up on Saturday with no tea hut and no linesman.

We trialled the Clubfold rota with the under-9s and under-10s from 10 May 2025 ([[clubfold-rota-pilot-2025-05-10]]). Feedback ([[clubfold-rota-trial-feedback-2025]]) was mostly positive, with two complaints: too many reminder emails, and grandparents without smartphones being left out. We cut reminders to one at 48 hours and kept a printed weekly sheet on the pavilion door, which Helen Marsh updates on Fridays.

Rejected options: a shared spreadsheet (we had one; nobody opened it) and a separate rota app (another login for families who already struggle with one).
