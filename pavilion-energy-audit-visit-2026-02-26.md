# Pavilion Energy Audit Visit 2026-02-26

Walk-round energy audit of the pavilion, Thursday 26 February 2026, by Marek Zielinski and a club parent who works as an electrician. Not a formal audit; a list of what we found.

- Changing room and corridor lights: old fluorescent tubes, some on all day because nobody can find the switch. Replace with LED tubes and a motion sensor (about £600).
- Water heater runs 24 hours. A timer to heat only on training and match days (about £90).
- Meeting-room heater has no thermostat. Replace or add one (about £150).
- Roof: south-facing slope over the meeting room could take panels, but the roof patch from 2025 is only good for about five years ([[pavilion-roof-repair]]).
- Draughts round the 1974 doors; not worth fixing on a building we hope to replace in part.

Marek will price the timer and LED tubes from the maintenance budget. Bigger ideas are in [[pavilion-energy-ideas-list]].
