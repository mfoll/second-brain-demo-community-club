# Tournament Should Stay Community-Sized

## Stance

The summer tournament should stay at about 52 teams or fewer. Growing it further would earn more money but turn a club weekend into an event the club serves.

## Scope

The summer junior tournament at Millrace Lane. Not a view on other fundraising.

## Why I Think This

- 52 teams is the site's limit for decent playing time ([[tournament-planning-meeting-2026-03-24]]).
- Parking broke at 52 ([[summer-tournament-2026-debrief]]). Every extra team is roughly two or three more cars.
- 58 of 64 volunteer slots filled; the same people did the most.
- Neighbours tolerate one busy weekend a year if it is well run. They will not tolerate a bigger one run badly.

## What Would Change My Mind

A second ground for a weekend, such as Oakreach's field and car park, with its own volunteers. Then a bigger event might be safe.

## Implications

Fundraising growth has to come from better catering, sponsorship and raffles, not more teams.
