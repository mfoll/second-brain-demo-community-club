# Relative age effect

## Summary

Explains the relative age effect: in selective youth sport, and in education, more participants than expected are born early in the selection year, season or school year. The bias is strongest at the higher levels of youth sport.

## Why It Matters

Tom Ashworth brought it up at the [[coaches-forum-2025-11-19]] when parents of under-8s asked for league tables. It is the main argument in [[should-under-7s-play-festivals-not-fixtures]] and behind the [[mini-football-festival]] format: early results reward the oldest in the age group, and we want every child to keep playing.

## Caveats

The article is about selection at higher levels. A grassroots club that picks no one is not the same setting; we use it as a caution, not as evidence about our teams. Article last edited 2026-09-21 when checked; licence CC BY-SA 4.0.
