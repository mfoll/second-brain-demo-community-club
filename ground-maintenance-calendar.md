# Ground Maintenance Calendar

Gareth's month-by-month plan for the pitches and outside jobs, which Marek added the pavilion checks to in August 2025.

## Summary

- August: mark out for the season; set goals and nets; check line marker and mower service.
- September to April: weekly mow (fortnightly in deep winter), weekly line marking, divot repairs after each match weekend.
- October: roof check from the ladder (Marek); gutters cleared; fire extinguishers serviced.
- November to February: watch pitch 2 after heavy rain; call-off decisions by 08:30 on match days.
- March: pavilion spring clean with volunteers; fence check.
- May to June: end-of-season renovation (scarify, seed, top dress, roll); goalmouths rebuilt.
- July: rest the pitches; mower service; asbestos register re-check.

## Why It Matters

This is the job list that would need covering if Gareth stepped back, and the starting point for a maintenance plan for the new building ([[who-maintains-the-new-changing-rooms]], [[new-building-maintenance-plan-outline]]).
