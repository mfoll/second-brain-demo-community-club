# Chair Succession Plan

Handing the chair on properly. I have chaired since the 2019 AGM and the 2025 constitution means I step down at the AGM on Saturday 12 June 2027. This project makes sure someone is ready, chosen fairly and not left to find things out the hard way.

## Outcome

A new chair, or co-chairs, elected at the AGM on 12 June 2027 after at least six months of shadowing me.

## Current State

- [[femi-oyelaran]], vice-chair, has shadowed me since 1 September 2026: he co-signs board papers, chairs the October and November meetings with me beside him, and sits in on calls with the council and the trust ([[femi-shadowing-starts-2026-09-01]]).
- On 8 September 2026 [[hamza-qureshi]] proposed co-chairs, Femi and himself, with the work split between them ([[hamza-co-chair-proposal-2026-09]]). Femi prefers a single chair with a strong vice-chair. I lean towards co-chairs. The board decides on 8 December 2026: [[should-fernhollow-have-co-chairs]].
- The constitution allows co-chairs only if the AGM agrees; Joanna has checked the wording ([[fernhollow-constitution-2025]]).
- The chair's job is written down for the first time ([[chair-role-description-2026]], [[what-the-chair-actually-does]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

This covers the chair only. The treasurer gap is a separate problem with its own Question ([[who-will-take-over-as-treasurer]]). Nobody is promised the role: the AGM elects, and any member may stand. I will not stay on the board after June 2027 ([[should-hazel-stay-on-the-board-after-2027]]).

## Working Context

[[colin-brightwell]] chaired from 2008 to 2019 and handed over to me in about three weeks. We both remember how much I had to find out by ringing him. That conversation in January 2026 ([[coffee-with-colin-2026-01-16]]) is the reason this project exists. The approach is [[succession-by-shadowing]]: real responsibility early, with the outgoing chair present but quiet.

The co-chair question is a fair disagreement. Hamza argues the job is too big for one volunteer with a full-time job; Femi worries that two chairs means nobody is sure who decides. Both are right about something ([[co-chairs-can-work-if-the-split-is-written-down]]).

## Next Actions

- [ ] Write both models side by side for the 8 December board, including who signs what and who the council rings.
- [ ] Ask ten or so members informally what they think before December.
- [ ] Complete the handover file by the end of March 2027.
- [ ] Hand Femi (or the co-chairs) the lead on the Lanternfield Trust relationship from January 2027.
- [ ] Put the chair election on the 2027 AGM timetable with nominations closing 29 May ([[agm-2027-planning-notes]]).
