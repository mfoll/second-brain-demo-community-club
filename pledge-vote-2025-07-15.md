# Pledge Vote 15 July 2025

The July 2025 board, moved back a week from the usual second Tuesday because of the end-of-season presentations, voted 7-2 to make the [[family-volunteer-pledge]] compulsory from September 2025.

## What Was Decided

- Every junior family to cover three volunteer sessions per season.
- Families who could not would pay a £30 opt-out, added to their fees in Clubfold.
- Coaches, team managers and board members counted as having met the pledge.
- Review in January 2026.

## The Discussion

The paper ([[pledge-board-paper-2025-07]]) was mine, written with [[donovan-clarke]] after a spring with three Saturdays without a tea hut and one under-12s match nearly called off for want of a linesperson. The arithmetic looked easy: about 150 junior families, three sessions each, more than covers the 140 or so slots a month.

The two votes against were [[ravinder-sahota]], who worried about families working weekend shifts, and [[sheila-mortimer]], who said the athletics parents already did timekeeping and should not be charged for not doing football slots. We exempted athletics-only families in response, which in hindsight should have told us something about one-size rules.

## Looking Back

I believed in this at the time. The January figures showed it did not work as hoped, and it was partly reversed at the [[board-meeting-2026-02-10]]. [[pledge-lessons-learned]] is where I wrote up why.
