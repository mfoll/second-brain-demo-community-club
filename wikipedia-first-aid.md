# First aid

## Summary

The English Wikipedia article on first aid: the immediate help given to someone with a medical emergency to preserve life, stop the condition getting worse or support recovery until professional help arrives, usually by someone with basic training. The lead also mentions extensions of the idea such as mental health first aid and psychological first aid. Last edited 12 October 2026 at the time of the record.

## Why It Matters

I pointed [[yusuf-demir]] to it in October 2025 when we rewrote the "first-aider on duty" role for the rota. Its three aims (preserve life, prevent worsening, promote recovery) are a good plain brief for a match-day first-aider who is a parent, not a professional: act, keep the player safe, get help. The role now asks for a current first-aid certificate and a short club briefing, and Yusuf keeps the six kits stocked ([[match-day-roles-overview]]).

## Caveats

General overview. Our first-aiders follow their certificate course, not this article.
