# Masters athletics

## Summary

Explains masters athletics: athletics for people aged 35 and over, in five-year age groups, across track and field, road and cross-country running. High-level championships exist, but many masters athletes are newcomers who enjoy the social side.

## Why It Matters

Sheila pointed me to it when I asked how to describe our masters group to the board. The point about newcomers and camaraderie is how our group of about 12 works, and why it is a natural base for marshals and pacers if we ever start [[community-running]].
