# Volunteer Thank-You Evening Budget 2025

Budget and actual spend for the [[volunteer-thank-you-evening-2025-11-21]], from the expenses return I sent to [[claire-whitlow]] on 24 November 2025.

## Key Details

| Item | Budget | Actual |
|---|---|---|
| Food and soft drinks | £250 | £236 |
| Long-service gifts (three engraved mugs) | £60 | £54 |
| Certificates and cards | £30 | £22 |
| Hall costs (pavilion, so none) | £0 | £0 |
| Contingency | £110 | £98 (extra food and a cake) |
| **Total** | **£450** | **£410** |

No alcohol was bought by the club; there was a bring-your-own arrangement for adults.

## Why It Matters

The recognition budget is £450 a year, and this was nearly all of it. Any new recognition ideas ([[volunteer-recognition-ideas]]) have to fit within it or replace part of the evening. Outside catering for 2026 was quoted at about £320 for 40 people, which would leave little else.
