# Lease

English Wikipedia article, last edited 21 September 2026. Licence CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Summary

The article describes a lease as a contract under which a user pays the owner for the use of an asset, such as land, a building, a vehicle or equipment. The owner is the lessor and the user the lessee; the lessee gets the right to use the asset for regular rent and agrees to conditions on how it is used. The lead gives the example of a car leased on condition that it is used only privately.

## Why It Matters

I read it the night before the first meeting with council estates in November 2025, mostly to get the vocabulary straight: who is the lessor, what the conditions mean, why the landlord cares about use. Our lease is exactly that bargain: the council owns Millrace Lane, we pay rent and accept conditions about sport and community use ([[millrace-lane-lease]]). It helped me see that the length and the conditions are what we were really negotiating, not the rent ([[pitch-lease-extension]]).

## Caveats

General background only. Lease law depends on the jurisdiction; the club's solicitor advises on our actual lease.
