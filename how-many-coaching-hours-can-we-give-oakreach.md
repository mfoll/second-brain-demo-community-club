# How many coaching hours can we give Oakreach?

## Question

If the school's review of the track arrangement asks for more from us, how much more coaching can the club actually give?

## Why It Matters

The school agreed on 6 October 2026 to keep the free arrangement until July 2027 while both sides review it. We give about 99 coach hours a year through the Wednesday club; the Tuesday track use we receive would cost about £3,150 at the proposed £45 an hour. The review may ask for a second weekly session, holiday camps or coaching for the school team.

## Current thinking

Kwabena can do Wednesdays and no more. Sheila could add a few holiday sessions. A second weekly session would need a third licensed coach, which we do not have. So the honest answer today is: the same hours, possibly a holiday camp, and help with the school's athletics meeting.
