# Fernhollow Sports and Youth Club Constitution 1998

> [!warning]
> Superseded on 14 June 2025 by [[fernhollow-constitution-2025]]. Kept for history and for the reasons behind the 2025 changes; do not quote it as current.

## Summary

The constitution adopted in 1998, when the club added athletics and became the Fernhollow Sports and Youth Club. Six pages, eleven clauses. It replaced the 1981 founding rules of Fernhollow Youth Football Club.

## Key Details

- Board of "up to seven", which had quietly grown to nine by 2019.
- Board quorum four.
- No limit on terms of office. Colin chaired for eleven years under it, and I was on my way to the same.
- No mention of safeguarding, a welfare officer, junior members' voice or conflicts of interest.
- Financial year to 31 December; accounts "audited by a member", which in practice meant a friendly parent.

## Why It Matters

It is the before picture for the [[constitution-review-2025]]. When someone asks why the 2025 rules are so specific, the gaps here are usually the answer.
