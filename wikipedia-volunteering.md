# Volunteering

## Summary

The English Wikipedia article on volunteering. Its lead defines volunteering as an optional, freely chosen act of giving time and work, often for the community, and notes that some volunteers bring specialist training while others help as needs arise. Last edited 16 October 2026 at the time of the record.

## Why It Matters

I first read it in February 2025, starting the [[volunteer-rota-overhaul]], mainly to get the vocabulary right for a board paper. I came back to it a year later when the pledge was being reviewed: "optional and freely chosen" is the plain reason a compulsory pledge sat badly with families ([[should-volunteering-be-a-condition-of-membership]]).

## Caveats

A broad overview. It does not cover small clubs or the mix of parents and non-parents we rely on.
