# Succession by Shadowing

Handing over a role by having the likely successor do the real job for months while the outgoing holder is still there, present but quiet, rather than by briefing them in the last few weeks.

It is the volunteer version of ward handover practice: you do not hand a patient over with a note and walk off; the next nurse does the round with you first. For the chair, it means the successor chairs real meetings, signs real letters and takes real calls, and the outgoing chair answers only when asked.

## How we are doing it

- [[femi-oyelaran]] chairs the board from September 2026, with me beside him ([[femi-shadowing-starts-2026-09-01]]).
- He is copied on all chair correspondence and leads the trust relationship from January 2027.
- My handover notes are written for him and for anyone else who might stand ([[what-the-chair-actually-does]]).

## Pitfalls

- It can look like anointing a successor. The AGM elects, and the co-chair question is still open ([[should-fernhollow-have-co-chairs]]).
- The outgoing person has to actually let go. I have to stop myself answering for Femi.
- Six months is a minimum. Colin's three weeks in 2019 was not enough ([[coffee-with-colin-2026-01-16]]).
