# Should we run a community fun run?

## Question

Should the club put on an annual fun run, or a monthly untimed community run, at Millrace Lane?

## Current Answer

Deferred until the [[match-funding-appeal]] closes. A route exists without roads ([[fun-run-route-measurement]]), the masters group would marshal ([[masters-group-running-interest-list]]) and 16 survey respondents wanted to run. What we lack is a run director who is not already doing another job, and I will not ask the volunteers who carried the tournament to take on another event while the appeal is still running. An annual spring fun run could raise perhaps £1,500 to £2,500 on entries of £8 to £10; a monthly community run would raise nothing but bring in adults.

## Revisit when

The appeal reaches £23,000, or someone volunteers to lead it.
