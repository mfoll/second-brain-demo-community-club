# Pavilion Roof Repair Finished, 18 July 2025

The roofer finished replacing the felt and flashings over the meeting room and kitchen on Friday 18 July 2025, after two weeks on site from 7 July.

## Notes

- Final cost £6,800 as quoted, paid from reserves on Claire's approval.
- Marek inspected from the ladder with the roofer and signed off. Ten-year workmanship guarantee received and filed with the lease papers.
- Two rotten fascia boards found and replaced within the price.
- The changing block roof was not touched (asbestos cement sheets, see [[asbestos-survey-report-2025]]).
- Gareth cleared the gutters the same week.

The meeting room was repainted by volunteers on 26 July and the board met there again in August. Project closed: [[pavilion-roof-repair]].
