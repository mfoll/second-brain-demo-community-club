# Colin Brightwell Email on Succession

## Summary

Email from [[colin-brightwell]], 3 February 2026, following our coffee in January ([[coffee-with-colin-2026-01-16]]). His list of "things a new chair needs and won't find written down".

## Key Details

Summarised from his list:

- The names of the people at the council who actually answer, and which ones to copy in.
- The pavilion's quirks: the boiler timer, the stopcock, the alarm code holders.
- The families who have been with the club for three generations and expect to be asked, not told.
- How to handle a parent's complaint at the touchline (listen, do not decide there, write it down).
- Which governing body deadlines carry fines.
- A reminder that the chair's job is to make sure things get done, not to do them.

## Why It Matters

Most of this went straight into my handover notes ([[what-the-chair-actually-does]]). Personal details of the people he named stay out of the vault.
