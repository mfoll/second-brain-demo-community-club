# Brian Coyle

Owner of Coyle Motors, the garage on the Draymoor road, and the club's kit sponsor since 2024.

## How we work together

Coyle Motors pays £1,500 a year towards kit for the junior teams, with its name on the shirts ([[coyle-motors-sponsorship-agreement]]). In August 2026 Brian also gave a one-off £1,500 to the changing-rooms [[match-funding-appeal]], separate from the sponsorship. He likes a photo of the teams in the new kit each autumn and a mention in the newsletter; anything involving juniors' photos goes through the club's consent rules first.

## Current threads

- The sponsorship runs to the end of the 2026-27 season. [[claire-whitlow]] and I want to renew for three years before Christmas.
- He has asked whether his name could go on the new changing rooms. That is for the board and the trust to decide, not something to promise.
