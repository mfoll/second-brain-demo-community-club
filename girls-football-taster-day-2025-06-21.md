# Girls Football Taster Day 2025-06-21

Free girls-only football taster morning, Saturday 21 June 2025, 10:00 to 12:00 on the mini pitches. Organised by Leanne Fairbairn with three players from the adult women's team.

- 23 girls aged 7 to 13 came, 15 of them with no club experience. Most heard through school book bags and the residents' association newsletter.
- Format: warm-up games, small-sided matches, no scores, squash and certificates at the end. Parents stayed pitchside.
- 14 girls signed up for a girls' team for September 2025. The other nine mostly said "not this year" or had Saturday clashes.
- What worked: female coaches, friends coming together, no pressure to sign anything on the day.
- What did not: nothing for under-7s, two of whom came with older sisters and had to watch.

This day is the starting point of the [[girls-football-section]]. Leanne wants to repeat the format, with something for the youngest next time ([[girls-only-entry-sessions]]).
