# Chair (officer)

## Summary

English Wikipedia article (last edited 23 October 2026 when rechecked). The chair (also chairperson, chairman or chairwoman) is the presiding officer of a board, committee or assembly, usually elected or appointed by its members, who presides over meetings and makes sure business is conducted in an orderly way.

## Why It Matters

Read after Hamza tabled the co-chair proposal ([[board-meeting-2026-09-08]]). The core definition is narrower than the job I have ended up doing: presiding over meetings and keeping business orderly. Much of the rest (council calls, grant work, complaints) could belong to other officers. That supports both sides of [[should-fernhollow-have-co-chairs]]: Femi's case that a strong vice-chair can carry the load, and Hamza's that the extra work could be split between two people.

## Caveats

General definition; nothing on co-chairs in a small volunteer body.
