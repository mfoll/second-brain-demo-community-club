# Should Fernhollow Have Co-Chairs?

Should the club elect two co-chairs at the AGM on 12 June 2027, or a single chair with a strong vice-chair? The board decides on 8 December 2026.

## Why It Matters

I step down in June 2027 under the 2025 constitution ([[fernhollow-constitution-2025]]). How the role is shaped decides who can realistically take it on, and the members will vote on whatever the board recommends.

## The Options

**Co-chairs (Hamza's proposal, 8 September 2026).** [[hamza-qureshi]] proposed that he and [[femi-oyelaran]] share the chair: Femi leading the board, football and the council; Hamza leading communications, fundraising and the trust ([[hamza-co-chair-proposal-2026-09]]). His reasons: the job as I have done it averages 10 to 12 hours a week and more in grant season; both candidates work full time; a club should not pick its chair by who has the most spare time; and a pair gives some resilience if one person has to step back.

**Single chair with a strong vice-chair (Femi's preference).** Femi argues that two chairs blur who decides and who the council, the trust and an anxious parent should ring; that the board already has officers who can take work off the chair; and that the real fix is to give the vice-chair and secretary clear jobs. He has said he would stand as sole chair and would want Hamza as vice-chair.

## Current Answer

> [!question]
> Not decided. I lean towards co-chairs, provided the split is written down and one of them is named as the contact for the council and the trust. The board deferred on 13 October so members could be sounded out first.

My reasons are in [[co-chairs-can-work-if-the-split-is-written-down]]. Joanna confirmed clause 7.5 allows joint chairs if the AGM agrees. [[colin-brightwell]] is sceptical but accepts the job has grown.

## What Would Settle It

- What members say when asked informally in November.
- Whether the split can be written so that every regular task has one named owner.
- Whether Femi would be content as co-chair; a model he does not want will not work.
