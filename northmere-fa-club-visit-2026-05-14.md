# Northmere FA Club Visit 2026-05-14

Vikram Joshi, club development officer at Northmere FA, visited Millrace Lane on Thursday 14 May 2026. Hazel, Leanne Fairbairn and Donovan Clarke.

- Walked the pitches and the pavilion. He said the changing rooms are the weakest he has seen at a club our size and that separate junior and officials' rooms would make our grant case stronger. Passed to Marek for the design.
- Offered three funded places on women-only coach education courses in 2026-27. We earmarked one for Ellie Tranter and two for future female coaches.
- Advised that the girls' under-14s enter the league this September rather than wait a year: the league has room and a team that only trains loses players.
- On the under-16s: if numbers fall, the association can approve a joint team with another club for one season.
- Reminded us that every coach needs a current qualification for league entry. To go into the season planning starting in June ([[season-2026-27-plan]]).
