# Independent Examiner's Report 2025

## Summary

[[farah-ansari]]'s report on the 2025 accounts, signed 22 May 2026 ([[accounts-signed-off-2026-05-22]]). No matters of concern. A separate management letter makes two recommendations.

## Key Details

1. **Cash at the tea hut and events:** two people should count takings and sign a count sheet before cash leaves the pavilion. On two occasions in 2025 a single volunteer banked takings a week late.
2. **Reserves policy:** the club relied on a 2019 board minute ("keep about three months' costs"). Write it down, say what reserves are for, and review it yearly.

## Why It Matters

Both were acted on. The board re-adopted a written [[club-reserves-policy]] in April 2026, and Helen Marsh now runs the tea hut with a paired count sheet. The reserves point became sharper a month later when the board committed £25,000 to match funding ([[board-meeting-2026-05-12]]).
