# Match Funding

Match funding is the part of a project's cost that the applicant raises itself, alongside a grant that will only pay a share. For the new changing rooms it is the £63,000 the club must find for the Lanternfield Trust to pay its £185,000.

## Definition

> [!abstract]
> A funder pays up to a fixed percentage of eligible costs and expects the rest from the applicant's own money, other grants or donations, secured or credibly pledged before the award. The Lanternfield Trust pays up to 75%; our build costs £248,000, so we must show £63,000.

General background in [[wikipedia-matching-funds]]. Funders use it to check that a project has local backing and that the applicant has a stake in it.

## How our £63,000 splits

| Part | £ | Status in October 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Club reserves | 25,000 | Committed by the board on 12 May 2026 ([[board-meeting-2026-05-12]]) |
| Draymoor Community Chest | 15,000 | Awarded 20 August 2026 |
| Members' and community appeal | 23,000 | £14,600 raised by 20 October 2026 ([[match-funding-appeal]]) |
| **Total** | **63,000** | £54,600 secured or committed |

The appeal includes £5,000 from the summer tournament ([[summer-tournament-2026-debrief]]) and a one-off £1,500 from Coyle Motors ([[brian-coyle]]).

## What it means for the club's own money

- Reserves fall from about £31,000 to about £6,000 when the £25,000 is paid at contract signature, well below the three months' costs in our [[club-reserves-policy]]. The 2026-27 budget plans a £5,800 surplus to start rebuilding ([[club-budget-2026-27]]).
- The fee rise was partly justified by match funding: the board wanted to show the trust that members contribute through fees as well as donations ([[board-meeting-2026-03-10]]). Ravinder's objection was that this put a capital cost on families least able to pay.
- If the appeal falls short, the board has said it will not take more from reserves. The fallback is a smaller contingency in the build or a second Community Chest bid.

## Pitfalls we have learned

- A funder counts pledges only if they are written and dated. Claire keeps the evidence for each part.
- Volunteer time does not count towards this fund's match, although some funders allow it.
- Money raised for the appeal must be kept separate in the accounts so it is not spent on running costs.
