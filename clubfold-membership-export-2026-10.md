# Clubfold Membership Summary October 2026

## Summary

Membership counts from Clubfold's summary report, run by [[ravinder-sahota]] on 15 October 2026. Counts only; member records stay in Clubfold.

## Key Details

- 220 members: 161 juniors and 59 adults.
- Juniors 2025-26 at the same point: 168. Seven fewer.
- Juniors who did not renew: 22. New juniors: 15.
- Of the 22 non-renewals, 9 were in the under-15s and under-16s; 6 moved away or changed club by their own account; 4 gave cost as a reason in the leaver form; the rest gave none.

- Fees paid in full: 148 juniors; on Clubfold card instalments: 13.
