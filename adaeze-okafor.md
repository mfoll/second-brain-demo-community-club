# Adaeze Okafor

Headteacher of Oakreach School. Final say on the school's side of the track-for-coaching swap.

## Role in our work

- Agreed the swap in summer 2025 on Luke Garvey's recommendation ([[oakreach-track-agreement]]).
- On Tuesday 6 October 2026 agreed to honour the free arrangement until July 2027 while both sides review it, after her business manager proposed a lettings fee ([[oakreach-lettings-meeting-2026-10-06]]).
- Wrote a short letter of support for the changing rooms grant, mentioning the after-school club ([[letters-of-support-changing-rooms]]).

## How we work together

We have met three times. She is direct, has little time, and wants to see what pupils get out of things rather than hear about the club's needs. In October she said she valued the after-school club but had to be able to show governors that school assets are not given away. I took that as fair and as the shape of the review: show both sides' value on paper.
