# Email From Rebecca Thorne on Changing Rooms June 2026

[[rebecca-thorne]]'s reply of 23 June 2026 to [[aoife-brennan]], copied to me, on the draft interim changing-room rules. Summarised; the email is in Aoife's safeguarding folder.

## Key Points

- The draft rules are proportionate for shared facilities that cannot be changed quickly.
- She endorsed the staggered slots and the two-adults-outside arrangement as common practice in clubs with older pavilions.
- She suggested adding the "if only one door adult, juniors change at home" fallback, which we did.
- She recommended officials always change separately and offered the meeting-room solution herself.
- No phones in changing rooms: she recommended it as a club-wide rule, not just for juniors.
- She asked that the rules be reviewed after half a season.
- She noted that the new building brief ([[changing-room-design-requirements]]) matched the association's facility guidance and offered a letter of support for the grant if useful.

## Why It Matters

Gave the board confidence to adopt the interim rules, and the offered support letter went into the grant application pack ([[changing-rooms-grant-application]]).
