# AGM 13 June 2026

Annual general meeting, Saturday 13 June 2026, 10:30, Millrace Lane pavilion. 52 members present.

## What happened

- **2025 accounts approved** after [[claire-whitlow]] took questions on the roof spend and reserves ([[annual-accounts-2025-statement]]).
- **Fees noted:** the 2026-27 fees set by the board in March were reported, with the bursary explained. One member asked why fees should fund a building; Claire answered that the fee rise covers running costs and rebuilds reserves, and that the building is funded separately ([[match-funding]]). Ravinder's dissent was referred to in the chair's report, as he had asked.
- **Officers re-elected** under the transitional clause. I told members this would be my last full year and that Femi would start shadowing me in September ([[chair-succession-plan]]).
- **Changing rooms:** [[marek-zielinski]] presented the revised design with separate junior rooms; the quote was due at the end of June.
- **Match funding:** the board's £25,000 reserves commitment was reported and the appeal announced for June.

## Afterwards

The appeal raised £1,100 in pledges at the AGM itself, mostly in tea hut cash. Papers in [[agm-2026-papers]].
