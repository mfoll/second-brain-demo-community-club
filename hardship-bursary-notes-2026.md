# Hardship Bursary Notes 2026

What we have learned from the first seven months of the bursary, from conversations with [[ravinder-sahota]]. Counts and patterns only.

- **How families asked:** 6 through a coach, 5 by email to the club, 3 by approaching Ravinder at registration. Coaches passed requests on without asking anything, as the criteria say ([[hardship-bursary-criteria]]).
- **When:** five in spring after the fee letter; most of the rest in late August and September when the payment was due.
- **Who:** 14 awards across 11 families; three families have two children.
- **Kit allowance:** most used it for boots; two for away-match travel.
- **What surprised Ravinder:** several families asked for less than the full award. One paid the full fee the following month and asked for the money to go to another family.
- **What worries him:** families who would qualify but will not ask. He thinks some of the four cost-related leavers were among them ([[did-the-fee-rise-cost-us-members]]).

For the January review: whether to top up the remaining £720, and whether these patterns favour a sliding scale ([[should-fees-use-a-sliding-scale]]).
