# Can the Recruitment Drive Reach Twelve by December?

## Question

The [[volunteer-recruitment-drive-2026]] aims for twelve new regular volunteers by 31 December 2026. At seven in October, will it get there, and does the number matter as much as which roles are filled?

## What We Know

- Seven new regulars from February to October: roughly one a month, with bursts after the taster morning and the volunteer centre fair ([[recruitment-drive-progress]]).
- 64 different adults have done one-off slots, so the pool exists.
- November and December are slow months: cold Saturdays, Christmas.
- Two people are "maybe after Christmas", one of them for the rota admin role.

## Current Thinking

Probably nine or ten by December, twelve by the end of the season. I would rather report that honestly to the board on 8 December than stretch the definition of "regular". Nisha's view is that the roles matter more than the count: a rota admin and a deputy fixtures secretary would be worth more than five extra tea hut helpers.

## What Would Answer It

The count reported to the board on 8 December 2026, and the board's decision whether to carry the drive into 2027 with a target by role instead of headcount.
