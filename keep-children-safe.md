# Keep Children Safe

The club has 161 junior members and the adults around them. This area covers how we keep them safe: the safeguarding policy, the welfare officers, training and background checks, codes of conduct and the practical rules for changing rooms, photos and travel. It never ends, and it is the one area where I would rather be boring and thorough than quick.

## Scope

- Policy and process only. Concerns about a child or an adult go to the welfare officer through the confidential route in [[report-a-welfare-concern]]. **No case, name or detail of a concern is ever written in this vault.**
- The welfare officer ([[aoife-brennan]]) and deputy ([[beatriz-ferreira]]).
- Safeguarding training and background-check status for volunteers, recorded in aggregate here and individually only in Clubfold ([[clubfold-volunteer-records]]).
- Codes of conduct, photo and social media guidance, changing-room supervision.
- Not here: coaching qualifications, which sit under [[run-the-season]]; the design of the new changing rooms, which sits under [[new-changing-rooms]], although safeguarding requirements feed into it.
