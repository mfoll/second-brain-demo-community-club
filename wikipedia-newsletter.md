# Newsletter

## Summary

Defines a newsletter as a printed or electronic report on an organisation's activities sent to its members, customers or subscribers, or a personal communication of opinions or interests.

## Why It Matters

Hamza cited it in his paper for the 10 February 2026 board, alongside the [[communications-audit-2026-02]], to argue for a members' newsletter as the one regular channel for club news. That proposal became part of the [[club-communications-plan]].

## Caveats

A definition, not evidence that newsletters work. Our own open rates are the evidence. Article last edited 2026-08-07 when checked; licence CC BY-SA 4.0.
