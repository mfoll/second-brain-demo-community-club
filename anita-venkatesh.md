# Anita Venkatesh

Sport and leisure officer at Draymoor Borough Council. Our day-to-day council contact for pitch hire, events on council land and community sport funding.

## Role in our work

- Sets the season's pitch hire charges and allocations; told us in January 2026 that pitch hire would rise 12% for 2026-27 ([[pitch-hire-schedule-2026-27]]).
- Encouraged us to apply to the Draymoor Community Chest and read our draft; the £15,000 award on 20 August 2026 owes a lot to her advice ([[community-chest-award-2026-08-20]]).
- Handles event permissions. After the July tournament she passed on the Residents' Association parking complaint and set the stewarded parking plan as a condition for 2027 ([[council-event-conditions-letter-2026-08]]).
- Wrote a supportive internal note for the lease report to cabinet.

## How we work together

Helpful, busy and careful about what she can promise. She answers email within a week and prefers a phone call for anything awkward. She separates her sport development role, where she is openly an ally, from council decisions, where she sticks to process. I respect that and try not to put her in a difficult position, for example by quoting her to the Gazette.
