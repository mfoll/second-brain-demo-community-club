# Duty of care

## Summary

The English Wikipedia article on duty of care in tort law: a legal obligation to take reasonable care to avoid careless acts that could foreseeably harm others, and the first element a claimant must show in a negligence case. Last edited 23 July 2026 at the time of the record.

## Why It Matters

The phrase kept coming up in the policy review, so I read the article in June 2026 to understand what people meant by it. The practical point for us: the club owes care to everyone on its activities, including its own young helpers. That is the reasoning behind not using 16- and 17-year-olds as the second adult ([[should-under-18-helpers-count-in-supervision-ratios]]). It also fed the board's interest in the legal standing of an unincorporated club, which sits with [[club-incorporation-review]].

## Caveats

A legal article, general and not specific to any country's law or to sport. It is background reading, not legal advice; the club takes advice through its governing bodies.
