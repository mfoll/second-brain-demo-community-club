# Qualified Coach Per Team

The club's minimum standard: every team has at least one named coach with a current governing body coaching qualification and current safeguarding training and background check. It is also a Northmere FA league requirement from 31 December 2026 ([[northmere-fa-league-handbook-2026-27]]).

Why we hold to it even when it is inconvenient: a qualified coach plans sessions rather than running matches in training, knows the age-appropriate formats, and knows when to stop. Unqualified helpers are welcome alongside, never instead.

The cost is real. Each introductory course is £95 and two Saturday mornings, and the club pays. In 2026-27 the standard is met by 12 of 13 football teams, with the under-12s coach on the November course. The athletics equivalent is the licensed coach rule on the [[northmere-athletics-club-licence-2026]].
