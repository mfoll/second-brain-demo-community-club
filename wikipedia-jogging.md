# Jogging

## Summary

Defines jogging as running at a slow or easy pace, mainly for fitness with less strain than faster running, and a form of aerobic endurance training over longer distances.

## Why It Matters

A small but useful point for the [[community-running]] idea: most of the 16 adults who said they wanted to run regularly are joggers, not athletes. Any community run should be pitched at that pace, untimed or with walkers welcome, rather than as an athletics event.
