# Welfare Officer Needs a Deputy

## Stance

A club with juniors should always have a named deputy welfare officer, trained to the same level, so that the confidential route never depends on one person.

## Scope

Any club of our size. Very small clubs may have to share a deputy with a neighbouring club, but the principle holds.

## Why I Think This

- When I was welfare officer from 2011 to 2019 there was no deputy. Holidays, illness, and any situation where the concern involved someone close to me all left the route uncertain.
- The 2022 policy still had a single welfare officer. Making [[beatriz-ferreira]] deputy in 2025 was one of the first things [[aoife-brennan]] asked for, and the 2026 draft names the role.
- A concern raised about the welfare officer must have somewhere else to go.

## What Would Change My Mind

Nothing I can think of. I hold this firmly.

## Implications

- The deputy role is in the [[volunteer-role-descriptions]] and in the revised policy.
- Succession for welfare: Aoife to 2028, Beatriz possibly after, so we need to start looking for the next deputy in 2027.
