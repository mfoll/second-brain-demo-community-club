# Role-Based Volunteering

Recruiting volunteers by asking for a specific, bounded job rather than for "help". The approach [[nisha-raghavan]] brought to the club in 2026.

## The Idea

A general appeal ("we need more volunteers") is heard by everyone and answered by the people already doing too much. A specific ask ("can you be car park steward for the under-10s home games, about two hours, six Saturdays before Christmas?") gives a person something they can picture fitting into their week, and a clear point at which they have done enough.

Each role has a written description with the task, time, who you work with, whether a check is needed, and what happens if you cannot make it ([[volunteer-role-descriptions]]). Alongside regular roles sit one-off slots: single sessions with no commitment beyond the day. One-off slots act as a low-risk way in; some people move on to a regular role once they know the club.

## How It Differs From What We Did Before

| | Before 2026 | Role-based |
|---|---|---|
| The ask | General appeal, group chat | Specific role, personal ask |
| Commitment | Unclear, tends to grow | Written, with hours |
| Who answers | The usual few | A wider pool |
| Handover | Nothing written | Description exists |

## Evidence So Far

Seven new regulars and 64 one-off volunteers between February and October 2026 ([[recruitment-drive-progress]], [[one-off-slots-uptake-2026]]). Three of the seven started in one-off slots. Small numbers, but better than the compulsory [[family-volunteer-pledge]] it replaced.

## Limits

Descriptions need maintaining each season. Personal asks take time, mostly Nisha's. Some roles, such as team manager, resist being boxed in.
