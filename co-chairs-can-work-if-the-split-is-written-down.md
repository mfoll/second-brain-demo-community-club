# Co-Chairs Can Work If the Split Is Written Down

## Stance

Co-chairs would suit Fernhollow better than a single chair, provided every regular task has one named owner and one of the two is the named contact for each outside body.

## Scope

Our club, with two candidates who both work full time. Not a general view that co-chairs are better.

## Why I Think This

- The job averages 10 to 12 hours a week ([[chair-role-description-2026]]). I could do it because I am retired. We should not need the next chair to be retired.
- Hamza's split is mostly sensible ([[hamza-co-chair-proposal-2026-09]]).
- Two people means cover for illness, work trips and the odd bad season.

## What Would Change My Mind

Femi's worry is the strongest argument against: if the council, the trust or a parent does not know who decides, the club looks muddled. If the split cannot be written so that never happens, a single chair with a strong vice-chair is better. And if Femi does not want to be a co-chair, it will not work at all. That is why this is marked Revising.

## Implications

The board paper for 8 December should be a task-by-task table, not a principle ([[should-fernhollow-have-co-chairs]]).
