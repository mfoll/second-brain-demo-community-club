# Northmere Athletics Club Licence 2026

The club's annual athletics licence from Northmere Athletics, renewed on 3 April 2026 for April 2026 to March 2027.

- Licence fee £180, paid from the athletics budget.
- Conditions: at least one licensed coach present at every junior session; a named welfare contact (Aoife Brennan); insurance for training at third-party venues, which covers the Oakreach track.
- Coaches on the licence: Sheila Mortimer and Kwabena Osei. A third would let the junior squad split into two groups.
- Events on roads or public paths that are timed or ranked need a separate permit; untimed runs may not, depending on route. Orla Keane's advice is to ask before advertising anything.

Why it matters: it is the reason Tuesday sessions stop if neither licensed coach can come, and the first check for any [[community-running]] event.
