# New Building Maintenance Plan (Outline)

The outline maintenance plan written for the grant application in September 2026. Marek and I drafted it; it needs filling in before the building opens.

## Running costs (estimate, per year)

- Electricity and water for showers: £1,100.
- Cleaning, one paid clean a week (about £30) in season: £600 after volunteer cover in the summer.
- Insurance uplift: £400.
- Total about £2,100, from the general fund.

## Sinking fund

£1,000 a year from 2028, held separately, for the bigger items: shower valves, the roof covering at 20 years, redecoration every five years.

## Checks

- Weekly (match days): rooms left clean, showers off, doors locked, as part of [[open-and-close-the-pavilion]].
- Monthly: water temperatures, shower heads descaled, fire doors and signs ([[pavilion-fire-risk-assessment-2025]]).
- Annually: inspection by Marek or a successor, written report to the board.

## Gaps

Who does it. See [[who-maintains-the-new-changing-rooms]]. A small facilities team of three, with a role description from Nisha, is the likely answer.
