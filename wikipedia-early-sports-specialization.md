# Early sports specialization

## Summary

Describes early sports specialization: children or teenagers training intensively in one sport all year instead of playing several. It is associated with injuries, mental health problems and psychosocial harm, and many such athletes quit early.

## Why It Matters

Kwabena shared it when two athletes in the junior squad were also in three football sessions a week. It supports our informal rule that athletics and football coaches talk to each other about juniors who do both, and our refusal to run extra elite sessions for under-12s. Relevant to [[youth-participation]].

## Caveats

Describes intensive, year-round training, which is rare at our level. The risk for us is overload from several clubs at once, not elite specialisation. Article last edited 2026-10-08 when checked; licence CC BY-SA 4.0.
