# Pitch Hire Schedule 2026-27

The council's letter of 20 January 2026 setting the club's pitch hire for the 2026-27 season at Millrace Lane, with the tariff sheet and the instalment dates.

## Summary

- Total pitch hire for 2026-27: £11,200, up from £10,000 in 2025-26, a 12% rise across the council's tariff.
- Covers both full-size pitches and the three mini pitches from September to May, including marking paint but not the club's own mowing and maintenance.
- Paid in three instalments: September, January and April.
- The letter says the tariff will be "reviewed in line with the community asset review" for 2027-28.

## Why It Matters

The 12% rise was the main cost pressure behind the fee review ([[membership-fee-review-2026]], [[board-meeting-2026-03-10]]). It is also one reason the 30-year lease looks good on paper: the heads of terms replace pitch hire with a rent of £1,500 a year plus our own maintenance ([[millrace-lane-lease]]). Claire's comparison of the two is in [[club-budget-2026-27]].
