# Sliding Scale Fees

Charging different prices for the same membership according to what members can afford, usually in a few bands. The member chooses a band, either on trust or with some evidence. A higher "supporter" band from those who can pay more helps balance the lower one.

## Compared with a bursary

| | Sliding scale | Flat fee plus bursary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asking for help | Ticking a box on a form | A conversation with someone |
| Predictable income | Less: depends on take-up | More: fixed fee, capped fund |
| Admin | Setting bands once | Each award handled |
| Privacy | High, if bands are on trust | Depends on how carefully awards are kept |
| Fairness to the middle | Can feel unfair to those just above a band | Same fee for everyone not helped |

## For Fernhollow

[[ravinder-sahota]] proposed £120, £165 and £190 for juniors ([[ravinder-sliding-scale-proposal]]). The board chose a flat fee and the [[hardship-bursary-fund]] in March 2026 but agreed to look again in January 2027 ([[should-fees-use-a-sliding-scale]]).
