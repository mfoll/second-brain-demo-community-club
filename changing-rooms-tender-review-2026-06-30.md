# Changing Rooms Quotes Reviewed, 30 June 2026

The board's facilities sub-group (Marek, Claire, Femi and me) met in the pavilion on Tuesday 30 June 2026 to choose between the three quotes.

## Decision

Hutchins & Kerr Building at £248,000, fixed to 31 March 2027. Unanimous. Reported to the July board, which confirmed it.

## Discussion

- Femi asked us to look properly at the modular option, at £229,000. We did: no officials' room, asbestos removal excluded, design life of 20 to 25 years. Once the officials' room and asbestos were added back, the saving fell to a few thousand pounds and the design life still fell short of the trust's 25-year horizon ([[value-engineering]]).
- The second local builder was £271,500 with a provisional sum for asbestos; not competitive.
- Claire wanted the fixed-price date in writing; Marek will ask whether it can be extended if the grant slips ([[will-the-hutchins-kerr-quote-hold-until-the-grant-decision]]).
- Nobody is to tell Hutchins & Kerr they have the job until the grant and lease are secure; Marek told Dale the club "intends to proceed subject to funding".

Project [[changing-rooms-design-and-quotes]] closed after this meeting.
