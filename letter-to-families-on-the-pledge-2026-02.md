# Letter to Families on the Pledge February 2026

My letter to all junior families, sent through Clubfold on 15 February 2026, after the board scrapped the pledge opt-out on 10 February.

## Summary

The letter said plainly that the compulsory pledge had not worked as hoped and that the board had listened. It explained the three changes: no more £30 opt-out, the pledge kept as a hope rather than a rule, and a new way of asking for help through role descriptions and one-off slots. It explained that the £930 already paid would not be refunded but would go to a confidential fund to help families who struggle with fees.

## Key Details

- About 380 words; checked by [[nisha-raghavan]] and [[ravinder-sahota]].
- Thanked families who had volunteered and those who had paid.
- Apologised to families who had felt pressured.
- Linked to the new [[volunteer-role-descriptions]] and the taster morning on 7 March.

## Response

Eleven replies: nine relieved or supportive, two asking if they could still help without being on a rota. No complaints about the refund decision.

## Why It Matters

The public record of the reversal, and the wording to reuse if the club ever has to explain a change of course.
