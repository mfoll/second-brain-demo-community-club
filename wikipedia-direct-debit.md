# Direct debit

## Summary

English Wikipedia article (last edited 14 April 2026 when rechecked). A direct debit is a payment the payer authorises in advance, letting the payee collect varying recurring amounts straight from the payer's bank account; it is common for utilities, loans and subscriptions. The payee's bank collects the money once the payer has given the authority, and it is also known as a pre-authorised debit or payment.

## Why It Matters

[[claire-whitlow]] circulated it before the trial began in October 2025 so the finance subgroup understood what families would be signing up to. The key point for us was that the family authorises once and the club then collects each month, which is why failed collections land on the treasurer to chase ([[direct-debit-pause-2026-01-27]], [[is-direct-debit-worth-the-bank-charges]]).
