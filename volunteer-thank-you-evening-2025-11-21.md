# Volunteer Thank-You Evening 21 November 2025

Our annual thank-you evening in the pavilion, Friday 21 November 2025, 19:00 to 22:00.

## What Happened

- 41 people came: volunteers, a few partners and two former volunteers.
- Food by [[helen-marsh]] and two helpers, which rather defeated the purpose of thanking Helen. Next year we cater it.
- Cost £410 against a budget of £450 ([[volunteer-thank-you-evening-budget-2025]]).
- Long-service mentions for three volunteers with ten or more years, read out by me.
- [[colin-brightwell]] came and said a few words about the club in the 1990s, which went down well.

## What I Learned

- About a third of regular volunteers did not come. When Nisha asked, several said a Friday evening is the hardest time for them, and two said they do not like being thanked in public.
- The evening thanks the people who already feel part of the club. It does not reach the occasional helpers we most want to keep.

This led to [[how-do-we-recognise-volunteers-without-money]] and the list in [[volunteer-recognition-ideas]]. The 2026 evening is planned for late November with outside catering.
