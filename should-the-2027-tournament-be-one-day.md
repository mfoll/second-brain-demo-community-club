# Should the 2027 tournament be one day?

## Question

Should the 2027 summer tournament shrink to a single Saturday, or stay as two days with a proper parking plan?

## Why It Matters

The tournament raised £8,900 net in 2026 and is the biggest event in the [[match-funding-appeal]]. The council will not confirm a 2027 date until it accepts a stewarded parking plan ([[council-event-conditions-letter-2026-08]]), and we need stewards for every hour the site is busy.

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> Not decided. I lean towards keeping two days but with fewer teams on the Saturday, because the Sunday older age groups bring fewer cars per team and Saturday was where the parking broke.

## Arguments so far

- **One day (Sam's first instinct):** half the stewarding, one set of toilets, less wear on volunteers, no wet-Sunday risk. But at most about 30 teams fit in a day, so net income would drop to perhaps £5,000 to £5,500.
- **Two days, smaller Saturday (my leaning):** cap Saturday at 22 teams, keep Sunday at 24, use the Oakreach car park on Saturday if the school agrees. Keeps most of the income.
- **Two days as 2026:** only if the parking plan works and we can fill 64 volunteer slots, which we did not quite manage this year.

## What would settle it

Whether Oakreach will lend its car park, and how many stewards Nisha thinks we can realistically find. Sam wants a decision by the January board so entries can open on 1 April.
