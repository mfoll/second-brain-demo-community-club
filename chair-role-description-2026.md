# Chair Role Description 2026

## Summary

A two-page role description for the club chair, written by me and [[joanna-pryce]] in February 2026 in the format [[nisha-raghavan]] uses for volunteer roles ([[volunteer-role-descriptions]]). Approved by the board on 10 March 2026 as a document for candidates.

## Key Details

- **Purpose:** leads the board, speaks for the club, keeps decisions moving and makes sure people are looked after.
- **Main tasks:** chair ten board meetings and the AGM; agree agendas with the secretary; be the club's contact for the council, the Lanternfield Trust, Oakreach School and the governing bodies; support the welfare officer; sign off major spending with the treasurer; deal with complaints that reach board level.
- **Time:** about 10 to 12 hours a week averaged over the year, more in grant season and around the AGM.
- **Not the chair's job:** coaching rotas, kit, fixtures, money handling. In practice I do too much of some of these, which the role description is meant to stop.
