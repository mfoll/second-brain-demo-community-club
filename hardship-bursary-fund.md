# Hardship Bursary Fund

A confidential fund so that the 2026 fee rise does not stop any child playing. It was the compromise that let the board agree fees on 10 March 2026.

## Outcome

A confidential fund of £2,400 for 2026-27 paying half fees for up to 20 juniors (14 awarded by October 2026).

## Current State

- 14 bursaries awarded by 13 October 2026, £1,680 committed, £720 left ([[bursary-awards-tracker]]).
- Each award is £120: half the £165 junior fee (£82.50) plus £37.50 towards kit or travel to away fixtures ([[hardship-bursary-criteria]]).
- Funded by the £930 of 2025-26 family pledge opt-out money, which the board moved to the bursary on 10 February 2026 ([[board-meeting-2026-02-10]]), and £1,470 from the season budget.
- [[ravinder-sahota]] decides awards with one other board member, currently [[claire-whitlow]]. Nobody else knows which families.

## Scope and Boundaries

> [!danger]
> No family, child or award detail ever goes into this vault, board papers or minutes. Only counts and totals leave Ravinder and Claire. Applications and decisions stay in the restricted Clubfold record ([[can-the-bursary-stay-confidential-in-clubfold]]).

## Working Context

We kept it simple on purpose. A family asks a coach, Ravinder or the club email; Ravinder has a short conversation, not a means test; the fee adjustment goes into Clubfold under a code only he and Claire can see ([[how-to-award-a-hardship-bursary]]). Coaches are told only that "the fee is sorted".

Ravinder still argues that a sliding scale would be fairer and less awkward for families than asking ([[should-fees-use-a-sliding-scale]]). He may be right; the first season of figures will tell us. My own view is in [[money-should-never-be-why-a-child-stops-playing]].
