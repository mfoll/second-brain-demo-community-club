# Changing Rooms Design, Revision C (May 2026)

Neil Cartwright's revised drawings for the new changing rooms, issued on 19 May 2026 after the first Saturday changing-room observation on 2 May. Plan, two elevations, a site plan and a one-page design note. Supersedes [[changing-rooms-design-brief-2025]].

## Summary

A single-storey block of about 136 square metres on the footprint of the 1974 prefab and the grass strip beside it, joined to the pavilion by a covered walkway. Four changing spaces with separate entrances from a shared lobby.

## Key Details

- Junior room A and junior room B, each with benches for 16, two showers and a WC. Each has its own door from the lobby, so the two can be used by different age groups or by girls and boys at the same time.
- Officials' room with its own shower and WC, sized for three referees.
- Accessible changing room with a level shower, a WC and a fold-down bench; step-free from the car park.
- Store for goals and nets; cleaner's cupboard.
- No windows at eye level into any changing space; obscured high-level glazing only.
- Lobby visible from the pavilion kitchen hatch, so a supervising adult can see who comes and goes without entering a room.

## Why It Matters

Revision A (2025) had one large junior room and one adult room, which repeated the old overlap problem. This revision is what the safeguarding review asked for ([[changing-rooms-design-revision-2026-05-19]], [[safeguarding-policy-review]]) and what the grant application leans on. The footprint grew by about 18 square metres and the estimate by about £21,000; see [[separate-junior-changing-is-worth-the-extra-cost]].
