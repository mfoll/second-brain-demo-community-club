# Co-Chair Proposal

## Summary

[[hamza-qureshi]]'s three-page board paper, circulated 1 September 2026 for the board on 8 September ([[board-meeting-2026-09-08]]). Proposes that the board recommend co-chairs to the 2027 AGM under clause 7.5 of the constitution.

## Key Details

- Starts from the 10 to 12 hours a week in the [[chair-role-description-2026]] and asks who can give that alongside a full-time job.
- Proposed split: [[femi-oyelaran]] leads board meetings, football, the council and the school; Hamza leads communications, fundraising, sponsorship and the Lanternfield Trust.
- Shared: the AGM, complaints, safeguarding escalations with the welfare officer.
- Both stand for election as a pair, but the AGM may elect either alone.
- A review after one year.
- Hamza names himself as a candidate and says so plainly; he would support Femi as sole chair if the board prefers.

## Why It Matters

The starting point for [[should-fernhollow-have-co-chairs]]. Femi's response, given at the meeting rather than in writing, is in the Question.

## Caveats

The split puts the trust relationship with Hamza, while Femi has been shadowing me on it since September. That needs resolving whichever way the board goes.
