# Oakreach Partnership History

How the club came to train at Oakreach School, for anyone who needs to explain it to a new person at the school.

- Before 2025: athletics trained on grass at Millrace Lane and hired the Oakreach track for about ten Saturday sessions a year at the lettings rate.
- Spring 2025: Luke Garvey mentioned to Sheila that the school had lost its after-school athletics club when a teacher left. Sheila brought it to me.
- June 2025: Sheila, Luke and I sketched the swap over coffee: our coaches run the Wednesday club; we get Tuesday evenings on the track.
- 28 August 2025: the letter of agreement signed by the head and me ([[oakreach-track-agreement]]).
- 17 September 2025: first after-school session ([[oakreach-after-school-club-first-session-2025-09-17]]).
- July 2026: Kwabena's year-end summary: 33 sessions, about 25 pupils a session, four pupils joined the club ([[oakreach-after-school-club-summary]]).
- 22 September 2026: new business manager proposes £45 an hour.
- 6 October 2026: head agrees to keep the arrangement free until July 2027 while both sides review it ([[oakreach-lettings-meeting-2026-10-06]]).

What I would do differently: write the value of both sides into the first letter.
