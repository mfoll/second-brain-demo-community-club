# Annual Accounts 2025

## Summary

Receipts and payments accounts for the calendar year 2025, prepared by [[claire-whitlow]], independently examined by [[farah-ansari]] and approved at the AGM on 13 June 2026 ([[agm-2026-06-13]]).

## Key Details

Rounded figures from the statement:

- Receipts £45,200: fees £34,600 (including £930 pledge opt-outs, held separately), tea hut and bar £3,000, summer tournament 2025 £4,300 net, Coyle Motors £1,500, small grants and donations £1,800.
- Payments £49,900: pitch hire £10,000, pavilion roof £6,800 ([[pavilion-roof-repair]]), referees £5,300, kit £4,400, utilities and maintenance £6,100, affiliations and insurance £3,700, courses and checks £2,900, Clubfold £1,900, other £8,800.

## Why It Matters

The reserves figure here is the "about £31,000" in every match-funding discussion ([[match-funding]]). Without the roof, the club would have had a surplus of about £2,100.

## Caveats

Receipts and payments, not accruals: fees paid in advance for 2026 sit in 2025. Claire explains this every year and every year someone asks.
