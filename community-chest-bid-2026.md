# Draymoor Community Chest Bid 2026

## Summary

[[claire-whitlow]]'s application to the Draymoor Community Chest, the council's small-grants scheme, submitted 30 June 2026 for £15,000 towards the changing-rooms match funding. Awarded in full on 20 August 2026.

## Key Details

- Asked for the scheme's maximum for capital projects, £15,000.
- Evidence: the Hutchins & Kerr quote of £248,000, the board's £25,000 reserves commitment, the appeal plan, and a letter of support from the ward councillor, [[rhys-penhallow]].
- Outcomes promised: changing rooms that let junior, adult and girls' teams change separately; an accessible room; officials' room.
- Conditions of award: spend within two years of award; release on evidence that the Lanternfield grant and lease are secured.

## Why It Matters

£15,000 of the £63,000 [[match-funding]]. The release condition ties it to the same lease problem as the main grant ([[pitch-lease-extension]]).
