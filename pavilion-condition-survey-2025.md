# Pavilion Condition Survey, Spring 2025

Marek's room-by-room walk-round of the pavilion and changing block after the February storm, written up for the April 2025 board. Six pages with photos.

## Summary

- Meeting room and kitchen: structurally sound; roof felt failed; ceiling tiles stained; electrics checked and safe.
- Changing block: one shared space with two showers and a single WC; shower tray cracked; extractor fan dead; the partition that was meant to split the space was removed years ago; no step-free access; no separate room for officials.
- Car park: potholes at the entrance; drainage gully blocked.
- Outside: fencing along the lane side needs two new panels.

## Why It Matters

Marek's conclusion was blunt: the pavilion is worth keeping and repairing; the changing block is not. That led directly to the board starting [[new-changing-rooms]] on 7 April 2025, and to the limited scope of the [[pavilion-roof-repair]].
