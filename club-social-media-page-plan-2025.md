# Club Social Media Page Plan 2025

A plan from May 2025 to make the club's public social media page the main way of telling members about fixtures and news, with team pages under it. Superseded by the [[club-communications-plan]] in February 2026 and kept for the reasoning.

Why it was dropped: in the [[members-survey-2025]] only 8% preferred social media for club news, several families do not use it at all, and posting fixture details publicly would show where junior teams play and when. Hamza's audit also found the page had not been updated since 2024 because nobody owned it. The public page survives for results and photos, run by Linh Pham.
