# Summer Tournament 2025

The 2025 summer tournament, Saturday 5 and Sunday 6 July 2025 at Millrace Lane. Sam Okonjo's first year as organiser.

- 44 teams from under-7 to under-14. Dry both days.
- Net £6,200 to general funds ([[summer-tournament-2025-accounts]]). The tea hut took £4,300.
- 52 volunteer slots, 47 filled, on the old paper and group-chat rota. Two people did four shifts each.
- Parking: cars on the Millrace Lane verges on Saturday, no complaint received.
- Sam's own notes: entries closed too late (mid-June), and the schedule printout had two pitch clashes on Saturday morning.

Sam's suggestions for next year, from his own debrief notes: open and close entries earlier with a cap, move the volunteer slots onto the new Clubfold rota, review the £50 entry fee, and decide early where the money goes. Parking was not on his list. The 2026 event is [[summer-tournament-2026]].
