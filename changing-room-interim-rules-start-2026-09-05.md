# Changing Room Interim Rules Start 5 September 2026

First Saturday of the 2026-27 season under the interim changing-room rules ([[changing-room-supervision-rules-2026]]).

## How It Went

- Changing slots were published in Clubfold with the rota on the Sunday before. [[martin-goss]] set them from the fixture list.
- Two door adults were on duty for every junior slot, booked as a role through [[run-the-saturday-changing-room-rota]]. [[beatriz-ferreira]] did the first shift with me.
- The adult men's team waited ten minutes outside for the under-16s to finish. Their captain grumbled and then helped keep the corridor clear.
- The referee changed in the meeting room, which he said was the best facility he had been given all season.
- One phone was out in the room; a quiet word sorted it.

## What We Adjusted

- Door adults wear the yellow volunteer bibs so juniors and parents know who they are.
- A laminated notice on the changing-room door with the slot times and the no-phones rule.
- The slot times must be set with fixtures, not added afterwards. This is the main review item for half term.
