# Board Meeting 12 May 2026

Monthly board, Tuesday 12 May 2026. Nine present (Aoife ex officio).

## The reserves decision

The board agreed to commit £25,000 of reserves to the changing-rooms [[match-funding]], payable when the building contract is signed. Vote 7-0, with [[sheila-mortimer]] abstaining. Sheila's concern, minuted: athletics has its own needs (hurdles, a storage container) and should not be forgotten while the building takes everything.

[[claire-whitlow]] set out the consequence: reserves fall from about £31,000 to about £6,000, below the three months' running costs in the [[club-reserves-policy]]. The board accepted that for a once-in-50-years building and asked her to plan a rebuild over three seasons ([[how-much-reserve-should-the-club-keep]]).
