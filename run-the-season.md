# Run The Season

Everything that has to happen for 13 football teams, a junior athletics squad and a masters group to train and play from September to July: coaches, fixtures, pitch slots, events and the summer tournament. It has no end date because there is always another season. Club communications sit here too, because most of what parents need to hear is about the season.

## Scope

- Football: 11 junior teams from under-7s to under-16s (including the girls' under-11s and girls' under-14s), the adult women's team and the adult men's team. Section lead: [[donovan-clarke]].
- Athletics: the junior squad (ages 9 to 17) and the masters group, training on the Oakreach track on Tuesdays. Section lead: [[sheila-mortimer]]. The track arrangement itself sits with [[school-partnership]]; this area holds the coaching side.
- Fixtures, referees and pitch slots, with [[martin-goss]] as fixtures secretary.
- Club events: the summer tournament, taster days, presentation evening.
- Club communications: newsletter, channels and who runs each one ([[club-communications-plan]]).
- Not here: safeguarding policy and background checks ([[keep-children-safe]]), the pavilion and lease ([[look-after-the-ground]]), fees ([[manage-club-money]]).

## Current State

- The 2026-27 season started on Saturday 5 September 2026. 161 juniors registered, down from 168 last season. See [[season-2026-27-team-list]].
- 12 of 13 football teams have a coach with a current qualification; the under-12s coach is booked on the Northmere FA course in November ([[season-2026-27-plan]]).
- The girls' section has 31 girls across two teams and two female coaches ([[girls-football-section]]).
- The 2026 summer tournament raised £8,900 net, £5,000 of it to the changing rooms appeal ([[summer-tournament-2026-debrief]]). The council wants a stewarded parking plan before it agrees the 2027 date.
- Saturday mornings are tight: three mini pitches, two full-size pitches, and one shared changing room that the adult teams also need ([[saturday-pitch-timetable-2026-27]]).
