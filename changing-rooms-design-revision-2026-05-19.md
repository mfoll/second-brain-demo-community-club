# Changing Rooms Design Revised, 19 May 2026

Neil Cartwright issued revision C of the changing rooms drawings on Tuesday 19 May 2026, giving juniors two separate rooms and adding an officials' room.

## How it came about

- On Saturday 2 May, the first of Aoife Brennan's three Saturday observations ([[changing-room-observation-2026-05-16]]), Neil and Marek joined Aoife, Beatriz and me at the changing block from 08:30 to 11:00, the busiest time. The under-14s and under-16s were still changing after early games when the adult men arrived for an 11:00 kick-off; the referee changed in his car. Nothing improper happened, but the design gave nobody a good option.
- Aoife's written requirements followed on 20 May, after the third observation, and confirmed what revision C already did ([[changing-room-design-requirements]]).
- Aoife's view, which went into the policy review: separation has to be by design, with supervision as the backstop, not the other way round.
- The board agreed the extra cost at its meeting on 12 May 2026, the same night it committed £25,000 of reserves ([[board-meeting-2026-05-12]]).

## What changed

- One junior room became two, each with its own entrance.
- An officials' room with its own shower was added.
- The accessible WC became a full accessible changing room.
- High-level glazing only; lobby visible from the kitchen hatch.

Estimate up about £21,000, footprint up about 18 square metres. See [[changing-rooms-design-v2-2026-05]] and [[separate-junior-changing-is-worth-the-extra-cost]].
