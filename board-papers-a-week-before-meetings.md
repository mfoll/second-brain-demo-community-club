# Board Papers a Week Before Meetings

## Stance

Any decision that needs a paper needs it a week before the meeting. If the paper is late, the decision waits a month.

## Why I Think This

- Before 2023 papers arrived the night before or on the table. Decisions went to whoever talked most confidently.
- With a week, people like [[ravinder-sahota]] come with numbers, and the debate is about the substance. The fee debate in March 2026 was hard but well informed because the options paper went out on 24 February ([[fee-review-options-paper-2026]]).
- Volunteers read papers on the bus and at the touchline. They need time.

## What Would Change My Mind

A genuine emergency. The board can decide anything urgent by email between meetings, as the constitution allows, and minute it next time.

## Implications

[[joanna-pryce]] sends papers on the Tuesday a week before. Late papers go to the next meeting. See [[how-to-run-a-board-meeting]].
