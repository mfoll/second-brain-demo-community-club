# Club Incorporation Review

Deciding whether the club should become an incorporated charitable body before it signs a 30-year lease and a £248,000 building contract.

## Outcome

A board recommendation on whether to become an incorporated charitable body before signing a 30-year lease and a £248,000 building contract.

## Current State

- Idea only. Raised by [[marek-zielinski]] at the board on 8 September 2026 and opened as a project on 14 September ([[board-meeting-2026-09-08]]).
- The club is an unincorporated members' association. In practice the officers who sign the lease and the building contract could be personally exposed if things went badly wrong ([[personal-liability-in-an-unincorporated-club]]).
- [[celine-dubois]] at the Draymoor Volunteer Centre sent a short briefing on the options ([[incorporation-options-briefing]]).
- No lead yet. Nobody on the board has done this before.

## Scope and Boundaries

A recommendation, not the conversion itself. It must not delay the lease or the grant: the Lanternfield Trust deadline for lease evidence is 15 December 2026, and the trust has said a change of legal form during the application is possible but needs notice.

## Working Context

Arguments raised so far. For: the officers signing a 30-year lease are currently the legal face of the club; an incorporated body can hold the lease and the contract itself. Against: cost, a new set of reporting duties for a treasurer we are already struggling to replace, and the risk of doing it in a hurry. Claire's view is that the timing is wrong but the direction is right.

## Next Actions

- [ ] Find a lead volunteer, ideally someone outside the board with legal or charity experience.
- [ ] Ask the Lanternfield Trust how a change of legal form would affect an award.
- [ ] Ask the council's estates team whether the lease could be assigned to a new body later ([[graham-sutcliffe]]).
- [ ] Bring a scoping paper to the board in January 2027.
