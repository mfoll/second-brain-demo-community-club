# Volunteer Centre Fair 25 April 2026

A stall at the Draymoor Volunteer Centre spring volunteering fair, Saturday 25 April 2026, offered free by [[celine-dubois]].

## What Happened

- [[nisha-raghavan]], [[ellie-tranter]] and I ran the stall from 10:00 to 14:00.
- 37 people took a leaflet; 14 left contact details; six booked a one-off slot in the following fortnight.
- Two became regular volunteers by June: a groundsman's helper and a newsletter co-editor.
- Most interest came from people without children at the club: retired people and two students looking for coaching experience.

## Notes for Next Time

- Ellie talking about coming back as a volunteer after playing here was the best thing on the stall.
- The role descriptions printed on one card each worked better than a leaflet.
- People without children assumed they would not be welcome; say clearly that they are.
- Safer recruitment needs explaining up front, because people who are not parents ask why a check is needed ([[safer-recruitment]]).
