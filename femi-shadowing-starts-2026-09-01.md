# Femi Starts Shadowing the Chair

Tuesday 1 September 2026. [[femi-oyelaran]] began shadowing me formally, as agreed at the July board.

## What we agreed

- Femi chairs the September, October and November board meetings, with me beside him to answer if asked.
- He is copied on all chair correspondence with the council, the Lanternfield Trust and Oakreach School.
- We meet for an hour every other Sunday to go through what is coming up, using my handover notes ([[what-the-chair-actually-does]]).
- From January 2027 he leads on the trust relationship.
- Nothing about this commits the club to Femi as chair. The AGM elects, and the co-chair question was still to come ([[should-fernhollow-have-co-chairs]]).

The approach is in [[succession-by-shadowing]].
