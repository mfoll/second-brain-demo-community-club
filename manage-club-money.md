# Manage Club Money

The club's money: the budget, membership fees, the hardship bursary, the accounts, how members pay and our sponsorship. It never ends, and it is the area where one mistake costs us trust fastest, so I keep it tidy even though [[claire-whitlow]] does the real work as treasurer.

## Scope

- The season budget and the calendar-year accounts ([[club-budget-2026-27]], [[annual-accounts-2025]]).
- Membership fees and how we set them ([[membership-fee-review-2026]], [[fee-history]]).
- The confidential [[hardship-bursary-fund]], run by [[ravinder-sahota]]. Only counts and totals appear here, never families.
- Payments through Clubfold and the bank ([[direct-debit-collection]], [[monthly-finance-check]]).
- Reserves and the club's share of the changing-rooms [[match-funding]].
- Sponsorship ([[sponsorship-overview]], [[brian-coyle]]).
- Not here: the match-funding appeal itself is run under [[look-after-the-ground]] as [[match-funding-appeal]]; tournament takings are reported by [[summer-tournament-2026]].

## Current State

- Turnover about £48,000 a year. Pitch hire from the council went up 12% for 2026-27 ([[pitch-hire-increase-letter-2026]]).
- 2026-27 fees: juniors £165 (from £140), adults £210 (from £190), set by a 6-1 board vote on 10 March 2026 ([[board-meeting-2026-03-10]]).
- Reserves stood at about £31,000 at the end of 2025. The board committed £25,000 of them to the changing-rooms match funding on 12 May 2026, which leaves us under our own reserves policy ([[how-much-reserve-should-the-club-keep]]).
- Hardship bursary: £2,400 for 2026-27, 14 awards (£1,680) by October 2026. Junior membership is 161, down from 168.
- Direct debit collection is paused while we compare bank charges ([[is-direct-debit-worth-the-bank-charges]]).
