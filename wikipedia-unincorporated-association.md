# Unincorporated association

## Summary

English Wikipedia article (last edited 11 February 2026). It describes an unincorporated association as a group of people in common law systems who organise around a shared purpose without forming a corporation or other legal entity. Such groups arise from an agreement among members, need few formalities to set up, and are flexible, but have no legal identity separate from their members. The article distinguishes them from partnerships, whose members unite for profit.
