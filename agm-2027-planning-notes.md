# AGM 2027 Planning Notes

Early planning for the AGM on Saturday 12 June 2027, my last as chair. Draft timetable with [[joanna-pryce]], October 2026.

- **December 2026:** board decides on co-chairs or a single chair ([[should-fernhollow-have-co-chairs]]).
- **February 2027:** if co-chairs, draft the AGM resolution under clause 7.5.
- **April 2027:** call for nominations in the newsletter; role descriptions for all four officers.
- **21 May 2027:** notice and papers out (22 days ahead).
- **29 May 2027:** nominations close.
- **12 June 2027:** AGM. Elections, accounts, and a thank-you to everyone stepping down under the transitional clause.

Officers affected by the transitional clause: I (chair since 2019) and Joanna (secretary since 2020) must stand down from those offices; Joanna may stand for a different role. Femi (vice-chair since 2023) and Claire (treasurer since 2022) are within six years and may stand again. We need a new secretary as well as a new chair.

Ideas: hold it in the evening after the last training session so more parents come; a short history of the club since 1981 for the programme, which [[colin-brightwell]] might write.
