# Design And Quotes

Get a design for the new changing rooms that the club, the council and a funder would all accept, and a fixed price we could put in a grant application.

## Outcome

Pro bono design agreed and a fixed quote of £248,000 received from Hutchins & Kerr Building on 26 June 2026. Reached.

## Current State

- Completed. The design is [[changing-rooms-design-v2-2026-05]]; the quote is [[hutchins-and-kerr-changing-rooms-quote]], fixed until 31 March 2027.
- The board's sub-group chose Hutchins & Kerr on 30 June 2026 ([[changing-rooms-tender-review-2026-06-30]]).
- Open threads have moved to the parent [[new-changing-rooms]] and to [[changing-rooms-grant-application]].

## Scope and Boundaries

Covered the brief, the drawings, the asbestos survey of the old block and the quotes. Did not cover planning consent, which waits for the grant, or the build contract, which waits for the lease and grant.

## Working Context

Neil Cartwright, an architect and former club parent, offered the design free in August 2025. His first brief ([[changing-rooms-design-brief-2025]], now archived) was one large junior room, one adult room, an accessible WC and a store. It was cheaper but repeated the old problem: any overlap of adult and junior sessions still meant sharing.

In May 2026 Aoife Brennan's safeguarding review reached the changing rooms. Marek and Neil joined her first Saturday observation of the old block on 2 May, and it was obvious. Neil redrew it in May: two junior rooms that can be split by age or by girls and boys, an officials' room with its own shower, and an accessible changing room with a WC and shower. The footprint went up by about 18 square metres and the estimate by about £21,000. The board accepted the increase on 12 May 2026 when it agreed the reserves commitment. See [[changing-rooms-design-revision-2026-05-19]].

Three quotes came back in June:

- Hutchins & Kerr Building, traditional block build, £248,000 fixed to 31 March 2027.
- A second local builder, £271,500, with a provisional sum for the asbestos removal.
- A modular supplier, £229,000, but without the officials' room and with a 20-year design life, which the trust's guidance discourages.

Details in [[changing-rooms-quote-comparison]]. We looked hard at the modular option, mostly because of the £19,000 saving, and turned it down; the design life and the missing officials' room were the reasons ([[value-engineering]]).
