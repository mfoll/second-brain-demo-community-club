# Board Skills Audit Questionnaire

## Summary

Two-page form sent to the nine board members on 8 September 2025, adapted from a template the Draymoor Volunteer Centre lends to small groups.

## Key Details

- 14 skills in four groups: running the club (chairing, minutes, constitution), money (accounts, budgeting, fundraising, grants), people (coaching, safeguarding, volunteer management, communications), and premises (facilities, contracts and legal, health and safety, project management).
- Each scored 0-3, with one line for "what I would like to learn".
- A last question: "If you stepped down tomorrow, what would nobody else know how to do?" This produced the most useful answers.

## Why It Matters

Reusable for the next audit in 2027. The last question is worth asking every year, not just in an audit.
