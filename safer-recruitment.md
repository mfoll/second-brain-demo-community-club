# Safer Recruitment

Building safeguarding into how volunteers are recruited, so that checks happen before contact with juniors rather than after someone has already started helping.

## The Elements We Use

1. **A written role** saying whether the job involves juniors ([[volunteer-role-descriptions]] mark these "check needed").
2. **A conversation**, not an interview, with Nisha or a section lead about the role and why the club asks for checks.
3. **A reference** for regular roles with juniors, usually from another volunteer or employer.
4. **A background check** through the governing body route ([[wikipedia-background-check]] for the general idea).
5. **Safeguarding training** before unsupervised contact.
6. **Induction** with the codes of conduct and the reporting route ([[report-a-welfare-concern]]).
7. **A settling-in period** working alongside an experienced volunteer.

The procedure is [[onboard-a-new-volunteer]].

## What Changed in 2026

Before 2026 the steps existed but in no fixed order, and people sometimes started helping with a team while their check was "in progress". Clubfold now blocks booking a "check needed" slot until training and check show as current, which takes the awkwardness out of saying no.

## Tension With Recruitment

Every step adds friction just when we are trying to make volunteering easier. People who are not parents ask why a check is needed for a car park role (it is not; only roles with juniors need one). Explaining this plainly at the start helps ([[volunteer-centre-fair-2026-04-25]]).
