# Oakreach Track Agreement

The written arrangement between the club and Oakreach School for the track-for-coaching swap, as signed in August 2025, with the school's lettings proposal of September 2026 and the extension agreed on 6 October 2026. The signed letter and the extension email are in the shared drive under Community/Oakreach.

## Summary

A two-page letter of agreement signed by the headteacher and by me as chair on 28 August 2025, running from 1 September 2025 to the end of the 2025-26 school year and then "rolling unless either party gives a term's notice". The club gets free use of the six-lane synthetic track on Tuesday evenings; the school gets a free after-school athletics club run by club coaches on Wednesdays. No money changes hands either way.

## Key Details

What the school gives:

- Track and infield on Tuesdays 18:00 to 20:00 in term time, with floodlights from October to March and the changing rooms by the sports hall.
- Caretaker cover for the slot; the club locks the track gate and returns the key to the caretaker.
- Storage for two hurdle sets and a starting block bag in the PE store.

What the club gives:

- A Wednesday after-school athletics club, 15:30 to 17:00 in term time, for pupils of any year, led by two club coaches holding a current athletics coaching licence, safeguarding training and background checks. A school PE teacher is always on site.
- About 25 pupils a session in 2025-26 ([[oakreach-after-school-club-summary]]).
- Help on the day of the school's own sports day.

Conditions: club insurance covers our sessions; the school's covers the after-school club; either side can cancel a session with 48 hours' notice; the arrangement is reviewed each July.

The September 2026 proposal: Priya Lal, the school's business manager, proposed on 22 September 2026 that from January 2027 the club pay the standard lettings rate of £45 an hour for the Tuesday slot and that the Wednesday club continue unchanged ([[oakreach-lettings-proposal-2026-09]]). About 70 hours a year, about £3,150.

The extension: at a meeting on 6 October 2026 the headteacher agreed to honour the free arrangement until the end of the summer term in July 2027, while both sides review it ([[oakreach-lettings-meeting-2026-10-06]]). Confirmed by email the next day.

## Why It Matters

This one letter is why the athletics squad trains on a proper track. Before September 2025 they trained on grass at Millrace Lane and paid for occasional Saturday track sessions. The weakness of the 2025 letter is that it never put a value on either side, so a new business manager reading it saw only a free letting. The review in 2027 needs to fix that ([[school-partnership]], [[keep-the-oakreach-swap-in-kind]]). Claire has the £3,150 as a sensitivity in the budget, not a cost ([[club-budget-2026-27]]).

## Caveats

- "Rolling unless either party gives a term's notice" means the school could have given notice in September 2026 for January. The extension to July 2027 is by email, not a new signed letter.
- Floodlight costs are not mentioned anywhere in the letter.
