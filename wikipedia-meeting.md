# Meeting

## Summary

English Wikipedia article (last edited 28 May 2026 when rechecked). It describes a meeting as two or more people coming together to discuss one or more topics, often formally, and notes that meetings serve both for group decision-making and for sharing information.

## Why It Matters

A small thing, but useful. Before the skills audit workshop in November 2025 ([[skills-audit-workshop-2025-11-11]]) I wanted to separate the two jobs our board meetings were trying to do at once: deciding, and telling each other news. We now put section reports in writing with the papers and keep the meeting time for decisions ([[how-to-run-a-board-meeting]]). Meetings got shorter by about twenty minutes.

## Caveats

General background; nothing specific to boards or clubs.
