# Should we start walking football?

## Question

Should the club run a weekly walking football session for older adults, and if so who runs it and when?

## What we know

- Demand: 11 adults in the [[members-survey-2025]], 14 at the trial, 12 would come weekly ([[walking-football-trial-feedback-2026-05]]).
- Money: £3 to £4 a session would cover pitch hire of a mini pitch in daytime.
- Blockers: no session leader; evening slots clash with junior training from September; the 1974 changing rooms are already the tightest part of the site.

## Current Answer

A spring 2027 trial term of daytime sessions, if someone from the trial group will lead it with Donovan's support for the first month. Not yet put to the board. My view is in [[walking-football-is-worth-a-trial-not-a-section]].
