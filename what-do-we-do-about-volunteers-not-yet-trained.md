# What Do We Do About Volunteers Not Yet Trained?

## Question

Six of 35 volunteers in contact with juniors were not current on training or checks on 15 October 2026. The draft policy says they step back from junior sessions after 31 December. How do we get them there, or let them step back, without losing good people or embarrassing anyone?

## What We Know

- Three have booked the online course, two have checks in progress, one has not replied to three reminders ([[safeguarding-training-status-october-2026]]).
- At least two of the six are long-serving volunteers with heavy loads. Telling them to stop would leave gaps on Saturdays.
- Individual details stay in Clubfold. Aoife and Beatriz know who; I know the count and the reasons in general terms.

## Current Thinking

A personal phone call from [[aoife-brennan]] or [[beatriz-ferreira]] rather than another email. Offer the January in-person session and help with the online course. For the one who has not replied, a kind conversation about whether they still want the role at all. Nobody is named to the board.

## What Would Answer It

The count on 31 December 2026. If anyone is still outstanding, Aoife decides the step-back and the rota is adjusted quietly.
