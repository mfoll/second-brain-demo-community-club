# Masters Group Running Interest List

Sheila Mortimer's note of who in the masters group and wider club would help with a community run, gathered after the members' survey, 12 November 2025. Counts and roles only.

- Masters athletes willing to marshal or pace: 8 of 12.
- Adults from the survey interested in running regularly: 16 (they were not contacted individually; the survey was anonymous).
- Possible run director: nobody yet. Sheila will advise but not lead; she already runs the athletics section.
- Suggested format: an untimed Saturday 5k at 09:00 once a month, before junior football fills the site, or an annual fun run in spring.

Why it matters: the only evidence we have of volunteer capacity for the [[community-running]] idea.
