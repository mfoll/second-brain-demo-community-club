# Role-Based Recruitment Proposal January 2026

[[nisha-raghavan]]'s options paper for the February 2026 board, circulated on 30 January 2026.

## Summary

Nisha set out three options for the pledge (keep and enforce, drop everything, or drop the fee and recruit by role) and recommended the third. Her case: families respond to specific asks, the opt-out had not filled slots, and her time was better spent asking people personally than chasing sessions.

## Key Details

- Proposed 25 to 30 role descriptions, each one screen long, in Clubfold by 1 March.
- One-off slots open every home Saturday, no commitment beyond the day.
- A target of twelve new regular volunteers by December 2026.
- The £930 opt-out money to go to families who struggle with fees rather than general funds.
- Her own time estimate: four hours a week chasing pledges, which she would rather spend on personal asks.
- Cited the Draymoor Volunteer Centre checklist ([[draymoor-volunteer-centre-good-practice-checklist]]).

In her own register, the paper is crisp: numbered options, a one-line recommendation and a table of costs and risks.

## Why It Matters

The board adopted the recommendation in full ([[board-meeting-2026-02-10]]). It is the founding document of the [[volunteer-recruitment-drive-2026]].
