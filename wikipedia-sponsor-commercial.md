# Sponsor (commercial)

## Summary

English Wikipedia article (last edited 13 August 2026 when rechecked). Sponsoring means supporting an event, activity, person or organisation with money, products or services; the supporter, much like a benefactor, is the sponsor.

## Why It Matters

Hamza read it while drafting a pitch for a second kit sponsor in August 2026, after Brian Coyle's extra gift to the appeal ([[brian-coyle]]). It helped him separate sponsorship, where the sponsor expects something such as a logo on shirts ([[coyle-motors-sponsorship-agreement]]), from donations like Brian's £1,500 to the appeal, which come with no promise attached. We now record them separately ([[sponsorship-overview]]).

## Caveats

Very short lead; nothing on sponsoring junior sport, where the club's own rules on photos and suitable sponsors apply.
