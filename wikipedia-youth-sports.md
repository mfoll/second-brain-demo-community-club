# Youth sports

## Summary

A general article on youth sports: any sport where the competitors are children or adolescents, whether in school or outside it, organised or informal. The body covers participation, benefits and the reasons young people stop.

## Why It Matters

Background reading when I set up the [[youth-participation]] topic after the [[members-survey-2025]]. The section on drop-out at adolescence matched what coaches described at the under-13 and under-14 age groups, and what Leanne fears for the girls ([[how-do-we-keep-girls-playing-past-under-14]]).
