# Stewarded Parking Plan

A written plan for how cars arrive, park and leave during a large event, with named volunteer stewards carrying it out. The council made one a condition for the 2027 tournament.

## What ours contains (Sam's draft of 27 August 2026)

- Capacity: 140 cars on the field-side car park and grass overflow, marked out with cones and tape.
- Overflow: Oakreach School car park (90 spaces) if the school agrees, with a stewarded walking route.
- Stewards: 4 per shift on Saturday, 2 on Sunday, in hi-vis, with a lead steward holding the event phone.
- No parking on Millrace Lane or Weir Close verges; cones on the verges and a steward at each end from 08:30.
- Advance message to visiting teams with entry instructions, car-sharing request and the overflow map.
- A contact number for residents, staffed all day.

## Why it matters beyond the tournament

The same plan would serve any large event at Millrace Lane, including the opening of the new changing rooms and any fun run. It is now a reusable part of [[run-tournament-day]].

## Weak point

Stewarding is the least popular volunteer slot. The plan only works if those slots are filled first.
