# Sheila Mortimer

Athletics section lead and board member since 2021. A former county-level middle-distance runner who still runs with the masters group.

## Role

- Leads the athletics section: the junior squad (ages 9 to 17, about 30 athletes) and the masters group (about 12 adults).
- Holds the club's Northmere Athletics licence and coach education records ([[northmere-athletics-club-licence-2026]]).
- Board member; speaks for athletics when football business dominates, which is most months.

## How we work together

Sheila prefers a short phone call to a long email and will say plainly when athletics is being treated as an afterthought. She is usually right. I check pitch and track decisions with her before they reach the board.
