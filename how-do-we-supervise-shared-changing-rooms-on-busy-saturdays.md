# How Do We Supervise Shared Changing Rooms on Busy Saturdays?

## Question

Until the new changing rooms open (planned for the 2028-29 season, and only if the grant and lease come through), how do we keep junior and adult changing apart in one shared 1974 room on Saturdays when fixtures overlap?

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> Interim rules since 5 September 2026: staggered changing slots, no adult changes while juniors are present, two adults outside the door, no phones in the room, officials in the meeting room. They work, but they depend on fixture times and on finding two door adults every slot.

Details in [[changing-room-supervision-rules-2026]].

## What Is Still Open

- **Fixture times.** Two of the first seven Saturdays needed a slot moved because an adult kick-off changed late. Could [[martin-goss]] set changing slots when he sets fixtures, or ask the league for later adult kick-offs?
- **Door adults.** Four door slots each match Saturday, all needing current training. That is a lot of asks on top of everything else.
- **Winter.** Asking the adult men to wait outside in the rain will wear thin by January.
- **If the grant fails.** If the Lanternfield bid rolls to the next round over the lease ([[pitch-lease-extension]]), these "interim" rules may last until 2029 or beyond. Is a temporary changing cabin worth costing?

## What Would Answer It

A review after half term with Aoife, Martin and [[marek-zielinski]], and the council cabinet decision on the lease on 19 November.
