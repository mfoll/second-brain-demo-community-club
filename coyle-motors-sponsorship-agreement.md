# Coyle Motors Sponsorship Agreement

## Summary

Three-season kit sponsorship between the club and Coyle Motors, signed 20 August 2024 by [[brian-coyle]] and me. Runs from the 2024-25 season to the end of 2026-27.

## Key Details

- £1,500 a year, paid each September, towards junior team kit.
- Coyle Motors logo on the front of junior shirts and on the club website.
- One team photo a year for the garage window, using only players whose parents have given photo consent under the club's guidance ([[photo-and-social-media-guidance]]).
- No exclusivity beyond motor trade sponsors.
- Either side may end it with notice before 1 July for the following season.

## Why It Matters

Our only regular sponsor. It ends after 2026-27, so renewal is on the money list ([[manage-club-money]]). Brian's separate £1,500 gift to the appeal in 2026 is outside this agreement ([[sponsorship-overview]]).
