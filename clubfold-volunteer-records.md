# Clubfold Volunteer Records

Pointer to the individual volunteer records in Clubfold: role, safeguarding training date, background-check status and references. These records are personal data and are never copied into this vault.

## Summary

Each volunteer's Clubfold profile holds their roles, the date of their last safeguarding training, background-check status and expiry, and a note that references were received. Clubfold uses these to block booking of "check needed" slots and to send 60-day expiry reminders.

## Why It Matters

The aggregate counts in [[safeguarding-training-status-october-2026]] come from here. It is the governed home for the facts that the policy requires the club to hold ([[fernhollow-safeguarding-policy]]).

## Access

- Full access: welfare officer ([[aoife-brennan]]) and deputy ([[beatriz-ferreira]]).
- Status only (current or not, no detail): volunteer coordinator and chair.
- Nobody else.

## Caveats

Concern records are not here. They are held only by the welfare officer, outside Clubfold.
