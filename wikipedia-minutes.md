# Minutes

## Summary

English Wikipedia article (last edited 17 July 2026 when rechecked). Minutes are the written record of a meeting or hearing, usually listing who attended, what was considered and what was decided. The article notes that "minutes" here means notes, not units of time.

## Why It Matters

[[joanna-pryce]] and I looked at it two days after the March 2026 fee vote, when Ravinder's request to minute his dissent made us think about what our minutes are for ([[board-meeting-2026-03-10]]). It confirmed what we already practised: the minutes record decisions and the reasons, not a transcript. We agreed to keep dissent in the member's own agreed words and to keep confidential items to a single line ([[minuted-dissent]], [[how-to-run-a-board-meeting]]).

## Caveats

A general description; it does not tell a club how detailed its minutes should be.
