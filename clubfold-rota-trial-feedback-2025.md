# Clubfold Rota Trial Feedback 2025

Feedback gathered from coaches, team managers and parents of the under-9s and under-10s after five Saturdays on the Clubfold rota pilot (May and June 2025). Collected by [[nisha-raghavan]] through a short online form and a chat with [[tom-ashworth]]'s team.

## Key Details

- 34 responses: 6 coaches and managers, 28 parents.
- 26 said the rota was easier than the group chat; 5 the same; 3 harder.
- Most-liked: seeing two weeks ahead (19 mentions), booking in one tap (11).
- Complaints: too many reminder emails (9), grandparents and some parents without smartphones left out (4), confusion about swaps (4).
- Coaches wanted to see who was on for their own game without opening the app; answered by the printed sheet.

## What We Did

Cut reminders to one at 48 hours; kept a printed sheet on the pavilion door; wrote a one-paragraph how-to on swaps. All went into [[clubfold-rota-setup-notes]].
