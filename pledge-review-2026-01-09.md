# Pledge Review 9 January 2026

[[nisha-raghavan]] and I went through the Clubfold figures for the [[family-volunteer-pledge]] at my kitchen table on Friday 9 January, ahead of the board on 13 January.

## What the Figures Showed

From [[pledge-review-data-january-2026]]:

- 44% of junior families had done at least one volunteer session since September. Only 18% had done two or more.
- 31 families had paid the £30 opt-out: £930 in total.
- Two families had left the club and named the pledge as part of the reason.
- The rota fill rate had not improved: 81% two weeks out in autumn 2025, the same as the year before the pledge.
- Nisha was spending about four hours a week chasing sessions.

## What We Made of It

The pledge was costing goodwill and Nisha's time and had not filled more slots. Several opt-out families had stopped the informal help they used to give, as if the £30 had settled the account ([[motivation-crowding-out]]). Nisha already had the outline of role-based recruitment and agreed to write it up as options for February ([[role-based-recruitment-proposal-2026-01]]).
