# Tom Ashworth

Under-9s coach since 2024, when his son's team needed one. Holds the Northmere FA introductory coaching qualification.

## Role

Runs Saturday training and festivals for the under-9s (about 14 players) on mini pitch 2. Also the person other young-age coaches ring when they are stuck for a session plan.

## How we work together

Tom is enthusiastic and reliable but stretched: he works shifts and sometimes swaps Saturdays with his assistant at a day's notice. He uses the Clubfold rota properly, which helps.

## Current threads

- A strong voice at the 2026 coaches' launch meeting for keeping the youngest age groups in festival format ([[should-under-7s-play-festivals-not-fixtures]], [[season-launch-meeting-2026-08-18]]).
- Interested in moving on to the next coaching qualification in 2027 if the club helps with the fee.
