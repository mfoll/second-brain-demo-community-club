# Oakreach After-School Club Summary

Kwabena Osei's end-of-year summary of the Wednesday after-school athletics club at Oakreach School, 2025-26, sent 20 July 2026. Counts only; the register itself belongs to the school.

- 33 sessions run in term time; 2 cancelled (exam hall use, snow).
- Average attendance 25 pupils, range 18 to 31. About 40% girls.
- Coaching time given: 33 sessions × 1.5 hours × 2 coaches = 99 coach hours, plus about 15 hours of preparation.
- 4 pupils joined the club's junior athletics squad during the year.
- The school entered six pupils in the borough schools' athletics meeting in June, all from the club.

Why it matters: our side of the track swap in hours. At the school's proposed lettings rate of £45 an hour, the Tuesday track use we receive (about 70 hours a year) would cost about £3,150. The 99 coach hours are what we give back ([[how-many-coaching-hours-can-we-give-oakreach]]).
