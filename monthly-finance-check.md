# Monthly Finance Check

What the treasurer does in the first week of each month, as [[claire-whitlow]] does it. Written for her successor ([[treasurer-succession-notes]]).

## Steps

1. Download the bank statement and the Clubfold payouts report for the month.
2. Match every Clubfold payout to the bank. Investigate any difference over £5.
3. Enter all spending in the cash book with its budget line ([[club-budget-2026-27]]). Check every payment over £500 had two signatories.
4. Record tea hut and event cash from the signed count sheets; chase any missing sheet within a week ([[independent-examiner-report-2025]]).
5. Check failed card payments flagged by Clubfold; ask the membership secretary to contact those families, gently.
6. Keep match-funding money in its own column; never spend it on running costs ([[match-funding]]).
7. Write a half-page note for the board: income and spending against budget, cash at bank, reserves position, bursary total from Ravinder (counts only), anything odd.

## Outputs

The board finance note, sent with the papers a week before the meeting. The cash book updated on the shared drive.

## Checks

Once a quarter, the chair looks at the bank statement alongside the cash book. Takes ten minutes; worth it.
