# Club Communications Plan

Sorting out how the club talks to its members, led by Hamza Qureshi for the board. Started in February 2026 after the survey told us parents could not find anything and volunteers were answering the same questions in a dozen chats.

## Outcome

A monthly newsletter, one club channel per audience and a named volunteer for each, in place by the end of 2026.

## Current State

- Monthly newsletter: seven issues since April 2026, sent through Clubfold to all members. Average open rate 58% ([[club-newsletter-archive]]).
- Channels: one broadcast channel per team for match-day information (13 of 13 set up), one parents' notices channel from Clubfold, and the club's public social media run by [[linh-pham]].
- Named volunteers: team managers own the team channels; Linh owns social media; Hamza edits the newsletter. Still missing: an editor who is not Hamza.
- The old club-wide group chat (230 members) was closed to new posts on 1 June 2026.
- The plan follows the [[photo-and-social-media-guidance]] for anything showing juniors.

## Scope and Boundaries

Routine club communications. Not here: press relations about the changing rooms (Marek and I handle those with [[carol-dunphy]]), safeguarding communications (Aoife), or anything that copies member contact details out of Clubfold.

## Working Context

Hamza's audit in February counted 23 active group chats run by different volunteers, three club email addresses and a social media page nobody had posted on since 2024 ([[communications-audit-2026-02]]). 71% of survey respondents wanted one place to find information ([[members-survey-2025]]).

We chose not to build a new website this year. The newsletter and the team broadcast channels cover most needs, and a website needs someone to keep it current. The superseded plan for a club social media page as the main channel is kept in [[club-social-media-page-plan-2025]].
