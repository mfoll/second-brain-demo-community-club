# Graham Sutcliffe

Estates and leases officer at Draymoor Borough Council. Handles the Millrace Lane lease.

## Role in our work

- Negotiated the heads of terms for the 30-year lease with us between November 2025 and July 2026 ([[lease-meeting-2025-11-12]], [[millrace-lane-lease]]).
- Wrote the officer report to cabinet recommending approval, and told me on the morning of 17 September that it would be deferred ([[council-cabinet-deferral-2026-09-17]]).
- Is the officer who would sign off any letter to the Lanternfield Trust about the council's intentions.

## How we work together

Graham is precise and cautious and does not like being chased by councillors, so I go through him first and Rhys second. He gave ground on drainage when we showed him Gareth's photos and Marek's costing; he did not give ground on the club taking full repair of any building on the site. He prefers a short email with the question in the first line. He has been straight with us about the [[community-asset-review]]: he does not think it will change our terms, but he cannot promise it.
