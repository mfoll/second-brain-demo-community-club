# Term limit

## Summary

English Wikipedia article (last edited 6 October 2026 when rechecked). A term limit is a rule restricting how many terms a person may serve in an elected office. The article explains that limits are used to check concentration of power, and that they can cap terms over a lifetime or only consecutive terms.

## Why It Matters

The constitution working group read it in February 2025 while deciding our own limit. The distinction between a lifetime cap and a consecutive cap settled one argument: we chose consecutive years, so an experienced officer can come back after a year out ([[officer-term-limits]], [[fernhollow-constitution-2025]]). The point about concentration of power is a grand way of saying what I felt after five years as chair: the club leaned on me too much.

## Caveats

Almost entirely about national politics. The reasons translate loosely to a club board, and I have kept the comparison light.
