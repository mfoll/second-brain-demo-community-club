# Heads of terms

English Wikipedia article, last edited 27 July 2026. Licence CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Summary

A one-line lead: heads of terms, also called heads of agreement or a letter of intent, are a non-binding document setting out the main issues in a tentative sale, partnership or other agreement.

## Why It Matters

Graham Sutcliffe used the phrase in every email from the spring of 2026 and I wanted to be sure what we were agreeing to. The word that mattered was "non-binding": agreeing heads of terms with officers in July 2026 did not give us a lease, which the cabinet deferral later proved ([[council-cabinet-deferral-2026-09-17]]). My own notes on how we used them are in [[heads-of-terms]].

## Caveats

General definition; our lease heads of terms were checked by the club's solicitor.
