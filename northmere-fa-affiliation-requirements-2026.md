# Northmere FA Affiliation Requirements 2026-27

## Summary

The Northmere Football Association's annual affiliation checklist for clubs with junior teams, received in May 2026 via [[vikram-joshi]], the club development officer.

## Key Details

- Affiliation due by 31 July 2026; late affiliation carries a fine.
- Required: a constitution adopted at a general meeting, a named welfare officer with current training, a safeguarding policy reviewed within three years, an equality policy, public liability insurance, and named officers' contact details.
- New for 2026-27: evidence that every team has at least one coach with a current entry-level coaching qualification.

## Why It Matters

The 2025 constitution met the governance items without change. The safeguarding policy review date (2022) is why the policy review had to finish within the year ([[safeguarding-policy-review]]). Affiliation was submitted on 21 July 2026.
