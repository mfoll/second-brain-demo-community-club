# Can Parents Photograph Their Own Children at Matches?

## Question

Under the new photo guidance, can parents take photos of their own child at matches, and what about other children in the background?

## Current Answer

Yes, parents may photograph and film their own child at matches and athletics events. We ask them not to post images where other children can be identified unless those families agree, and never in or near changing rooms or toilets. This is a request backed by the parents' code of conduct ([[codes-of-conduct-2026]]), not something we can enforce on a public touchline.

Adopted with the guidance on 14 July 2026 ([[photo-guidance-adoption-2026-07-14]]).

## Why We Landed Here

Banning parents' photos was raised and rejected: unenforceable on open playing fields, resented, and it would push photos into hiding rather than into good habits. [[aoife-brennan]]'s view was that families whose child must not be photographed, for whatever reason, need the team manager to know quietly, which the consent field in Clubfold now handles ([[photo-consent-form-2026]]).

## Caveats

Tournaments with visiting teams are harder. For the 2027 tournament we will add the request to the visiting-team pack.
