# Look After The Ground

Everything to do with Millrace Lane playing fields: the two full-size grass pitches, the three mini pitches, the 1974 pavilion, the new changing rooms we are trying to build, and the lease that lets us be there at all.

## Scope

- The pitches (marking, mowing, drainage, end-of-season renovation) and the pavilion (roof, kitchen, meeting room, the prefabricated changing block, keys and lock-up).
- The new changing rooms: design, quotes, the Lanternfield Trust grant, the match funding appeal and, later, the build.
- The lease with Draymoor Borough Council and the pitch hire terms that come with it.
- Not here: pitch allocation between teams on a Saturday (that is [[run-the-season]]), who supervises the changing rooms (that is [[keep-children-safe]] and [[changing-room-supervision-rules]]), and the reserves decision itself (that is [[manage-club-money]]).

## Current State

- The new changing rooms are the biggest thing the club has attempted since it moved to Millrace Lane. The grant went in on 30 September 2026 ([[changing-rooms-grant-submission-2026-09-30]]) and the decision is due in February 2027.
- The weak point is the lease. Cabinet deferred the 30-year lease on 17 September ([[council-cabinet-deferral-2026-09-17]]); the next chance is 19 November and the trust wants evidence by 15 December.
- Match funding is £63,000: £25,000 reserves, £15,000 Community Chest, £23,000 appeal. The appeal stood at £14,600 on 20 October ([[match-funding-appeal]]).
- The 2025 roof patch is holding. Marek expects to look at it again in 2029 or 2030.
- Pitch 2 still drains badly at the bottom end after heavy rain; Gareth has it on his list.
