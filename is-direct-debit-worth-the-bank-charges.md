# Is Direct Debit Worth the Bank Charges?

Should the club restart monthly direct debit for fees, given the bank's new community account charges?

## What We Know

- Bank charges from April 2026: about £250 to £300 a year for the club ([[bank-community-account-charges-2026]]).
- Clubfold card instalments cost about £5 per child per season in ten instalments, with no monthly charge ([[clubfold-payment-settings]]).
- The trial had six failures in one month, chased by hand.

## Status

Deferred until [[claire-whitlow]] has compared other community accounts, due before registrations open in June 2027. Also depends on whether the club incorporates and changes bank anyway ([[club-incorporation-review]]).
