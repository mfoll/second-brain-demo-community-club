# Volunteer Hours Estimate 2025

An estimate of volunteer hours for September to December 2025, made by [[nisha-raghavan]] from Clubfold rota bookings plus the fixed roles that are not on the rota (coaching, board, welfare). Prepared for my December 2025 note to the board on volunteer load.

## Key Details

- About 2,300 volunteer hours over the four months, or roughly 130 hours a week in season.
- Seven people did 53% of those hours.
- The heaviest single load was about 12 hours a week.
- Coaching accounts for about half of all hours; match-day roles about a quarter.
- Rota-only volunteers (no fixed role) averaged under two hours a month.

## Why It Matters

The "seven people, half the hours" figure is the number I quote whenever [[volunteer-burnout]] comes up, and it is why the recruitment drive aims at regular roles rather than more one-off helpers alone.

## Caveats

Fixed-role hours are self-estimated and probably understated. Informal help is not counted.
