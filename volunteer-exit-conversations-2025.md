# Volunteer Exit Conversations 2025

My anonymised summary of short conversations with six volunteers who stopped during or at the end of the 2024-25 season. Written in October 2025; no names, and nothing that would identify anyone.

## Key Details

| Reason given | Count |
|---|---|
| Own child moved up, left or stopped playing | 3 |
| Too much, or "it kept growing" | 1 |
| Work or family changes | 1 |
| A specific disagreement about team selection | 1 |

What came up across several conversations:

- Nobody had told them what the job was when they started, so nobody could tell when they had done enough.
- Four of six said they would have stayed in a smaller role if one had been offered.
- Two said they had not felt missed when they stopped. That one stung.

## Why It Matters

This fed the thinking behind [[volunteer-burnout]] and later [[role-based-volunteering]]. The "smaller role" point is why one-off slots exist.

## Caveats

Six conversations, all with people willing to talk to the chair. Those who left unhappy may not have been.
