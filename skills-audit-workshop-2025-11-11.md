# Skills Audit Workshop 11 November 2025

A 45-minute session after the November board, Tuesday 11 November 2025. All nine board members.

## What we did

We put the self-scores from the [[board-skills-audit-questionnaire]] on a sheet of flip chart paper and looked for the gaps, not the stars. Then each of us named one thing we would be willing to learn.

## What came out

- **Treasurer succession:** only [[claire-whitlow]] scored above 1 on accounts and budgeting. Biggest single risk.
- **Communications:** nobody owned it. [[hamza-qureshi]] offered to lead.
- **Facilities project management:** only [[marek-zielinski]], at the moment we are about to build.
- **Strong:** coaching, welfare, knowing the families, local contacts.
- Femi said he would learn the council and trust side; Ravinder offered to shadow some of Claire's month-end work, though he said accounts were not his thing.

Written up in [[board-skills-matrix-2025]].
