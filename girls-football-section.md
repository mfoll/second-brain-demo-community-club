# Girls Football Section

Building a girls' football section that lasts, started by Leanne Fairbairn in June 2025 after years of girls drifting away from mixed teams at about age ten.

## Outcome

A sustainable girls' section with at least two teams and two female coaches. As of October 2026: 31 girls, two teams (girls' under-11s and girls' under-14s) and two female coaches. The outcome is met on paper; it is not yet sustainable because it depends on Leanne.

## Current State

- 31 girls registered in October 2026: 17 in the girls' under-11s and 14 in the girls' under-14s ([[girls-section-growth-log]]).
- Coaches: [[leanne-fairbairn]] (girls' under-11s, Northmere FA qualified) and [[ellie-tranter]] (assistant with the girls' under-14s, course booked). The under-14s head coach is a parent volunteer who has agreed to one season only.
- The girls' under-14s played their first league match on 13 September 2026 ([[girls-under-14s-first-league-match-2026-09-13]]).
- 19 families in the [[members-survey-2025]] said a daughter would play if there were a girls' team; 9 of those girls have since joined.
- Northmere FA visited on 14 May 2026 and offered coach education places for women ([[northmere-fa-club-visit-2026-05-14]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

Girls' teams, their coaches, recruitment and retention. Girls who play in mixed teams stay with those teams; nobody is moved without their family asking. No names of players. The adult women's team is a separate team but a natural next step for the oldest girls.

## Working Context

The 2025 taster day brought 23 girls; 14 signed up for the first season. Leanne's view, which I share, is that girls stay when they have a friend on the team and a coach who is there every week ([[girls-section-needs-its-own-coaches]]). The weak point is coaches: borrowing a dad for a season fills a gap but does not build anything.

The girls' under-11s train Thursday 17:30 on mini pitch 3, which has no floodlight after October. From November they move to the Oakreach track infield, subject to the school agreement.
