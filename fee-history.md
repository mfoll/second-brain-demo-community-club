# Fee History

Annual membership fees since 2017, from old minutes and Claire's budgets. Useful when someone says "fees always go up".

| Season | Juniors | Adults | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2017-18 | £100 | £150 | |
| 2019-20 | £115 | £165 | Pitch hire rise |
| 2020-21 | £115 | £165 | Partial refunds for the lost spring |
| 2023-24 | £140 | £190 | First rise in four years; Clubfold not yet in place |
| 2025-26 | £140 | £190 | Plus £30 pledge opt-out for families who did not volunteer ([[family-volunteer-pledge]]) |
| 2026-27 | £165 | £210 | Board vote 6-1 ([[board-meeting-2026-03-10]]); bursary introduced ([[hardship-bursary-fund]]) |

Over nine seasons junior fees rose 65%, roughly in line with pitch hire. A family with two juniors in 2025-26 who did not volunteer paid £310; in 2026-27 they pay £330.

Siblings: no discount so far. Ravinder has suggested one; it would cost about £1,400 a year at 10% for second children.
