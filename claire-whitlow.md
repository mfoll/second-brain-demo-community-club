# Claire Whitlow

Treasurer since the 2022 AGM. Accountant by profession, parent of two former juniors, and the reason our money is in good order.

## Role

- Keeps the books, prepares the season budget and the calendar-year accounts ([[club-budget-2026-27]], [[annual-accounts-2025]]).
- Runs the [[monthly-finance-check]] and second signatory on bursary decisions with [[ravinder-sahota]].
- Wrote the Draymoor Community Chest bid that won £15,000 of [[match-funding]] in August 2026, despite scoring herself 1 out of 3 on fundraising in the [[board-skills-audit]].

## How we work together

Claire works in spreadsheets and expects questions to be specific. She sends the budget with a one-page summary for those of us who will not read the tabs. She is careful not to let her professional standards make the job look impossible to a successor, and she asks the board to keep processes simple for that reason.
