# Open And Close The Pavilion

How to open and lock up the pavilion on a match day or training evening. For any keyholder. Printed copy on the inside of the kitchen door.

## Before you start

- You need a pavilion key and the alarm code from the keyholder list (Marek holds the list; codes are never written in the vault or in group chats).
- Know where the first aid kit (kitchen, top shelf), the defibrillator (outside wall by the car park) and the fire extinguishers are.

## Opening

1. Unlock the main door, turn off the alarm within 30 seconds.
2. Lights on in the corridor and kitchen. Check the meeting room ceiling for drips after heavy rain.
3. Unlock the changing block and check it is clean and the changing block fire exit is clear of bags.
4. Put the changing room rota sign on the door. On Saturdays, follow the slot times in [[changing-room-supervision-rules]].
5. Unlock the container for goals and nets.
6. Tea hut volunteers: water heater on, fridge checked.

## Closing

1. Walk every room. Nobody left in the changing block; showers off; windows shut.
2. Kitchen: heater off, fridge closed, bins out to the car park bins.
3. Container locked. Mower store locked.
4. Test the smoke alarm button on the first Saturday of the month and sign the logbook.
5. Lights off, alarm set, main door locked. Check the gate.

## If something is wrong

Leaks, damage, a broken lock or anything unsafe: follow [[report-a-ground-or-pavilion-fault]]. Anything to do with a child's welfare goes to the welfare officer, never into a fault report.
