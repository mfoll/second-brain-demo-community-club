# Conflict of interest

## Summary

English Wikipedia article (last edited 4 October 2026 when rechecked). A conflict of interest arises when a person or organisation has several interests, financial or otherwise, and serving one could mean working against another. Typically it concerns a personal interest that might affect a duty to decide in someone else's best interest.

## Why It Matters

[[joanna-pryce]] used it in July 2025 to write the short guidance that goes with the [[conflict-of-interest-register]], which clause 9 of the [[fernhollow-constitution-2025]] now requires. The phrase about a duty owed to others is the bit board members find helpful: on the board we decide for the club, not for our own child's team. It matters most now that the club is choosing a contractor and has sponsors with links to the building trade ([[sponsorship-overview]]).

## Caveats

General definition; it does not tell a club when a declared interest means leaving the room. Our rule is in the constitution.
