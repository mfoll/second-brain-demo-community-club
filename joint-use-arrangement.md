# Joint Use Arrangement

An agreement for two organisations to share a facility, usually a school and a community group, with each side's use, costs and responsibilities written down.

## The version we have

Ours with Oakreach School is a swap in kind: track time for coaching time, no money either way ([[oakreach-track-agreement]]). It works while the people who agreed it are in post and remember why.

## What a good one sets out

- The value each side gives and receives, in hours and pounds, even if no money changes hands.
- Who pays for lights, cleaning, caretaking and wear.
- Insurance, safeguarding and vetting for each activity.
- How long it runs, how it is reviewed and how either side ends it.

## Lesson

Ours left out the first two points, which is why a new business manager could read it as a free letting ([[oakreach-lettings-proposal-2026-09]]). The 2027 review should put both sides' value on paper ([[keep-the-oakreach-swap-in-kind]]).
