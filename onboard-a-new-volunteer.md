# Onboard a New Volunteer

The steps [[nisha-raghavan]] and I follow when someone takes on a regular role. One-off slot volunteers only need steps 1 and 6.

## Inputs

- The role description ([[volunteer-role-descriptions]]), which says whether a check is needed.
- The volunteer's Clubfold account.

## Steps

1. **Welcome message** within two days of their first interest, from Nisha, naming the role and a person to meet.
2. **Short conversation** (15 minutes, at the ground or by video call): the role, the time it takes, what happens if they cannot make a week, and why we ask for checks for roles with juniors ([[safer-recruitment]]).
3. **For roles with juniors:** start the background check and book safeguarding training. Clubfold blocks "check needed" slots until both are current. Ask for one reference.
4. **Induction** (about 30 minutes): codes of conduct ([[codes-of-conduct-2026]]), the welfare concern route ([[report-a-welfare-concern]]), the photo rules, the first-aid kits and the pavilion.
5. **Buddy** for the first three sessions: an experienced volunteer in the same role.
6. **Thank-you message** after the first session, from Nisha.
7. **Six-week check-in:** still enjoying it? Is the time what we said? Added to [[recruitment-drive-progress]] as a new regular.

## Guardrails

- No unsupervised contact with juniors before step 3 is complete, however urgent the gap.
- Background-check results and references stay in Clubfold ([[clubfold-volunteer-records]]), never in email or this vault.

## Verification

The volunteer appears in Clubfold with the role, training and check status set, and in the headcount at the next update ([[volunteer-headcount-october-2026]]).
