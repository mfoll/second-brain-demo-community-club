# Council Contacts Map

Who at Draymoor Borough Council deals with what, for whoever takes over from me. Contact details are in the club's shared contacts list, not here.

| Need | Who | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Pitch hire, allocations, event permission | [[anita-venkatesh]], sport and leisure | Email; replies within a week |
| Community Chest and small grants | Anita first, then the grants team | She will read a draft |
| Lease, boundaries, anything the council owns | [[graham-sutcliffe]], estates | Short emails, question in the first line |
| Cabinet decisions, political support | [[rhys-penhallow]], ward councillor | Keep informed early; never ask him to lobby officers |
| Parking complaints, residents | Anita, with the Residents' Association | See [[residents-association-meeting-2026-07-22]] |
| Planning | Planning duty officer (Neil deals with them) | [[do-we-need-planning-consent-for-the-changing-rooms]] |
| Bins, fly-tipping on the lane | Council contact centre | Report online with a photo |

## Rules of thumb

- Officers first, councillor second ([[council-officers-carry-the-relationship]]).
- Anything over 10 years or a large sum goes to cabinet: allow three months and one deferral.
- Copy Joanna on anything formal so the secretary's file is complete.
