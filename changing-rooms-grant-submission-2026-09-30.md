# Changing Rooms Grant Submitted, 30 September 2026

The club submitted its application to the Lanternfield Trust Community Facilities Fund on Wednesday 30 September 2026, the closing day of round 2, asking for £185,000 towards new changing rooms costing £248,000.

## At a Glance

> [!success]
> Submitted at 16:40 through the trust's portal, with all ten attachments. Acknowledged by the trust on 2 October. Decision expected in February 2027.

## What went in

- Ask: £185,000, 74.6% of £248,000.
- [[match-funding]] of £63,000: £25,000 club reserves (board, 12 May 2026), £15,000 Draymoor Community Chest (awarded 20 August 2026), £23,000 members' and community appeal (pledged; £12,750 banked at submission).
- Project: two junior changing rooms, an officials' room with its own shower, an accessible changing room with WC and shower, a store, and removal of the 1974 prefabricated block including its asbestos cement roof. Fixed price from Hutchins & Kerr Building ([[hutchins-and-kerr-changing-rooms-quote]]); pro bono design by Neil Cartwright ([[changing-rooms-design-v2-2026-05]]).
- Need and demand: 161 juniors and 59 adults sharing one block with two showers; 31 girls in the girls' section; adult and junior sessions overlapping on Saturday mornings; the [[members-survey-2025]].
- Safeguarding case: written by Aoife Brennan. It explains that the current block is a single shared space, describes the interim [[changing-room-supervision-rules]] in force since 5 September 2026 (staggered changing, no adult changing while juniors are present, two adults outside the door, no phones in the room), and says plainly that these depend on two volunteers being free every Saturday. Separate junior rooms and an officials' room remove the overlap by design. It refers to the policy review ([[safeguarding-policy-review]]) and not to any individual case.
- Access: no step-free changing at the ground at all at present.
- Tenure: the honest position. Current lease to 31 March 2031; heads of terms for 30 years agreed with officers; cabinet deferred on 17 September to 19 November ([[council-cabinet-deferral-2026-09-17]]).
- Attachments: drawings, quote and quote comparison, budget, constitution ([[fernhollow-constitution-2025]]), safeguarding policy summary, accounts for 2025, the heads of terms, letters of support ([[letters-of-support-changing-rooms]]), the maintenance plan outline.

## Who did what

Joanna and I wrote the narrative over three weekends. Claire did the budget and match funding statement. Marek did the deliverability section and the procurement record. Aoife wrote the safeguarding section. Femi read the whole thing as a fresh pair of eyes on the Sunday before.

## Lessons

- The maintenance plan section was the weakest; we had to describe it as an outline. See [[who-maintains-the-new-changing-rooms]].
- The portal times out after 60 minutes without saving. We lost the needs section once and rewrote it from Joanna's draft.
- Submitting with the tenure gap was a judgment call, recorded in [[changing-rooms-grant-application]].
