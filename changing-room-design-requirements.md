# Changing Room Design Requirements

The safeguarding brief that [[aoife-brennan]] and I sent to [[marek-zielinski]] and [[neil-cartwright]] on 20 May 2026, after the Saturday observations ([[changing-room-observation-2026-05-16]]). It asked the new building to make safe supervision the default rather than something volunteers have to arrange around the walls.

## Requirements

1. **At least two separate junior changing rooms**, so two junior teams, or a junior team and an adult team, never share a room.
2. **A separate officials' room** with its own shower and toilet, so referees never change with players.
3. **An accessible changing room** usable by any player, official or coach who needs privacy.
4. **Shower areas with individual cubicles**, not open showers.
5. **A corridor or lobby outside each room** wide enough for two adults to stand without blocking the door, and with sight of the doors from the corridor.
6. **No windows into changing areas at head height**; obscured glass where windows are needed.
7. **Toilets that do not open directly into changing rooms.**
8. **No CCTV inside changing rooms or toilets**; corridor coverage only, if any.
9. **A lockable store** for first-aid and welfare kit near the changing area.

## What Happened Next

Neil revised the design in late May to include two junior rooms, an officials' room and an accessible room ([[changing-rooms-design-and-quotes]]). Items 4 to 9 were all accepted. The grant application cites the safeguarding case ([[changing-rooms-grant-submission-2026-09-30]]).

The building itself belongs to [[new-changing-rooms]]; this note only records the brief.
