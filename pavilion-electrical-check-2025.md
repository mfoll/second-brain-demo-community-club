# Electrical Check After the Roof Leak, March 2025

A short report from the electrician who checked the meeting room and kitchen circuits after the February storm. Cost £240.

## Summary

- Water had reached a ceiling light fitting in the meeting room; the fitting was replaced.
- Insulation resistance tests on the lighting and socket circuits passed.
- No damage found in the consumer unit.
- Recommendation: keep the meeting room lights off until the roof is repaired if any further water comes in.

## Why It Matters

Meant we could keep using the kitchen and the meeting room safely while the roof was sorted ([[pavilion-roof-repair]]).
