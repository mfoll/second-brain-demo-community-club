# Beatriz Ferreira

Deputy welfare officer since September 2025, and a parent of two players in the girls' section.

## Role

- Second point of contact on the confidential reporting route ([[report-a-welfare-concern]]) when [[aoife-brennan]] is away or is herself the subject of a concern.

## How We Work Together

Beatriz is a pharmacist, calm and exact. She joined the May 2026 changing-room observations and wrote the clearest of the three logs. She does not want a board seat. Aoife hopes she will take over as welfare officer in 2028; Beatriz says maybe.

Beatriz started as a parent helper with the girls' under-11s when [[leanne-fairbairn]] set up the section in 2025, and offered to help with welfare after a safeguarding talk at the AGM. Aoife asked her to be deputy that September, which closed a gap the 2022 policy had left open for years.
