# Board Skills Audit

A plain look at what the nine of us on the board can and cannot do, before the changing-rooms project and my own departure made the gaps expensive.

## Outcome

A skills matrix for the nine board members, showing gaps in treasurer succession, communications and facilities project management. Done.

## Current State

- Completed on 27 November 2025. The matrix is in [[board-skills-matrix-2025]].
- Three gaps: nobody but [[claire-whitlow]] can produce accounts or a budget; nobody owned communications in a planned way; [[marek-zielinski]] is the only person who has managed a building project.
- Follow-up: [[hamza-qureshi]] took on communications and the [[club-communications-plan]]; the treasurer gap is still open ([[who-will-take-over-as-treasurer]]).

## Working Context

Each board member filled in a short questionnaire in September 2025 ([[board-skills-audit-questionnaire]]), scoring themselves 0-3 on 14 skills. We went through it at a workshop after the November board ([[skills-audit-workshop-2025-11-11]]). People scored themselves modestly; Claire scored herself 1 on fundraising and then wrote the Community Chest bid that won £15,000.

What we learned: our strengths are coaching, welfare and knowing the families. Our risk is concentration. Two people hold most of the specialist knowledge, and Claire has said she wants to hand over within two years.
