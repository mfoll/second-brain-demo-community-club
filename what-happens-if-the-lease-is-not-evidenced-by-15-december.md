# What happens if the lease is not evidenced by 15 December?

## Question

If cabinet defers the Millrace Lane lease again on 19 November, or approves it too late for us to send evidence to the Lanternfield Trust by 15 December 2026, what happens to the changing rooms, and what should the club do?

## Why It Matters

This is the single thing that could sink the grant this winter. Everything else (design, quote, match funding) is in place or on track.

## What we know

- The trust will not offer a grant without 25 years' tenure. It will accept a cabinet resolution ([[lanternfield-tenure-deadline-email-2026-10-09]]).
- If evidence is late, the application rolls to round 1 of 2027 without resubmission; decisions in July 2027.
- The Hutchins & Kerr price is fixed only to 31 March 2027 ([[hutchins-and-kerr-changing-rooms-quote]]). A July decision would mean indexation, perhaps 3 to 5%, so £7,000 to £12,000 more.
- The build is planned for the 2028 close season, opening for 2028-29 ([[changing-rooms-timeline]]). A July 2027 decision leaves planning, the contract and the asbestos licence notice to be squeezed into autumn 2027, with no slack left before May 2028.
- The Community Chest money must be used by 31 December 2028 ([[community-chest-award-2026-08-20]]).

## Current Answer

Provisional: we would let it roll rather than withdraw. Before 1 December I will ask Esther whether a letter from the council's head of estates confirming the recommendation would hold our place. Marek will ask Dale Hutchins to extend the fixed price to 30 June 2027. The board discusses on 8 December.

## What would close it

Cabinet approval on 19 November and the resolution sent to the trust that week.
