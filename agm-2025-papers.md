# AGM 2025 Papers

## Summary

The pack sent to members on 23 May 2025 for the AGM on 14 June: notice and agenda, the proposed constitution with a one-page summary of changes, the 2024 accounts, the chair's report, section reports and nomination forms.

## Key Details

- The summary of changes was the most read page: six bullets, with the six-year limit first.
- Chair's report themes: the pavilion roof, the move to the Clubfold rota, plans for a girls' section, and early thinking about the changing rooms.
- Nominations: four officers and five board places; all filled without a contested vote.

## Why It Matters

A model for future AGM packs. The one-page summary of a long document worked better than anything else we sent.
