# Wednesday 30 September 2026

## The grant went in

We [[changing-rooms-grant-submission-2026-09-30|submitted]] the Lanternfield application at twenty to five on closing day: £185,000 towards £248,000, with all ten attachments. The [[match-funding|match funding]] went in as reserves, the Community Chest award and the appeal, with the appeal's banked figure stated as it stood.

## The tenure gap

We wrote the lease position as it is: heads of terms agreed, cabinet deferred to 19 November. It was a judgment call and it is recorded in the [[changing-rooms-grant-application|project note]] with the reasons.

## The safeguarding case

Aoife's section was the strongest part. It describes the shared 1974 block and the interim [[changing-room-supervision-rules|supervision rules]], says plainly that they depend on two volunteers being free every Saturday, and refers to the policy review and never to any case.

## Lessons for next time

The portal times out after an hour without saving; we lost the needs section once and rebuilt it from Joanna's draft. The maintenance plan was weak and went in as an outline ([[who-maintains-the-new-changing-rooms|open question]]). Joanna and I wrote over three weekends, and Femi's fresh read on Sunday caught more than I expected.
