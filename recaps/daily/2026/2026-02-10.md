# Tuesday 10 February 2026

## Dropping the opt-out, keeping the ask

The [[board-meeting-2026-02-10|February board]] partly reversed the decision of [[pledge-vote-2025-07-15|15 July 2025]]. The £30 opt-out fee is scrapped from tonight, but the three-sessions pledge stays on the membership form as a voluntary ask. The [[family-volunteer-pledge|pledge project]] is now closed and superseded.

## Why

Nisha's January figures ([[pledge-review-2026-01-09|pledge review]]) were the evidence: fewer than half of families had done a session, 31 had paid to opt out and two families had left over it. Donovan argued for enforcing the rule until March; nobody joined him once we talked about how many more families might go. Going back to the group chat was rejected too, because the rota gaps are still there. The vote was unanimous in the end.

## What replaces it

Nisha's role-based recruitment: role descriptions and one-off slots in Clubfold, and asking people for something specific. That is now the [[volunteer-recruitment-drive-2026|recruitment drive]], aiming for twelve new regulars by the end of the year.

## The money already collected

Ravinder's point carried: the £930 came mostly from families working weekends, so it goes to a ring-fenced fees fund rather than general funds. No refunds; I owe every junior family a letter by the 16th explaining where it went. My own thoughts on what went wrong are in [[pledge-lessons-learned]].
