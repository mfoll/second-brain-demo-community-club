# Tuesday 6 October 2026

## Oakreach and the track

Sheila and I [[oakreach-lettings-meeting-2026-10-06|met the head]], Priya Lal and Luke Garvey about the proposed lettings fee for the Tuesday track slot. Priya's reasons are real: lettings income is down and the floodlights and caretaker cost money. I set out what the club gives back through the Wednesday after-school club ([[oakreach-after-school-club-summary|the hours]]), and Sheila explained that the squad cannot winter on grass.

## The outcome

The head will honour the free arrangement until the end of the summer term in July 2027 while both sides review it. She wants a written value on both sides to show her governors, which is fair and overdue: the 2025 letter never put a number on anything ([[oakreach-track-agreement|the agreement]]).
