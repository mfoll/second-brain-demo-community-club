# Tuesday 10 March 2026

## The fee vote

The [[board-meeting-2026-03-10|March board]] set 2026-27 fees at £165 for juniors and £210 for adults, by six votes to one. Claire's case was the 12% rise in pitch hire and rebuilding reserves after the match-funding commitment ([[club-budget-2026-27|budget]]). That closes the [[membership-fee-review-2026|fee review]].

## Ravinder's dissent

Ravinder voted against and asked for his dissent to be minuted, which it was in words he agreed ([[minuted-dissent|on minuted dissent]]). His case was the 23 families who asked for payment plans last season, and a sliding scale instead of a flat rise. Claire's answer was practical: we cannot check household income fairly or privately, and Clubfold cannot do bands before September. He accepted the timing, not the principle, and wants it back after one season.

## The compromise

Agreed in principle without a vote: a confidential hardship bursary, starting from the £930 of opt-out money moved in February and topped up from the budget. Ravinder offered to run it. Aoife asked that coaches never know which children are helped, and everyone agreed. Criteria to be settled with Claire and Ravinder within a week; that became the [[hardship-bursary-fund|bursary fund]].
