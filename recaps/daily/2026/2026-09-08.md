Femi chaired the [[board-meeting-2026-09-08|September board]] for the first time, with me beside him, a week after he began shadowing me. He kept to time. I spoke twice.

## Co-chairs

No vote: both models to be written up ([[should-fernhollow-have-co-chairs|open question]]).

## Who carries the risk

We opened the [[club-incorporation-review|incorporation review]]; my reading so far is in [[personal-liability-in-an-unincorporated-club]].
