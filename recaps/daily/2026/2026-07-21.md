# Tuesday 21 July 2026

## Tournament debrief

Sam ran the [[summer-tournament-2026-debrief|debrief]] in the pavilion. Fifty-two teams, a wet Sunday that closed pitch 2 in the afternoon, no serious injuries, and £8,900 net. As the board agreed in May, £5,000 goes to the [[match-funding-appeal|changing-rooms appeal]] once Claire closes the accounts this week.

## The parking complaint

The residents' association complaint to the council was fair, and we said so. Two volunteers at the gate was not a plan. Sam will draft a [[stewarded-parking-plan|stewarded parking plan]] by the end of August, and I will write to the council and Imran this week.

## Other lessons

A wet-weather rule agreed in advance, so nobody decides in the rain who plays. The unpopular car-park slots were the ones left empty, so next year they get filled first, with a barbecue voucher. Fifty-two teams is as many as Millrace Lane can take. Linh had no photo consent problems under the new guidance.

## Raised, not settled

Sam asked whether 2027 should be one day. We did not discuss it tonight ([[should-the-2027-tournament-be-one-day|open question]]).
