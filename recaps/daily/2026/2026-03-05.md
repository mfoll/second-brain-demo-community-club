# Thursday 5 March 2026

## Call with the Lanternfield Trust

Marek and I had forty minutes on video with Esther Lowell ([[lanternfield-pre-application-call-2026-03-05|call notes]]). The useful surprises: the 25 years' tenure is never waived and heads of terms alone will not count, only a cabinet resolution; safeguarding and access have to be the reason for the building, not a paragraph at the end; and they want demand tested, not asserted.

## What we decided

We aim for round 2, closing 30 September, which means the lease has to move faster than it has been. I will ask Aoife whether her policy review can look at the changing rooms early. The Community Chest application for part of the [[match-funding|match funding]] should start now rather than after the summer.

## Open

Whether the council can get a 30-year lease to cabinet before September. Everything else in the [[changing-rooms-grant-application|grant plan]] depends on it.
