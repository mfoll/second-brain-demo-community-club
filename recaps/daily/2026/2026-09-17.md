# Thursday 17 September 2026

## Cabinet defers the lease

Graham rang at quarter past nine to warn me, so it was no shock by the evening. [[council-cabinet-deferral-2026-09-17|Cabinet deferred]] our 30-year lease to 19 November, with three other community leases, until the council's [[community-asset-review|community asset review]] reports. The officer report still recommended approval; Graham's view is that this is about policy, not about us.

## In the gallery

Marek and I watched. Rhys spoke for the club, set out the grant deadline and got a date, though "subject to the review's interim findings". We could not speak without notice.

## What we talked about afterwards

Whether to submit the grant on 30 September anyway or wait for the spring round. Marek's worry is the fixed quote; mine is losing six months and the momentum. We leaned towards submitting with the gap stated plainly, to settle with Joanna and Claire over the weekend. I will tell Esther Lowell first thing tomorrow rather than let her read it somewhere.

## Open

What we do if November slips too. That is the risk to the whole [[changing-rooms-grant-application|grant]] now.
