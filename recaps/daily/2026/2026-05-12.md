# Tuesday 12 May 2026

## Committing the reserves

The [[board-meeting-2026-05-12|May board]] committed £25,000 of reserves to the changing-rooms [[match-funding|match funding]], payable only when the building contract is signed. Sheila abstained and asked that athletics, with its hurdles and storage, not be forgotten while the building takes everything. That is fair and minuted.

## What it costs us

Claire was clear that reserves drop to about £6,000, well under the three months' running costs in our own [[club-reserves-policy|reserves policy]]. We accepted that for a building meant to last fifty years and asked her for a three-season plan to rebuild them ([[how-much-reserve-should-the-club-keep|open question]]).

## Changing rooms and safeguarding

Aoife reported that the first two Saturday observations had already shown adults and juniors in the shared room together. The board agreed the extra cost of separate junior rooms and an officials' room the same night. Neil is redrawing, and the board accepted the higher estimate without much argument.

## Also

The appeal target is £23,000, to launch at the AGM. Five bursaries awarded so far.
