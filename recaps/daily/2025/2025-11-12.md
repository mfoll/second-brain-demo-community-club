# Wednesday 12 November 2025

## First lease meeting with the council

Marek and I saw Graham Sutcliffe at the council offices to ask for a lease long enough for a capital grant ([[lease-meeting-2025-11-12|meeting notes]]). We asked for at least 30 years so a funder's 25 would survive a slow grant, and for the pitches to come into the lease rather than being hired season by season.

## What we learned

Length is not the problem: anything over 10 years goes to cabinet, which takes a report and about three months. Repairs are the problem. The council expects full repair of buildings and pitches, drainage included, and Marek is wary of pitch 2. Graham also asked about the club's legal status, which I had not thought about; an unincorporated club signs through holding trustees.
