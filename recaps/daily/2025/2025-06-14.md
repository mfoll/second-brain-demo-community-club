# Saturday 14 June 2025

## The new constitution

The [[agm-2025-06-14|AGM]] adopted the [[fernhollow-constitution-2025|2025 constitution]] comfortably, which closes the [[constitution-review-2025|constitution review]] after six months. The few members against it objected to the six-year officer limit; Joanna minuted their reasons, and I think they deserved to be heard rather than argued down.

## Officers and my own date

All four officers were re-elected under the transitional clause, so the current team runs to the 2027 AGM. Two parents asked when I would stand down and I said 2027 out loud for the first time. It felt better said than not, and it is really where the [[chair-succession-plan|succession plan]] began, even though nothing was written down until January.

## What members actually asked about

More questions about the roof and a possible changing-rooms bid than about the constitution. Worth remembering when I plan next year's chair's report.
