# Tuesday 15 July 2025

## The family volunteer pledge

The board, meeting a week late because of the end-of-season presentations, made the [[family-volunteer-pledge|family volunteer pledge]] compulsory from September: three sessions per junior family per season, or a £30 opt-out on the fee. The [[pledge-vote-2025-07-15|vote]] went 7-2, on the paper Donovan and I wrote ([[pledge-board-paper-2025-07|board paper]]).

## The two objections

Ravinder worried about families who work weekend shifts. Sheila argued that athletics parents already time-keep and should not pay for missing football slots, and we exempted athletics-only families in response. Both points were fair and both were treated as details at the time. A review is booked for January.
