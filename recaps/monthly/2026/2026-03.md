# March 2026

## Fees and the bursary

The month's main decision was the [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-03-10|fee vote of 10 March]]: £165 for juniors and £210 for adults, carried six to one with Ravinder's dissent minuted. The compromise followed within the week, when Claire, Ravinder and I agreed the criteria and the [[hardship-bursary-fund|hardship bursary]] opened on 16 March with £2,400, including February's £930. Ravinder still wants a sliding scale; that argument has a date in January 2027 ([[should-fees-use-a-sliding-scale|open question]]).

## The grant takes shape

The [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-03-05|call with the Lanternfield Trust]] turned a vague bid into a plan for round 2, closing 30 September. The two things that changed our thinking were that tenure is never waived and that safeguarding has to be the reason for the building. Both pointed at the lease and at Aoife, and the [[changing-rooms-grant-application|grant project]] now leads with them.

## Volunteers after the pledge

Nisha's first [[volunteer-taster-morning-2026-03-07|taster morning]] on 7 March was the first test of role-based recruitment, a month after the opt-out was scrapped ([[volunteer-recruitment-drive-2026|recruitment drive]]).

## Tournament

Sam's planning meeting on 24 March fixed the shape of the July tournament: a cap of 52 teams, a £60 entry fee and entries open from 1 April ([[tournament-planning-meeting-2026-03-24|planning notes]]). Parking was raised and left at two volunteers on the gate unless entries reached the cap.

## Open at month end

- Whether the council can get the lease to cabinet before September.
- Whether the fee rise will cost us families. We will not know until registrations close.
