# September 2026

## Handing over, slowly

Femi started [[femi-shadowing-starts-2026-09-01|shadowing me]] on 1 September and chaired his first board a week later. At that [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-09-08|board]] Hamza proposed co-chairs, Femi argued for a single chair, and the board asked for both models in writing. Marek's question about personal liability opened the [[club-incorporation-review|incorporation review]]. The [[chair-succession-plan|succession plan]] now has two live questions instead of one.

## The lease and the grant

The month turned on two days. On 17 September [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-09-17|cabinet deferred the lease]] to 19 November pending its community asset review. On 30 September we [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-09-30|submitted the grant]] anyway, with the tenure gap stated honestly. Everything on the [[new-changing-rooms|changing rooms]] now waits on November.

## Safeguarding in practice

The interim [[changing-room-supervision-rules|changing-room rules]] came into force on 5 September and held up through the first month of Saturdays. The [[safeguarding-workshop-2026-09-19|workshop on 19 September]] brought training to 29 of 35 volunteers. Aoife circulated the second draft of the revised policy on 28 September.

## The season starts

161 juniors registered, seven fewer than last season. The girls' under-14s played their [[girls-under-14s-first-league-match-2026-09-13|first league match]] on 13 September.

## Oakreach

Priya Lal's email of 22 September proposing a £45 an hour lettings fee for the Tuesday track slot arrived late in the month ([[oakreach-lettings-proposal-2026-09|the proposal]]). Meeting booked for 6 October.
