# Tournament Risk Assessment 2026

Sam Okonjo's risk assessment for the 2026 summer tournament, reviewed by Hazel and Aoife Brennan on 20 June 2026.

- Hazards listed: goalposts (all checked and weighted), heat, lightning, slips on wet grass, barbecue burns, lost children, vehicles in the car park, crowding at the tea hut.
- Controls included: a named first aid point, a lost-child point at the pavilion door with two background-checked volunteers, a lightning rule (play stops at the first thunder and resumes 30 minutes after the last), and cones separating cars from pedestrians at the gate.
- Gaps found afterwards: no wet-weather plan for closing pitches, no control for parking beyond the gate, and no plan for sheltering 400 people from rain.

Why it matters: the gaps are now actions in the debrief and the [[run-tournament-day]] procedure.
