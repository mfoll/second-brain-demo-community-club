# Walking Football Trial Session 2026-05-20

One-off walking football trial, Wednesday 20 May 2026, 18:30 to 19:30 on mini pitch 1. Run by Donovan Clarke with Hazel; free.

- 14 adults came, aged roughly 50 to 75. Nine were parents or grandparents of juniors, three were former club players, two came from the residents' association newsletter.
- Format: a warm-up, a short rules talk (no running, no contact, ball below head height), then two small-sided games of 15 minutes.
- One player turned an ankle stepping off the kerb in the car park, not on the pitch. Noted for any future risk assessment.
- 12 of the 14 said they would come weekly ([[walking-football-trial-feedback-2026-05]]).

No decision followed. Donovan cannot run it weekly, and Wednesday evening clashes with junior training from September. See [[should-we-start-walking-football]].
