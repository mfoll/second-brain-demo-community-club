# Name-a-Peg Scheme

Helen Marsh's one-page flyer and terms for the name-a-peg scheme, launched on 20 June 2026.

## Summary

- £25 buys a small engraved plate above a coat peg in one of the new junior changing rooms.
- Plates can carry a family name, a team and season ("Under-12s 2026-27"), or "In memory of". No juniors' full names; first names and team only if a family wants a child named.
- 120 pegs available across the two junior rooms; 86 sold by 20 October 2026.
- Money is refunded if the building does not go ahead.

## Why It Matters

Small money, but it gives people something to point at in the new building and it has brought in grandparents and former players who would not give to a general appeal. Helen manages the list; Claire banks it into the appeal account ([[match-funding-appeal-tracker]]).
