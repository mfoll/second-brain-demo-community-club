# Sliding scale fees

## Summary

English Wikipedia article (last edited 31 July 2026 when rechecked). Sliding scale fees are prices that vary with a customer's ability to pay, so that people with lower incomes, or less to spare after essential costs, pay less. The article treats them as a form of price discrimination, or differential pricing.

## Why It Matters

[[ravinder-sahota]] sent the link round with his proposal in February 2026 ([[ravinder-sliding-scale-proposal]]). It helped the board talk about the idea with a common vocabulary. Claire picked up on ability to pay as measured by money to spare rather than income, which is exactly what a volunteer club cannot assess, and was one reason the board chose a bursary instead ([[board-meeting-2026-03-10]], [[sliding-scale-fees]]).

## Caveats

Describes the general idea; it does not show how well such schemes work for sports clubs, which is what we need to know in January 2027.
