# Summer Tournament 2026

The club's two-day junior football tournament at Millrace Lane on Saturday 4 and Sunday 5 July 2026, run by Sam Okonjo. Our biggest fundraiser and the one weekend a year most families see the whole club at once.

## Outcome

A safe, well-run tournament for 52 teams from under-7 to under-14, raising at least £8,000 net, with £5,000 of it going to the [[match-funding-appeal]] for the [[new-changing-rooms]]. Reached: net £8,900.

## Current State

- Completed. Final accounts closed on 24 July 2026: income £10,310, costs £1,410, net £8,900 ([[summer-tournament-2026-budget]]).
- £5,000 transferred to the appeal on 24 July; £3,900 to general funds.
- 52 teams entered (44 in 2025), 21 of them from outside Draymoor. Saturday was under-7 to under-10 festivals, Sunday under-11 to under-14 competition.
- Sunday rain from about 11:00 closed pitch 2 after 14:00; the under-13 and under-14 finals were played as 8-minute halves on pitch 1.
- The residents' association complained to the council about verge parking on Saturday. The council's reply makes a stewarded parking plan a condition for 2027 ([[council-event-conditions-letter-2026-08]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

The 2026 event only: entries, schedule, volunteers, catering, first aid, money and the debrief. The 2027 tournament is not yet a Project; its open questions sit in [[should-the-2027-tournament-be-one-day]]. Photos of juniors followed the [[photo-and-social-media-guidance]] adopted later that month in draft form.

## Working Context

Sam ran the entries and schedule from January, with [[helen-marsh]] on the tea hut and barbecue and [[martin-goss]] on referees. We needed 64 volunteer slots across the weekend and filled 58 through the Clubfold rota ([[tournament-volunteer-plan-2026]]). The parking problem was predictable: in 2025 we had 44 teams and cars were already on the verges of Weir Close. We had no steward plan beyond two volunteers in hi-vis at the gate.

The money matters more this year than usual because the tournament is the biggest single event in the appeal. The £5,000 was agreed by the board in May, before we knew the weather.
