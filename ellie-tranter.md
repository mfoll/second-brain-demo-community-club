# Ellie Tranter

Volunteer assistant coach, 19, a former junior member who played here from under-8s to under-16s. Studies at university and coaches in her holidays and on weekends when she is home.

## Role

- Assistant coach with the girls' under-11s alongside [[leanne-fairbairn]].
- Helps at the summer tournament and holiday sessions.
- Holds current safeguarding training and a background check since June 2025.

## How We Work Together

Ellie is the first of our former juniors to come back as an adult volunteer, and she prompted a gap in the old policy: it had nothing to say about young adults coaching children only a few years younger, some of whom they knew as teammates' siblings. The draft policy now covers this in plain terms ([[safeguarding-policy-draft-2026-09]]): same codes of conduct as any adult, no private messaging with juniors, team group chats only through the parent-managed channel. Ellie helped write that section and said she found it useful rather than awkward.

During term she is away, so she books one-off slots rather than a weekly role.

## Current Threads

- Coaching on home weekends through the autumn.
- Possibly taking her first coaching badge in the summer of 2027.

## Background With the Club

Ellie played in mixed teams until she was 16, at a time when the club had no girls' section and she was often the only girl in her age group. That experience is part of why she cares about the girls' section now. She did her first coaching sessions with [[leanne-fairbairn]] in summer 2025 after her check and training came through.
