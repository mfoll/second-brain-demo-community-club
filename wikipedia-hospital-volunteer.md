# Hospital volunteer

## Summary

The English Wikipedia article on hospital volunteers: people who work unpaid in health care settings, usually under the direct supervision of nurses. Last edited 10 August 2026 at the time of the record.

## Why It Matters

Found next to the volunteer management article when Nisha and I were reading in January 2026. It reminded me how ward volunteers were handled when I was nursing: a defined task, a named nurse to report to, an induction, and nobody let loose with patients until checks were done. That is close to what we now do with [[role-based-volunteering]] and [[safer-recruitment]], and it helped me explain to the board why it is not bureaucracy for its own sake.

## Caveats

About hospitals, not clubs. The parallel is mine.
