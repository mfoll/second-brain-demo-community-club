# Photo Guidance Adoption 14 July 2026

The July board, Tuesday 14 July 2026, adopted the club's photo and social media guidance ([[photo-and-social-media-guidance-2026]]) as the first finished part of the safeguarding review.

## The Discussion

[[aoife-brennan]] presented the guidance with [[linh-pham]]. The main debate was whether to stop posting photos of juniors at all. Aoife said she had been tempted, as it would be simplest. Linh and [[hamza-qureshi]] argued that match photos are how families, sponsors such as Coyle Motors and the trust see that the club is alive, and that consent plus clear rules gives parents real choice. [[sheila-mortimer]] asked that athletics be covered too, since parents film races; the guidance already did.

## Decisions

- Guidance adopted unanimously, in force from 2026-27 registration.
- Photo consent added as a required yes/no field in Clubfold registration ([[photo-consent-form-2026]]).
- No names with any photo of a junior, ever.
- Linh to brief team managers before the first fixture.

## Afterwards

The consent field went live the next day, which closed the [[photo-and-social-media-guidance]] project.
