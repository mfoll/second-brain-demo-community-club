# Parasports

## Summary

Explains that the term parasports covers sports officially recognised and regulated by the international Paralympic governing body, both those on the Paralympic programme and other recognised federations.

## Why It Matters

I looked it up after a parent asked whether we had anything for a disabled child. We do not have a disability football session, and this article is about elite governance, so it helped mostly by showing how far it is from our level. Kept under [[inclusion-and-access]] as a reminder that the gap is real; the [[new-changing-rooms]] accessible room is the first practical step.

## Caveats

Not about grassroots provision at all. Anything we do would start with the regional football association's advice, not this. Article last edited 2026-10-18 when checked; licence CC BY-SA 4.0.
