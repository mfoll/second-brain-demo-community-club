# Who maintains the new changing rooms?

## Question

Once the new building exists, who cleans it, checks it, repairs it and pays for that, every week for 25 years?

## Why It Matters

The trust asks for a maintenance plan and our application only gave an outline ([[lanternfield-application-form-2026]]). Under the new lease the club carries full repair of the building ([[repairing-obligations]]). Today the pavilion is looked after by Marek, Gareth and goodwill, and Marek has said he will not be the long-term caretaker.

## What we have

- Running costs estimated at £2,100 a year and a £1,000 a year sinking fund from 2028.
- An outline plan: weekly clean by a paid cleaner (about £30 a week), monthly checks by a small facilities team, annual inspection ([[new-building-maintenance-plan-outline]]).

## Status

Deferred until the grant and lease are decided; there is no point recruiting a facilities team for a building that may not happen. Revisit at any trust site visit and in spring 2027. Nisha has offered to write a role description for a facilities volunteer ([[volunteer-role-descriptions]]).
