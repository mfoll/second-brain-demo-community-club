# Walking Football Trial Feedback 2026-05

Paper feedback forms from the walking football trial on 20 May 2026, typed up by Hazel on 27 May. 14 forms, anonymous.

- Would come weekly: 12 yes, 2 maybe.
- Would pay: 10 said £3 to £4 a session was fine; 2 said free or nothing.
- Preferred time: weekday daytime 6, weekday evening 7, weekend 1.
- Best thing: "being back on a pitch" (several), meeting people, no pressure.
- Worries: knees, the car park kerb, playing next to junior training.
- Three offered to help run it; none wanted to lead.

What I take from it: demand is there and daytime might suit half the group, which would avoid the evening clash with junior training. Leadership is the gap.
