# Fernhollow Safeguarding Policy

The club's safeguarding policy: the version adopted in 2022 and still in force, and the 2026 revision going to the board on 17 November 2026. This note summarises structure and changes only. It is a pointer to the governed copy, not the policy itself, and it holds no cases.

## Summary

The 2022 policy (version 3, adopted at the board in March 2022) follows the [[northmere-fa-safeguarding-template]] closely. It names a welfare officer, sets out a reporting route, includes codes of conduct and requires safeguarding training and background checks for adults working with juniors. It was sound when written but had fallen behind how the club works: no deputy welfare officer, one paragraph on photos, no refresher period for training, and an assumption of separate changing for adults and juniors.

The 2026 revision (draft version 2, 28 September 2026, [[safeguarding-policy-draft-2026-09]]) keeps the same skeleton and closes the eleven gaps in [[safeguarding-review-gap-list]].

## Structure of the Policy

1. Statement of commitment and scope: all members, volunteers, visitors and contractors on club activities, including away fixtures and athletics at Oakreach School.
2. Roles: the board holds overall responsibility; the welfare officer ([[aoife-brennan]]) and, from the 2026 draft, a named deputy ([[beatriz-ferreira]]); coaches and team managers; parents.
3. Reporting route: any concern goes to the welfare officer or deputy the same day, in person or by phone, never by group chat. If a child is in immediate danger, the emergency services first. The welfare officer decides on referral to Northmere FA, Northmere Athletics or statutory services. The process is set out for volunteers in [[report-a-welfare-concern]].
4. Safer recruitment: role descriptions, references for roles with juniors, background checks and training before unsupervised contact ([[safer-recruitment]]).
5. Codes of conduct for coaches, volunteers, parents and players ([[codes-of-conduct-2026]]).
6. Supervision: ratios, the [[two-adult-rule]], transport, away trips, changing rooms.
7. Photos, video and online ([[photo-and-social-media-guidance-2026]]).
8. Training and review.

## What Changed in the 2026 Draft

- A named deputy welfare officer, and cover arrangements when both are away.
- Training refreshed every three years, with a 60-day expiry reminder and stepping back from junior sessions if lapsed ([[how-often-should-volunteers-refresh-safeguarding-training]]).
- A full photo and social media section replacing one paragraph.
- Changing rooms: the interim supervision rules for the shared 1974 rooms as an appendix ([[changing-room-supervision-rules-2026]]).
- Young adult volunteers aged 18-21, including former juniors, under the same codes as any adult, with no private messaging with juniors.
- Under-18 helpers never count towards supervision ratios.
- Records: individual training and check records live only in Clubfold ([[clubfold-volunteer-records]]); concern records live only with the welfare officer.

## Why It Matters

This is the document every other safeguarding note hangs from. The revision is the main output of the [[safeguarding-policy-review]], and its changing-room section is why the [[new-changing-rooms]] design includes separate junior rooms.
