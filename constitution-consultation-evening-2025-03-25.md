# Constitution Consultation Evening 25 March 2025

Members' evening on the draft constitution, Tuesday 25 March 2025, 19:00, pavilion. 38 members came, more than I expected.

## What we heard

- Strong support for a limit on officer terms. Most people wanted six years; a few said three. Two long-serving volunteers worried it would push out people who still wanted to help; we explained the limit applies to officers only, and only consecutively.
- Questions about why the welfare officer should not vote. [[aoife-brennan]] explained it herself: the welfare officer must be able to report without being part of a vote on the same issue.
- Several parents asked how juniors would have a say. That led to the youth forum clause instead of a junior board seat.
- One member asked about electronic voting at general meetings. Left out; the AGM stays in person.

Written responses afterwards are summarised in [[constitution-review-consultation-2025]]. Ran by [[joanna-pryce]] and me, with tea from [[helen-marsh]].
