# Facility For Coaching Swap

An arrangement where the club pays for use of someone else's facility in coaching time instead of money. Ours: free use of the Oakreach School track on Tuesday evenings in exchange for club coaches running the school's Wednesday after-school athletics club.

## Why it works

Both sides get something they cannot easily buy. The school gets qualified coaches; the club gets a synthetic track it could never afford to build. It also builds goodwill and brings pupils into the club.

## Why it is fragile

- It depends on a few people: on our side Kwabena Osei, on theirs Luke Garvey.
- Nobody writes down what the hours are worth until someone asks. When the school's business manager proposed a £45 per hour lettings fee in September 2026, we had to work it out: about £3,150 a year of track time against about 99 coach hours ([[oakreach-after-school-club-summary]]).
- A change of staff on either side can end it.

The lesson: put the exchange in writing with both values in it, which the renewed [[oakreach-track-agreement]] now does.
