# Event management

## Summary

Describes event management as applying project management to creating and running events of any size, from festivals to ceremonies, including the concept, the audience and the technical coordination.

## Why It Matters

Sam read it after the [[summer-tournament-2026-debrief]] while drafting the [[stewarded-parking-plan]]. The useful idea for us was treating the tournament as a project with a plan for each part, including arrival and parking, instead of a club day that grows each year.

## Caveats
