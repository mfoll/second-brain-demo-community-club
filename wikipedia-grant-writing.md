# Grant writing

English Wikipedia article, last edited 23 October 2026. Licence CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Summary

The article treats grant writing as the work of completing an application for a financial grant from a government, company, foundation or trust. It names the skill involved "grantsmanship".

## Why It Matters

Joanna and I had never written a capital grant bid. I read this the week we started the Lanternfield application in March 2026, mainly to check we were not missing a standard section. It pushed us to treat the application as one argument, need first and money last, rather than a set of answers to separate boxes ([[lanternfield-application-form-2026]]). Joanna found the idea of grantsmanship as a skill reassuring: something you can learn, not something you are born with.

## Caveats

Generic guidance. The trust's own guidance and Esther Lowell's advice outrank it ([[lanternfield-pre-application-call-2026-03-05]]).
