# Safeguarding Review Gap List

[[aoife-brennan]]'s list of gaps between the 2022 policy and how the club runs in 2026, produced on 14 May 2026 for the [[safeguarding-policy-review]]. Summarised here with where each gap was closed. Policy and process only.

| # | Gap | Closed by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Photos and social media covered in one paragraph | [[photo-and-social-media-guidance]] |
| 2 | No refresher period for training | Three-year rule ([[how-often-should-volunteers-refresh-safeguarding-training]]) |
| 3 | Training and check expiry not tracked | Clubfold reminders ([[safeguarding-training-tracker]]) |
| 4 | Policy assumes separate adult and junior changing | Interim rules ([[changing-room-supervision-rules]]) and new building brief |
| 5 | No named deputy welfare officer | [[beatriz-ferreira]] named in the draft |
| 6 | Nothing on volunteers aged 18-21 who were recently juniors | Young adult volunteer section in the draft |
| 7 | Under-18 helpers' status unclear | [[should-under-18-helpers-count-in-supervision-ratios]] |
| 8 | Online contact: no rule on private messages | Codes of conduct ([[codes-of-conduct-2026]]) |
| 9 | Athletics away meets and the Oakreach School track not mentioned | Scope extended; checking with [[orla-keane]] |
| 10 | Volunteers could start before checks cleared | Clubfold booking block ([[safer-recruitment]]) |
| 11 | No date for reviewing the policy | Annual light review each November |

## Aoife's Note on Priority

Gaps 4, 5 and 10 were the ones she would not wait for the full policy to fix. The deputy was named in September 2025 already; the booking block went live in March 2026; the changing rooms took longest because they needed observation first.
