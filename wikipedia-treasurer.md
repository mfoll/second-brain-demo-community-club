# Treasurer

## Summary

English Wikipedia article (last edited 27 May 2026 when rechecked). Its lead defines a treasurer simply as the person responsible for the financial operations of a government, business or other organisation.

## Why It Matters

I went looking for a neutral description of the role when [[claire-whitlow]] and I first talked about her handover in April 2026 ([[treasurer-succession-notes]]). The one-line definition is the point: responsible for the finances, which is not the same as doing every entry personally. That supports Claire's idea of splitting bookkeeping from the treasurer's oversight so the role is less daunting for a non-accountant ([[who-will-take-over-as-treasurer]]).

## Caveats

Mostly about government treasurers. Nothing on volunteer roles.
