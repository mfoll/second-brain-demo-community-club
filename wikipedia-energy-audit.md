# Energy audit

## Summary

Defines an energy audit: a survey and analysis of how energy flows through a building, as the first step to cutting energy use and cost without losing what the building does.

## Why It Matters

I read it to decide what our February walk-round should cover. We did a very light version: bills first ([[pavilion-energy-bills-2025]]), then a room-by-room look at lights, heating and hot water ([[pavilion-energy-audit-visit-2026-02-26]]). The article made me ask for the bills before the visit, which was the useful part.

## Caveats

Describes formal audits by professionals. Ours was two volunteers with a notebook, and should be called a walk-round, not an audit. Article last edited 2026-09-29 when checked; licence CC BY-SA 4.0.
