# Safeguarding Policy Draft September 2026

Version 2 of the revised safeguarding policy, circulated by [[aoife-brennan]] to the board on 28 September 2026. Comments close on 3 November; adoption is due on 17 November 2026.

## Summary

Fourteen pages plus three appendices (codes of conduct, photo guidance, changing-room rules). Keeps the structure of the 2022 policy ([[fernhollow-safeguarding-policy]]) and closes the eleven gaps in [[safeguarding-review-gap-list]].

## Key Details

- Named deputy welfare officer ([[beatriz-ferreira]]) and cover when both are away.
- Three-year training refresh; step back from junior sessions after lapse.
- Young adult volunteers (18-21): same codes as all adults; no private messaging with juniors; team communication only through parent-managed channels. [[ellie-tranter]] helped draft this section.
- Under-18 helpers never count in ratios.
- Athletics away meets and the school track covered.
- Annual light-touch review each November.

Aoife writes in short numbered clauses with plain words; she cut the 2022 policy's legal-sounding phrases wherever Rebecca Thorne said they were not needed.

## Why It Matters

The main output of the [[safeguarding-policy-review]]. Once adopted it replaces the 2022 policy and this draft becomes history.

## Caveats

Draft only. Not for circulation to parents before adoption.
