# Pavilion Fire Risk Assessment 2025

Marek's annual fire risk assessment of the pavilion and changing block, reviewed by the board on 9 September 2025.

## Summary

- Escape routes: two exits from the meeting room, one from the changing block; the changing block exit was partly blocked by kit bags in summer and is now marked.
- Kitchen: deep fat fryer removed in 2024; fire blanket and CO2 extinguisher in place.
- Alarms: battery smoke alarms tested monthly by whoever does the Saturday lock-up; logbook on the kitchen wall.
- Electrics: periodic inspection due 2028.
- Actions: new exit sign for the changing block, extinguisher service in October, keep the changing block exit clear.

## Why It Matters

Required by our insurer and by the lease. The lock-up checks in [[open-and-close-the-pavilion]] come from this assessment. The new building will need its own assessment before it opens.
