# Walking Football Is Worth A Trial, Not A Section

## Stance

Walking football deserves a proper trial term in 2027, but not a new section with its own lead on the board, at least not yet.

## Why I Think This

The trial showed demand and real enjoyment ([[walking-football-trial-feedback-2026-05]]). It also showed nobody wants to lead it yet, and a new section on the board means another person to find in the year I am trying to hand over the chair. A term with a clear end date lets us find out if a leader emerges.

## What Would Change My Mind

Someone from the trial group offering to run it weekly. Then I would back a section straight away.

Low confidence: I have played walking football once, and badly.
