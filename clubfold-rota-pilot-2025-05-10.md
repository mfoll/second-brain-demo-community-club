# Clubfold Rota Pilot 10 May 2025

First Saturday on the Clubfold rota, with the under-9s and under-10s only.

## What Happened

- 16 slots published two weeks ahead: tea hut, pitch setup, two linespeople and a first-aider for each home game.
- 14 filled by the Wednesday before; the two gaps were filled by [[tom-ashworth]]'s parents' group on the Friday.
- Three people turned up without having booked, out of habit; we let them help and showed them the app.
- The 48-hour and 24-hour reminder emails both went out, and two parents complained about getting four emails each for two slots.

## What We Changed

- Reminders cut to one, at 48 hours.
- A printed copy of the week's rota on the pavilion door for anyone without the app.
- Kept the pilot running to the end of the season (four more Saturdays) before extending it.

Coaches' feedback is collected in [[clubfold-rota-trial-feedback-2025]]. Part of the [[volunteer-rota-overhaul]].
