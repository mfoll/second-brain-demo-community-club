# Meeting with Oakreach School on the Track Lettings Fee, 6 October 2026

Tuesday 6 October 2026, 16:00, the head's office at Oakreach School. Adaeze Okafor, Priya Lal and Luke Garvey for the school; Sheila Mortimer and me for the club.

## Discussion

- Priya explained the proposal ([[oakreach-lettings-proposal-2026-09]]): the school's lettings income is down, floodlights and caretaker on a Tuesday cost real money, and the club's slot is the only free letting in the diary.
- I set out what the club gives: 99 coach hours a year through the Wednesday club, about 25 pupils a session, four pupils since joining the club squad, and vetted, trained coaches the school does not have to vet itself ([[oakreach-after-school-club-summary]]).
- Sheila said that the squad could not move to grass for the winter without losing athletes.
- Luke spoke up for the after-school club.

## Decision

The head agreed to honour the free arrangement until the end of the summer term in July 2027, while both sides review it. She asked for the review to put a value on both sides in writing so she can explain it to governors. Confirmed by email on 7 October.

## Follow-up

- Club to send a short paper on the value of the swap by the end of January 2027 ([[school-partnership]]).
