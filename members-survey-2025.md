# Members Survey 2025

Results of the autumn 2025 members' survey: 142 responses from about 210 member households, collected 6 to 26 October 2025 through an online form linked from Clubfold and the team chats. Hazel and Hamza Qureshi compiled the summary on 4 November 2025. Answers were anonymous; the form did not ask for names or children's details.

## Summary

The survey gave the board its first numbers on four things we had been arguing about from anecdote: what families think of the fees, why most do not volunteer, how they want to hear from the club, and whether there is demand for more girls' football. It became an input to the fee review, the volunteer recruitment drive, the communications plan and the girls' section.

## Why It Matters

- **Fees.** It gave the [[membership-fee-review-2026]] a sense of the ceiling, and Ravinder Sahota used the 27% figure in his dissent at [[board-meeting-2026-03-10]].
- **Volunteering.** The "don't know what's needed" answer is the main evidence behind Nisha Raghavan's role descriptions ([[volunteer-role-descriptions]]) and the move away from the compulsory pledge ([[board-meeting-2026-02-10]]).
- **Communications.** The 71% figure started the [[club-communications-plan]].
- **Girls.** 19 families with a daughter who would play shaped the [[girls-football-section]].
- **Adults.** The walking football and running answers opened the [[walking-sports]] and [[community-running]] clusters.

## Key Details

Respondents: 142 households (about 68%). 118 had at least one junior member, 24 were adult members only.

**Fees (2025-26 junior fee £140)**
- 61% said the fee was fair or good value; 12% said too high.
- 27% said they would struggle to pay a junior fee above £150.
- 34% would use monthly instalments if offered.

**Volunteering**
- 62% had volunteered at least once in 2024-25; 38% had not.
- Barriers among those who had not (could tick several): not enough time 52%, did not know what help was needed 44%, worried about commitment once started 31%, needed a background check and did not know how 9%.
- 41% said they would do a one-off task if it were clearly described.

**Communications**
- 71% wanted one place to find all club information.
- Average number of club chats per household: 2.6; maximum reported 7.
- Preferred: email newsletter 48%, team messaging channel 39%, social media 8%, website 5%.

**Girls' football and new activities**
- 19 households had a daughter who would play if there were a girls' team near her age.
- 11 adults interested in walking football; 16 in a regular community run.

**Coaching**
- 82% rated coaching good or very good.

## Caveats

Families already involved answered more than families on the edges, so the volunteering figures probably flatter us, and the survey says almost nothing about people who never joined. Percentages are of respondents to each question, not of all 142. The 27% struggle figure was a hypothetical question and should not be read as a prediction of leavers.

## Source Location

Raw responses and the summary sheet are in the club's shared drive under Board / Surveys / 2025, with access for board members only. No personal data was collected.
