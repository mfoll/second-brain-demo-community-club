# Board Meeting 10 February 2026

Monthly board, Tuesday 10 February 2026, 19:30, pavilion meeting room. Nine present, so quorum met; [[nisha-raghavan]] attended for the volunteer item. Minutes by [[joanna-pryce]]; these are my own notes on the decisions.

## At a Glance

> [!note]
> The board scrapped the £30 pledge opt-out fee, kept the three-sessions pledge as a voluntary ask, adopted Nisha's role-based recruitment and agreed that the £930 already collected would go to a hardship fund for fees. The decision was unanimous after a long discussion.

## Background

The [[family-volunteer-pledge]] had been compulsory since September 2025: three volunteer sessions per junior family per season or a £30 opt-out added to fees. In January the board heard Nisha's review ([[pledge-review-2026-01-09]], first discussed at the [[board-meeting-2026-01-13]]): 44% of families had done a session, 31 had paid the opt-out and two families had left over it. The board deferred a decision to February so the options could be written up.

## Options Discussed

Nisha's paper ([[role-based-recruitment-proposal-2026-01]]) set out three options:

1. **Keep and enforce.** Add the opt-out automatically to every family that had not done a session by March. [[donovan-clarke]] argued that the teams still needed the cover and that dropping the rule mid-season would look weak. Nobody else supported enforcement once the likely number of further leavers was discussed.
2. **Drop everything.** Return to asking in the group chat. Rejected: the rota gaps that started the pledge had not gone away.
3. **Drop the fee, keep a voluntary ask, recruit by role.** Publish role descriptions and one-off slots in Clubfold, ask families personally for something specific, and keep "three sessions a season" as a hope rather than a rule.

[[ravinder-sahota]] pointed out that families who had paid the opt-out should not lose out, and that the money had come disproportionately from families who work weekends. [[claire-whitlow]] confirmed £930 had been collected and was sitting in the general account.

## Decisions

- The £30 opt-out fee is scrapped with immediate effect. No further opt-out payments will be taken.
- The pledge stays on the membership form as a voluntary request.
- Role-based recruitment adopted; Nisha to lead a recruitment drive with a target of twelve new regular volunteers by the end of 2026 ([[volunteer-recruitment-drive-2026]]).
- The £930 collected is moved to a ring-fenced fund to help with fees, to be set up properly alongside the fee review. This became the [[hardship-bursary-fund]] after the [[board-meeting-2026-03-10]].
- No refunds to opt-out payers; a letter from me explains where the money went.

Vote: unanimous, with Donovan saying he supported it now that the money was going to families.

## Actions

- Nisha: role descriptions in Clubfold by 1 March; taster morning in early March ([[volunteer-taster-morning-2026-03-07]]).
- Hazel: letter to all junior families explaining the change, by 16 February.
- Claire: move the £930 to a separate cost code.
- Hamza: change the wording on the website and membership form.

## Afterwards

The letter went out on 15 February ([[letter-to-families-on-the-pledge-2026-02]]). I had eleven replies, nine of them relieved, two asking whether they could still help without being on a rota (yes, that is exactly what one-off slots are for). My own reflections are in [[pledge-lessons-learned]].
