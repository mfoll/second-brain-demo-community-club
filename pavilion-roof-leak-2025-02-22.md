# Pavilion Roof Leak in the February Storm, 22 February 2025

The storm on Saturday 22 February 2025 lifted the felt over the pavilion meeting room. Water came through the ceiling above the board table during the under-10s' matches.

## What happened

- Helen noticed water dripping on the meeting room table at about 10:30. Matches carried on; the meeting room and kitchen were closed and the tea hut ran from the doorway.
- Marek came down at lunchtime, put buckets out and turned off the meeting room lights at the consumer unit.
- Lost: a box of minutes from the 1990s stored on the floor, two trophy plinths and the noticeboard. The current minute book was in Joanna's bag.
- Gareth and Yusuf put a tarpaulin over the worst section on the Sunday.

## What followed

- An electrician checked the circuits on 6 March ([[pavilion-electrical-check-2025]]).
- The March board met in the kitchen and started the [[pavilion-roof-repair]].
- Marek's walk-round turned into the [[pavilion-condition-survey-2025]], which in turn started [[new-changing-rooms]].

Lesson for whoever comes next: nothing of value should be stored on the floor of the meeting room. The old minutes that survived are now in the secretary's boxes.
