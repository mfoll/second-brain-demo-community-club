# Coaches Forum 2025-11-19

Mid-season coaches' forum, Wednesday 19 November 2025, pavilion. 19 coaches and managers.

- Hazel presented the [[members-survey-2025]] results that matter to coaches: families rate coaching highly (82% good or very good) but 44% of non-volunteers said they did not know what help was needed.
- Coaches asked for one fixtures channel per team instead of the club-wide chat. This fed the [[club-communications-plan]].
- Several coaches of older age groups described players stopping at 13 or 14 because of exams, other sports or friends leaving. Agreed to track leavers with a short reason in Clubfold.
- Leanne reported 18 girls in the girls' section after one term.
- Tom Ashworth asked for a session-plan library for the youngest age groups; Donovan to set up a shared folder.
