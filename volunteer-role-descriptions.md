# Volunteer Role Descriptions

[[nisha-raghavan]]'s set of role descriptions and one-off slots, published in Clubfold on 1 March 2026 and used for all volunteer recruitment since.

## Summary

27 role descriptions for regular roles and 11 one-off slot types. Each description fits on one screen and answers the same questions: what the job is, when and for how long, who you work with and report to, whether a background check and safeguarding training are needed, what training we give, and what to do if you cannot make a week. Nisha drafted them from conversations with the people already doing each job, then [[celine-dubois]] at the Draymoor Volunteer Centre read them through.

## Key Details

Regular roles, grouped as Nisha grouped them:

- **Match day:** tea hut lead and helpers, car park steward, linesperson (club assistant referee), pitch setup, first-aider on duty.
- **Team:** team manager, assistant coach, kit washer, fixtures contact.
- **Club running:** rota administrator, deputy fixtures secretary, newsletter editor, social media and photos, kit and equipment ([[yusuf-demir]]'s role was the template), groundsman's helper.
- **Welfare:** deputy welfare officer, changing-room door adult ([[run-the-saturday-changing-room-rota]]).

One-off slots, bookable in Clubfold with no further commitment: tea hut helper, car park steward, pitch setup, line marking, tournament helper, kit sort, fun run marshal and four others.

Typical time asks: two hours for a match-day slot; three to five hours a week for team manager; about two hours a week for rota administrator.

Roles that bring a junior into contact with the volunteer are marked "check needed", and Clubfold will not let a volunteer book them until their check and training show as current.

## Why It Matters

The descriptions are the core of the [[volunteer-recruitment-drive-2026]], which replaced the compulsory [[family-volunteer-pledge]] after the [[board-meeting-2026-02-10]]. They also feed [[safer-recruitment]], because the "check needed" flag is how we stop someone helping with juniors before they are cleared. Three of the seven new regulars so far started in one-off slots.

## Caveats

- Hours are Nisha's estimates from talking to role holders, not timed.
- Some roles, such as team manager, vary a lot by age group.
- The descriptions need a light review each June before the season; nobody owns that yet.
