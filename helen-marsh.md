# Helen Marsh

Tea hut and events volunteer for eleven years. Runs the tea hut on home Saturdays and the catering at the summer tournament.

## Role

- Tea hut lead: stock, float, food hygiene certificate, and a rota of about ten helpers.
- Updates the printed rota sheet on the pavilion door every Friday for families without the app.
- Organises food for the thank-you evening and the [[summer-tournament-2026]].

## How We Work Together

Helen is one of the people [[volunteer-burnout]] is about. She is there every home Saturday and most of the summer, and she will not say no. Since early 2026 Nisha has put two one-off helpers on the tea hut every week so Helen can take a proper break for part of the morning. She prefers a phone call to a message.

## Current Threads

- Planning food for the November thank-you evening.
- Training two tea hut helpers to run it without her ([[volunteer-recognition-ideas]] has the long-service nomination I want to make).

## Background With the Club

Helen started helping in the tea hut in 2015 when her own children played, and kept going after they left. She is one of the three people who received a long-service mention at the [[volunteer-thank-you-evening-2025-11-21]]. She has a food hygiene certificate the club paid for in 2024, renewed every three years.
