# Yusuf Demir

Kit and equipment volunteer since 2023.

## Role

- Looks after match kit, training equipment, nets, corner flags and the first-aid kits in the container behind the pavilion.
- Issues team kit at the start of each season and chases its return in May.
- Checks and restocks the six first-aid kits monthly, logging it on the kit sheet.

## How We Work Together

Yusuf works shifts, so he does most of his club work on weekday mornings and messages photos of what needs replacing. His role description was the first one Nisha wrote, because nobody else knew what the job involved. He has asked for one person to shadow him so the kit side does not stop when he is away.

## Current Threads

- Finding a kit shadow through a one-off slot ([[volunteer-recruitment-drive-2026]]).
- Replacing two first-aid kits before the winter.

## Background With the Club

Yusuf took on kit in 2023 when the previous kit volunteer moved away and left a container nobody could make sense of. He sorted it over one winter, labelled every box and started the kit sheet. He also checks the first-aid kits against the list in the club's first-aid guidance, and flags anything used so it can be replaced before the next weekend.
