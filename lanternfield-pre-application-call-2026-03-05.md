# Pre-Application Call with the Lanternfield Trust, 5 March 2026

Forty-minute video call on Thursday 5 March 2026 with Esther Lowell. Marek and I on the club side.

## What we learned

- Tenure: 25 years from the date of the offer, never waived. A cabinet resolution would count; heads of terms alone would not.
- Safeguarding and access should be part of the reason for the project, not paragraphs added at the end. She asked whether we had a current safeguarding policy and whether it was being reviewed.
- Demand: they want evidence the club has tested demand, not just asserted it. The [[members-survey-2025]] and girls' section numbers would help.
- Maintenance: applicants often underwrite running costs. Have a plan.
- Round 2 closes 30 September; decisions in February.

## What we decided afterwards

- Aim for round 2 of 2026, which means the lease needs to move faster.
- Ask Aoife whether the policy review could look at the changing rooms early (it did, from 20 April).
- Start the Community Chest application for the second part of the [[match-funding]].

My notes from the call are the basis of the plan in [[changing-rooms-grant-application]].
