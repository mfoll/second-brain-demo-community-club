# Imran Sheikh

Chair of the Fernhollow Residents' Association. Our main contact with the neighbours round Millrace Lane.

## Role in our work

- Sent the Residents' Association's complaint to the council about tournament parking after the July 2026 tournament ([[residents-parking-complaint-2026-07]]).
- Met me, Sam Okonjo and Anita Venkatesh on 22 July 2026 to talk it through ([[residents-association-meeting-2026-07-22]]).
- The Residents' Association also wrote a letter of support for the changing rooms grant, which says something about how the relationship stands ([[letters-of-support-changing-rooms]]).

## How we work together

Imran is reasonable and organised; he represents people who were genuinely blocked in on Saturday 4 July, and he does not overstate it. He wants to be consulted before the event rather than apologised to after it. I have promised him a draft parking plan before Christmas 2026. He reads the Gazette and our newsletter, so anything we publish about the ground reaches the residents through him.
