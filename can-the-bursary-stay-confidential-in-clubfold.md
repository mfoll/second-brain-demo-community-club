# Can the Bursary Stay Confidential in Clubfold?

When the bursary was set up in March 2026, could Clubfold record reduced fees without coaches, team managers or other volunteers seeing which children had them?

## Current Answer

Yes, with care. [[ravinder-sahota]] tested it with a dummy account before the first award:

- Bursaries are a fee adjustment under a restricted code; only the "Bursary" role (Ravinder and Claire) can see the code or the reason.
- Team managers see "paid" or "not paid", nothing else.
- Payment reports for the board show the bursary total as one line.
- Exports are switched off for the Bursary role.

The one gap: Clubfold's default receipt email showed "adjustment applied". Ravinder changed the template so the family sees only the amount paid. Checked again in September 2026 after a Clubfold update. See [[bursary-awards-tracker]].
