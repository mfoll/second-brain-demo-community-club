# Changing Rooms Grant

Win £185,000 from the Lanternfield Trust Community Facilities Fund towards the £248,000 new changing rooms.

## Outcome

£185,000 awarded by the Lanternfield Trust Community Facilities Fund. Application submitted 30 September 2026; decision expected February 2027.

## Current State

- Submitted on Wednesday 30 September 2026, round 2 of 2026 ([[changing-rooms-grant-submission-2026-09-30]]). Acknowledged by the trust on 2 October.
- We asked for £185,000, 74.6% of the £248,000 cost, just under the fund's 75% ceiling.
- Security of tenure is not yet evidenced. The trust needs 25 years; our lease ends 31 March 2031. Esther Lowell confirmed on 9 October that we have until 15 December 2026 to send a signed lease or a cabinet resolution, or the application rolls to round 1 of 2027 ([[lanternfield-tenure-deadline-email-2026-10-09]]).
- Match funding is fully pledged: £25,000 reserves and £15,000 Community Chest are committed; the £23,000 appeal is counted as pledged with £14,600 banked by 20 October.

## Scope and Boundaries

This project is the application and the relationship with the trust until a decision. The lease itself is [[pitch-lease-extension]]; the appeal is [[match-funding-appeal]]. If the grant is awarded, the offer letter and claims process will start a new child project under [[new-changing-rooms]].

## Working Context

We first talked to Esther Lowell on 5 March 2026 ([[lanternfield-pre-application-call-2026-03-05]]). She was clear about three things: the 25-year tenure rule is never waived, the trust likes to see safeguarding and access as reasons rather than add-ons, and it scores how well the club has tested demand. That shaped the application:

- Need: 161 juniors and 59 adults using one shared 1974 block with no separate officials' or accessible room; the girls' section at 31 players and growing; Saturday overlap of adult and junior sessions.
- Safeguarding: the [[changing-room-supervision-rules]] in force from 5 September 2026 are a stopgap that depends on two adults being free every Saturday. Separate junior rooms remove the overlap problem by design. Aoife wrote the safeguarding paragraph herself; no cases are described or hinted at.
- Access: no step-free changing at the ground at all at present. The accessible room helps the masters group and visiting teams.
- Demand: the [[members-survey-2025]] (142 families) and girls' football growth.
- Deliverability: fixed quote, pro bono design, a facilities lead who managed school building sites for 30 years, letters of support ([[letters-of-support-changing-rooms]]).

The application form text is filed as [[lanternfield-application-form-2026]]. Claire's budget pages reference [[club-budget-2026-27]].
