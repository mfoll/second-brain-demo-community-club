# Rebecca Thorne

Safeguarding officer at Northmere Football Association, covering about 80 affiliated clubs in the region.

## Role

- Our first call for safeguarding advice on football; she also escalates concerns to the association when the policy requires it.
- Maintains the [[northmere-fa-safeguarding-template]] that the 2022 policy and the 2026 draft both follow.
- Ran the in-person workshop at the pavilion on 19 September 2026 ([[safeguarding-workshop-2026-09-19]]).

## How We Work Together

[[aoife-brennan]] is the main contact; I am copied on policy matters. Rebecca is quick and practical by email and prefers a call for anything sensitive. She reviewed our interim changing-room rules ([[email-from-rebecca-thorne-on-changing-rooms-2026-06]]) and the draft policy, and asked for two changes to reporting timescales. She knows the 1974 pavilion from a club visit in 2023.

## Current Threads

- Final check on the revised policy before board adoption on 17 November 2026.
- Possible second workshop in January 2027 for new recruits.

## Background

Rebecca has been the association's safeguarding officer for about six years. She ran the welfare officer course that both Aoife and [[beatriz-ferreira]] attended. Her model policy ([[northmere-fa-safeguarding-template]]) is used by most clubs in the area, so she can tell us quickly how others handle a problem without naming them.
