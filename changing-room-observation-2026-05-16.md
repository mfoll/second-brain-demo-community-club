# Changing Room Observation 16 May 2026

The third of three Saturday observations of the 1974 changing rooms (2, 9 and 16 May 2026) by [[aoife-brennan]], [[beatriz-ferreira]] and me, for the [[safeguarding-policy-review]]. We recorded times and headcounts only: who was in the room by group (junior team, adult team, officials), never names.

## What We Found

Across the three Saturdays ([[changing-room-usage-log-saturdays-2026]]):

- Adults and juniors were in the shared room at the same time on all three days, for between 10 and 25 minutes.
- The overlap happened between about 09:45 and 10:30, when the under-14s and under-16s were changing after early games and the adult men or women arrived for 11:00 kick-offs.
- Referees changed in the same room or in their cars; there is no officials' room.
- There was one adult in the room supervising juniors on two of the three days, with no second adult nearby.
- Phones were out in the room on all three days, mostly for music.

Nobody behaved badly. The building and the fixture times made a safe arrangement impossible without rules.

## What Followed

- Neil, who joined the first observation with [[marek-zielinski]], redrew the design on 19 May to give two junior rooms and an officials' room ([[changing-rooms-design-revision-2026-05-19]]). Our written design requirements went to Marek and [[neil-cartwright]] on 20 May ([[changing-room-design-requirements]]) and confirmed it.
- Interim rules drafted over the summer and in force from 5 September ([[changing-room-interim-rules-start-2026-09-05]]).
