# Changing Room Usage Log Saturdays 2026

The combined log from three Saturday observations of the pavilion changing rooms, 2, 9 and 16 May 2026, typed up by [[beatriz-ferreira]]. Times and headcounts by group only; no names.

## Key Details

| Saturday | Overlap window | Longest overlap | Groups overlapping | Second adult present |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 May | 09:50 to 10:15 | 25 min | Under-16s and adult men | No |
| 9 May | 10:05 to 10:20 | 15 min | Under-14s and adult women | Yes |
| 16 May | 09:45 to 09:55 | 10 min | Under-14s and referees | No |

Other observations: phones out on all three days; referees changing in cars on two days; the room at capacity (about 22 people) twice.

## Why It Matters

The evidence base for the interim rules and for the design brief ([[changing-room-design-requirements]]). The grant application quotes the overlap finding in summary ([[changing-rooms-grant-submission-2026-09-30]]).

## Caveats

Three Saturdays at the end of the season, when fixtures are irregular. Autumn Saturdays may be busier.
