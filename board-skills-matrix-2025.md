# Board Skills Matrix 2025

## Summary

The output of the [[board-skills-audit]]: a one-sheet grid of the nine board members against 14 skills, scored 0 (none) to 3 (could teach it), from self-assessment and the workshop on 11 November 2025. Circulated 27 November 2025.

## Key Details

Skills with only one person scoring 2 or more:

- Accounts and budgeting: [[claire-whitlow]] only.
- Building and facilities projects: [[marek-zielinski]] only.
- Grant writing: nobody above 1 (Claire later proved herself wrong).

Skills nobody owned at all: planned communications; legal and contracts.

Well covered: coaching (five members), safeguarding awareness (all nine trained), knowledge of local families, working with the council.

## Why It Matters

It named three gaps: treasurer succession, communications and facilities project management. Communications went to [[hamza-qureshi]]; the treasurer gap is still open ([[who-will-take-over-as-treasurer]]). The legal gap has come back as the [[club-incorporation-review]].

## Caveats

Self-scores. People underrate what they do every week and overrate what they did once.
