# Northmere FA Safeguarding Template

Northmere Football Association's model safeguarding policy for affiliated clubs, version 2025.2, issued March 2025 and maintained by [[rebecca-thorne]].

## Summary

A model policy that clubs adapt. It sets the minimum the association expects for affiliation: a named welfare officer, a reporting route to the association, safer recruitment, codes of conduct, supervision ratios, photography rules and training. Clubs fill in their own names, venues and arrangements.

## Key Details

- Minimum ratios for junior football by age group, with at least two adults in all cases.
- Welfare officer must complete the association's welfare course; a deputy is recommended for clubs with more than 100 juniors.
- Safeguarding training renewed every three years ([[northmere-fa-safeguarding-training-requirements]]).
- A changing-room section assuming separate adult and junior facilities, with a short note on "where facilities are shared".
- Policy reviewed by the club at least every three years.

## Why It Matters

The 2022 policy followed the 2021 version closely, and [[aoife-brennan]] used 2025.2 as the backbone for the 2026 draft ([[safeguarding-policy-draft-2026-09]]). The "shared facilities" note was too thin for our pavilion, which is why we wrote our own interim rules.
