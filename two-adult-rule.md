# Two-Adult Rule

The principle that no adult is alone with a junior in a closed or private setting, and that supervision of juniors involves at least two adults, so that children are protected and adults are not placed in a position where they could be misunderstood.

## How We Apply It

- Training sessions and matches: at least two adults with current training and checks for each junior group, whatever the ratio says.
- Changing rooms: two adults outside the door while juniors change, never one adult inside ([[changing-room-supervision-rules-2026]]).
- Lifts to matches: no adult drives a single junior who is not their own child, unless the parent has arranged it directly.
- Messaging: no one-to-one private messages between adults and juniors; team communication goes through the parent-managed channels ([[codes-of-conduct-2026]]).
- First aid: treat in view of another adult wherever possible.

## Who Counts as an Adult

A volunteer aged 18 or over with current safeguarding training and a background check. Under-18 helpers never count ([[should-under-18-helpers-count-in-supervision-ratios]]).

## Why It Matters to Us

It is the rule most affected by our building. The shared 1974 changing rooms make "never one adult with juniors in private" hard on busy Saturdays, which is why the interim rules exist and why the new changing rooms have separate junior rooms ([[changing-room-design-requirements]]). It also adds to volunteer demand: every door slot is two adults.
