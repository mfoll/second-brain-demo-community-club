# Rhys Penhallow

Ward councillor for Fernhollow. Not on cabinet, but well connected and willing to speak for local groups.

## Role in our work

- Spoke in favour of the Millrace Lane lease at the cabinet meeting on 17 September 2026 and asked for a firm date when it was deferred; that is how we got 19 November rather than "the new year" ([[council-cabinet-deferral-2026-09-17]]).
- Wrote a letter of support for the Lanternfield application ([[letters-of-support-changing-rooms]]).
- Came to the presentation evening in June 2026 and to the summer tournament.

## How we work together

Rhys likes to be kept informed before things happen rather than asked for help after. I send him a short update every month or so, and only ask for something specific when officers have done what they can. He is careful not to look as if he is favouring one club, so I never ask him to lobby officers directly. He also hears from the Residents' Association about parking, and has been even-handed about it.

## Current threads

- Making sure the lease is first on the cabinet agenda on 19 November.
- The 2027 tournament parking plan, which he wants to see before it goes to the council.
