# Changing Rooms Appeal Leaflet, June 2026

The A5 leaflet launching the appeal, handed out at the presentation evening and posted on the club's channels in June 2026. Hamza laid it out; I wrote the text.

## Summary

- Headline: "New changing rooms for Millrace Lane: help us raise £23,000".
- Why: one shared changing room since 1974, two showers, no space for referees, no step-free changing, girls' and boys' teams sharing.
- How the money works: a big grant we have applied for, money the club has set aside, a council grant we have applied for, and the appeal.
- Ways to give: online giving page, cash at the tea hut, name-a-peg, events.
- A promise that every penny goes to the building and is refunded if it does not go ahead.

## Why It Matters

It set the tone: plain, no pressure, no pictures of juniors. The line about refunds came from Ravinder, and I think it helped. The text is the basis for any later appeal update in the newsletter ([[match-funding-appeal]]).
