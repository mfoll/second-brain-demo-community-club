# Volunteer Expenses Policy

The club's one-page policy on out-of-pocket expenses for volunteers, revised by [[claire-whitlow]] and approved by the board on 9 September 2025.

## Key Details

- Volunteers can claim agreed out-of-pocket costs: equipment bought for the club, training course fees, mileage to away fixtures over 15 miles at 30p a mile.
- Claims within 60 days, with receipts, through Clubfold's expenses form.
- Paid within 14 days of approval.
- No payment for time, ever.
- Coaching course fees reimbursed in full once the course is passed.

In 2025-26 the club paid £1,140 in volunteer expenses, mostly coaching courses and mileage.

## Why It Matters

Prompt expenses are one of the Volunteer Centre's good-practice items ([[draymoor-volunteer-centre-good-practice-checklist]]). It also matters for the line between thanks and payment ([[motivation-crowding-out]]): we reimburse costs and never pay for time.
