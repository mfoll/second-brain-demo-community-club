# Should Fees Use a Sliding Scale?

Should junior fees from 2027-28 be banded by what families can afford, as [[ravinder-sahota]] proposed, instead of one flat fee plus a bursary?

## Why It Matters

The board chose a flat rise with a bursary in March 2026 ([[board-meeting-2026-03-10]]) and minuted Ravinder's request to revisit it after one season. It comes back to the board in January 2027.

## What We Know

- Ravinder's proposal: £120, £165 and £190 bands, chosen on trust ([[ravinder-sliding-scale-proposal]]).
- The bursary reached 14 families by October, with no evidence asked for ([[bursary-awards-tracker]]).
- Four of 22 junior leavers gave cost as their reason ([[clubfold-membership-export-2026-10]]).
- Clubfold can now handle fee bands; it could not in March.

## Current Answer

> [!question]
> Open. My leaning is that a sliding scale is fairer in principle and less awkward than asking, but I want to see whether the bursary families would have chosen the lowest band and whether enough families would pay the higher one.

## What Would Settle It

A full season of bursary figures, Claire's modelling of band take-up, and what families told Ravinder about asking for help.
