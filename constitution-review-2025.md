# Constitution Review 2025

Rewriting the club constitution, last revised in 1998, so that it matched how we actually work and stopped any of us holding an office indefinitely.

## Outcome

New constitution adopted at the AGM on 14 June 2025, with a six-year limit on consecutive officer terms and a transitional clause letting current officers serve until the 2027 AGM. Done.

## Current State

- Completed. Adopted at the AGM on 14 June 2025 by 41 votes to 3 with 2 abstentions ([[agm-2025-06-14]]).
- The constitution is now the reference for the succession work ([[chair-succession-plan]]) and for any move to incorporate ([[club-incorporation-review]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

We changed the rules on officers, the board, meetings, membership classes and dissolution. We deliberately did not change the club's legal form: the working group thought incorporation deserved its own decision, which came back in 2026.

## Working Context

The 1998 document ([[fernhollow-constitution-1998]]) had no limits on terms, a quorum of four for a board that had grown to nine, and no mention of junior members' voice, safeguarding or electronic voting. Colin chaired for eleven years and I was heading the same way.

The working group was [[joanna-pryce]], [[ravinder-sahota]] and me, with comments from [[aoife-brennan]] on the welfare officer's place. We used the Northmere FA model constitution as a checklist and held a members' evening on 25 March 2025 ([[constitution-consultation-evening-2025-03-25]]).

Main decisions, with the reasons in [[constitution-review-consultation-2025]]:

- Six consecutive years for any officer, then at least one year off ([[officer-term-limits]]).
- A transitional clause, so the four officers then in post could serve to the 2027 AGM rather than all leaving at once.
- Board quorum raised to five.
- The welfare officer attends the board ex officio without a vote, so welfare reports are never caught up in other votes.
- A dissenting board member may ask for their dissent to be minuted ([[minuted-dissent]]).
- Co-chairs allowed if the AGM agrees.

Rejected: a three-year limit (too short for a small club), and a youth seat on the board (we set up a youth forum instead).
