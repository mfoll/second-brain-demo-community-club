# Flat roof

English Wikipedia article, last edited 5 October 2026. Licence CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Summary

The article describes a flat roof as one that is almost level, unlike sloped roofs. It notes that a roof's slope is properly called its pitch, and that flat roofs have a pitch of up to about 10 degrees.

## Why It Matters

The pavilion's meeting room and kitchen have a felted flat roof, and I read this before the board compared the three roof quotes in March 2025 so I could follow Marek's explanation of why water had pooled and found its way in ([[pavilion-roof-quotes-2025]]). It helped me ask the roofer sensible questions about falls and drainage when we chose the £6,800 option ([[pavilion-roof-repair]]).
