# Hamza Qureshi

Board member for communications since the 2024 AGM. Works full-time in marketing and coaches nobody, which gives him a useful outsider's view.

## Role

- Took on communications after the [[board-skills-audit]] showed nobody owned it; leads the [[club-communications-plan]].
- Proposed the co-chair model at the board on 8 September 2026 ([[hamza-co-chair-proposal-2026-09]], [[board-meeting-2026-09-08]]).

## The co-chair proposal

Hamza's case is that the chair's job, as I have done it, is too much for one volunteer with a full-time job, and that the club should not choose a successor by who has the most spare time. He suggested co-chairs, himself and [[femi-oyelaran]], with Femi leading on the board, football and the council, and Hamza on communications, fundraising and the trust. He was clear he would support Femi as sole chair if the board preferred it.

## How we work together

Quick, positive, sometimes ahead of the rest of us. He likes to try things and is good at taking "not yet" for an answer. He prefers messages to meetings. I check his proposals with Joanna against the constitution before they go to the board, which he welcomes.
