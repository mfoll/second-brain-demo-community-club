# Run the Saturday Changing Room Rota

How the interim changing-room rules ([[changing-room-supervision-rules-2026]]) are run each home Saturday in the shared 1974 pavilion.

## Before the Weekend

1. **Monday:** [[martin-goss]] sends the fixture times for the Saturday after next.
2. **By Sunday:** the rota admin sets changing slots in Clubfold: each junior team gets a 20-minute slot before and after its game; adult teams change in gaps or arrive changed. Door-adult slots (two per junior slot) are created with "check needed".
3. **Wednesday:** the rota admin checks door slots are filled; gaps go to one-off volunteers with current checks.

## On the Day

1. The first door adult unlocks the meeting room for officials and puts up the slot notice.
2. During each junior slot, two door adults in yellow bibs stand outside the changing-room door. Neither goes in unless a child needs help, and then both go, or the door is propped open.
3. No adult changes while juniors are in the room. Adults wait in the corridor or change at home.
4. No phones in the changing room, for anyone. Remind politely; do not confiscate.
5. If slots slip because a game overran, juniors keep their slot and adults wait.

## Guardrails

- Under-18 helpers never act as door adults ([[should-under-18-helpers-count-in-supervision-ratios]]).
- If only one door adult is available, the junior team changes at home or after the adults have left; the slot does not run with one adult.
- Anything worrying goes to the welfare officer the same day ([[report-a-welfare-concern]]).

## Verification

The rota admin checks on Monday that every junior slot had two door adults recorded; any slot that did not is reported to [[aoife-brennan]].
