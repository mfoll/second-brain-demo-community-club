# Board Induction Pack Outline

What every new board member should get, drafted in October 2026 so the new board after the 2027 AGM has it. Not yet assembled.

1. A one-page welcome: what the club is, the nine board roles, meeting dates.
2. The [[fernhollow-constitution-2025]] with the clauses that matter most marked: 6, 7, 8 and 9.
3. The last six months of approved minutes ([[board-minutes-archive]]) and the [[board-decision-log]].
4. The current budget summary ([[club-budget-2026-27]]).
5. Safeguarding: the policy, the welfare officer's contact, and the training they must do within three months ([[fernhollow-safeguarding-policy]]).
6. Confidentiality: bursaries and welfare matters never discussed outside the board, and never with names.
7. The [[conflict-of-interest-register]] form.
8. Who to ask: the chair, the secretary, the treasurer.

Keep it short; 20 pages at most. Joanna to assemble by May 2027.
