# Should the Club Incorporate Before the Lease?

Should Fernhollow become an incorporated body before signing the 30-year Millrace Lane lease and the £248,000 building contract, or sign as an association and incorporate afterwards?

## What We Know

- As an unincorporated association, the officers who sign are the club's legal face ([[personal-liability-in-an-unincorporated-club]]).
- Incorporating takes six to twelve months for a volunteer group ([[incorporation-options-briefing]]).
- The Lanternfield deadline for lease evidence is 15 December 2026; cabinet decides on 19 November ([[council-cabinet-deferral-2026-09-17]]).

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> Probably not before. Sign the lease as an association if cabinet approves it, then incorporate during 2027 and transfer the lease and contract with the council's and the trust's consent. Not yet agreed by the board.

## What Would Settle It

Whether the council would consent to transfer the lease later ([[graham-sutcliffe]]), and whether the trust would accept a change of legal form mid-award.
