# Tournament Planning Meeting 2026-03-24

Planning meeting for the summer tournament, Tuesday 24 March 2026, pavilion. Sam Okonjo, Hazel, Helen Marsh, Martin Goss, Nisha Raghavan.

## Decisions

- Cap at 52 teams. Sam's pitch plan shows 52 is the most the three mini pitches and two full-size pitches can take without cutting games below 10 minutes.
- Entry fee £60 per team (£50 in 2025). Nobody objected; neighbouring tournaments charge £60 to £75.
- Entries open 1 April through Clubfold, closing 31 May or at 52.
- Saturday for under-7 to under-10 festivals, Sunday for under-11 to under-14 competition.
- Volunteer slots go on the Clubfold rota by 1 May: 64 slots across both days ([[tournament-volunteer-plan-2026]]).
- First aid cover booked for both days.

## Not decided

- How much goes to the changing rooms appeal. Hazel to take it to the May board.
- Parking. Sam raised it; we agreed to "put two volunteers on the gate again" and come back to it if entries reached the cap.

The first schedule draft from this meeting is in [[summer-tournament-2026-draft-schedule]].
