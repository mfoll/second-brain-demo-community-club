# Clubfold Rota Setup Notes

Handover notes on how the Clubfold rota is configured and why, written by [[nisha-raghavan]] and me at the end of the [[volunteer-rota-overhaul]] in August 2025, updated October 2026. For whoever becomes the next rota admin.

## Structure

- One rota template per team (13 football, 1 athletics) plus a "club" template for tea hut, car park, line marking, kit and the changing-room door slots.
- Slots are created automatically from the fixture list each Sunday for two weeks ahead.
- Slot types match the [[volunteer-role-descriptions]]. Slots marked "check needed" can only be booked by volunteers whose training and check show as current.

## Settings We Chose Deliberately

- One reminder at 48 hours. Two reminders annoyed people in the pilot ([[clubfold-rota-pilot-2025-05-10]]).
- Team managers can swap slots within their team; only admins can change templates.
- Swaps must be done in the app, not by private message, or the printed sheet goes wrong.
- Fill-rate report emailed to the admins every Monday.

## Weekly Routine

1. Sunday evening: check the new fortnight has generated, fill obvious gaps by personal message, publish.
2. Wednesday: look at unfilled slots for Saturday; ask one-off volunteers.
3. Friday: export the printed sheet for the pavilion door; [[helen-marsh]] puts it up.

## Known Quirks

- Rearranged fixtures do not move their slots automatically; delete and recreate.
- Changing-room slot times must be entered by hand from [[martin-goss]]'s schedule.
