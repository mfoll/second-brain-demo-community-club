# Community Asset Review

Draymoor Borough Council's review, started in August 2026, of the roughly 40 leases of council land and buildings to community groups. In this vault the term means that specific review and what it does to our lease.

## What it is looking at

Rent levels, repairing obligations, community use clauses and how community benefit is measured ([[community-asset-review-briefing-2026-08]]). Interim findings to cabinet in November 2026, final report spring 2027.

## Why it matters to us

It is why cabinet deferred our 30-year lease on 17 September 2026. The council does not want to sign new long leases that the review might want to change. Officers think our heads of terms already fit what the review is likely to say.

## How to read it

Treat it as timing risk, not a threat to the lease itself, unless the interim findings say otherwise. If they propose higher rents for community leases, Claire will need to re-run the comparison with pitch hire ([[pitch-hire-schedule-2026-27]]).
