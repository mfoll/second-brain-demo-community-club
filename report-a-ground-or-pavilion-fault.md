# Report A Ground Or Pavilion Fault

What to do when something is broken, unsafe or worn at Millrace Lane. For coaches, team managers, tea hut volunteers and keyholders.

## Steps

1. Make it safe. Tape off, close the room or move people away. If anyone is hurt, first aid first and then the accident book in the kitchen.
2. Decide how urgent it is:
   - Urgent (water, electrics, a broken lock, a goal that is not secure, a fire door): phone Marek. If he does not answer, phone me.
   - Pitch problems (holes, waterlogging, broken nets): text Gareth with a photo.
   - Everything else: fill in the fault form on Clubfold (Facilities > Report a fault) with a photo.
3. Do not attempt repairs to electrics, gas, the changing block roof (it contains asbestos) or anything above head height.
4. Do not move goals alone; two adults always.
5. If a match has to be called off because of a pitch or building problem, tell Martin Goss, who deals with the league.

## What happens next

Marek reviews the Clubfold fault list every Monday and reports anything costly to the board. Gareth adds pitch issues to the [[pitch-condition-log-2025-26]]. Repairs over £250 need the treasurer's approval.

## Not for this route

Safeguarding concerns go to the welfare officer through the confidential route in the [[fernhollow-safeguarding-policy]], never on the fault form.
