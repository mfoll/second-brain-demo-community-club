# Quorum

## Summary

English Wikipedia article (last edited 13 September 2026 when rechecked). It defines a quorum as the smallest number of a group's members who must be present for a meeting to act for the group, and distinguishes it from a plenum, a meeting of the whole body. A meeting or vote with enough members present is described as quorate.

## Why It Matters

I read it in February 2025 when the constitution working group was deciding the board quorum. The old rule of four, for a board that had grown to nine, meant fewer than half the board could commit the club. We raised it to five, and set the AGM quorum at 20 members ([[fernhollow-constitution-2025]]). It also settled a point Joanna raised: the welfare officer attends without a vote, so she does not count towards the quorum ([[how-to-run-a-board-meeting]]).

## Caveats

General background only. What counts as a quorum for us is whatever our constitution says.
