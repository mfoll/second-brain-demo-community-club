# Voluntary association

## Summary

English Wikipedia article (last edited 2 March 2026 when rechecked). It defines a voluntary group or union as people who agree, usually as volunteers, to form a body for a shared purpose, and gives examples such as trade associations, learned societies, service organisations and environmental groups.

## Why It Matters

Background reading at the start of the [[constitution-review-2025]]. Clause 1 of the old constitution called us "a club of members" and nothing more. Reading this helped Joanna and me write a clearer status clause: a not-for-profit members' association whose members agree to the constitution when they join ([[fernhollow-constitution-2025]]). It also reminded us that the members, not the board, own the club, which is why constitutional changes go to the AGM.

## Caveats

Very general; it covers bodies far larger than ours.
