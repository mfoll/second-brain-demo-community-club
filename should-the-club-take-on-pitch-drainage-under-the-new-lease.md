# Should the club take on pitch drainage under the new lease?

## Question

The council's first draft of the 30-year lease made the club responsible for all pitch maintenance including drainage. Should we accept that?

## Why It Matters

Pitch 2 lost 14 fixtures to waterlogging in 2025-26 ([[pitch-condition-log-2025-26]]). Relaying field drains on one pitch could cost well over £20,000, which the club could never fund from fees.

## Where it stands

The heads of terms of July 2026 split it: the council keeps the main surface water drains; the club does routine maintenance. Gareth's view is that the problem on pitch 2 is the field drains, not the main drains, and that the wording leaves it unclear who fixes those. The solicitor has been asked to look at exactly this ([[club-solicitor-lease-quote-2026-10]]).

## Current Answer

Accept routine maintenance; do not accept field or main drainage. Kept open until the lease wording is final.
