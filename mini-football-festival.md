# Mini Football Festival

A festival is how the youngest children play matches: several teams meet at one ground, play a series of short small-sided games against different opponents, and nobody records results or league positions.

## How we run them

- Under-7s and under-8s, 5-a-side on the mini pitches, games of 8 to 10 minutes.
- Every child plays roughly equal time; rolling substitutions.
- No tables, no trophies, a sticker or certificate for everyone.
- Coaches stay on the touchline and keep instructions to a minimum. Parents stay behind a rope line two metres back.

## Why

More touches of the ball per child, more games, less pressure, and no early sorting into "good" and "not good" players that mostly reflects birth month ([[wikipedia-relative-age-effect]]). The Saturday of the [[summer-tournament-2026]] uses the same format for under-7 to under-10.

## Limits

Some parents read festivals as "not real football". The answer is to explain it at registration, not to argue on the touchline.
