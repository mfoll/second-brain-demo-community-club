# Luke Garvey

Head of PE at Oakreach School. The person who made the track swap happen and our everyday contact at the school.

## Role in our work

- Proposed the swap with Sheila Mortimer and me in summer 2025 and still champions it.
- Hosts the Wednesday after-school athletics club that Kwabena Osei runs; a PE teacher is always on site.
- Books the Tuesday track slot and sorts out the floodlights and the caretaker.

Termly check-ins, usually a coffee in the PE office after the after-school club.
