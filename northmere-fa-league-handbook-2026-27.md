# Northmere FA League Handbook 2026-27

The Northmere Football Association's junior and adult league handbook for 2026-27, received by Martin Goss on 28 July 2026.

## Summary

The rules we must meet to enter teams: affiliation, coach qualifications, pitch sizes by age, match lengths, registration deadlines and the festival rules for the youngest age groups.

## Key Details

- Team entries for 2026-27 closed 14 August 2026. All 13 of our teams entered on 10 August.
- Every team must name a coach with a current association coaching qualification and a current background check by 31 December.
- Under-7s and under-8s: festivals only, no results recorded, 5-a-side.
- Under-9s and under-10s: 7-a-side; under-11s and under-12s: 9-a-side; under-13s up: 11-a-side.
- Girls' teams may include girls up to one year older than the age group, with approval.
- Home clubs must provide a referee for under-12 and younger games; the league appoints referees from under-13 up.

## Why It Matters

The coach qualification deadline set the one hard date in the [[season-2026-27-plan]]. The festival rule for under-8s settled most of the argument in [[should-under-7s-play-festivals-not-fixtures]].
