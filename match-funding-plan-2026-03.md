# Match Funding Plan, March 2026

Archived: my first plan for the changing rooms match funding, written after the trust call on 5 March 2026. Replaced by the split in [[match-funding]] once the reserves decision and the Community Chest award came through.

## The plan as it was

At that point we were working to the old estimate of £227,000, so the club needed about £57,000 against a grant of 75%.

- Reserves: £20,000 (to be agreed by the board).
- Appeal: £37,000 to £38,000, if no other grant came through.
- Possible Community Chest bid of up to £15,000, which would bring the appeal down to about £23,000.

## Why it changed

The May redesign raised the cost to £248,000 and the match to £63,000. The board committed £25,000 of reserves on 12 May, and the Community Chest awarded £15,000 on 20 August ([[community-chest-award-2026-08-20]]). The appeal settled at £23,000. Kept to show how close we came to needing a £38,000 appeal.
