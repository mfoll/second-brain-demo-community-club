# Pavilion Roof Quotes, March 2025

Three quotes Marek collected for the pavilion roof after the storm, compared on one page for the board on 11 March 2025.

## Summary

- Option 1, patch only: £3,100, no guarantee, "one or two winters".
- Option 2, replace felt and flashings over the meeting room and kitchen: £6,800, ten-year workmanship guarantee.
- Option 3, new warm roof over the whole building including the changing block: £14,200, excluding asbestos removal on the block, which would have added several thousand more.

## Why It Matters

The board chose option 2. Option 3 would have spent money on a block we wanted to replace, and the asbestos sheets made it dearer than it looked. Option 1 was false economy. The reasoning is in [[pavilion-roof-repair]].
