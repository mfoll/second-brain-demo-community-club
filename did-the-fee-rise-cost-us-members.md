# Did the Fee Rise Cost Us Members?

Junior membership fell from 168 to 161 for 2026-27. How much of that is the £25 fee rise?

## What We Know

- 22 juniors did not renew and 15 joined ([[clubfold-membership-export-2026-10]]).
- Four leavers gave cost as their reason; nine of the 22 were under-15s or under-16s, an age when some players stop anyway.
- 14 bursaries were awarded, so at least 14 families who might have struggled stayed.
- Girls rose from 24 to 31, which hides some of the loss.

## Current Answer

Open, partly answered. [[donovan-clarke]] thinks age drop-off explains most of it. [[ravinder-sahota]] points out that families who leave over money rarely say so. Both may be right.

## What Would Settle It

A short phone round of leavers in November, done by Ravinder so it stays confidential, and the January bursary review.
