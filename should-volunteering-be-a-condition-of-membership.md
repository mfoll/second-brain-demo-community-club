# Should Volunteering Be a Condition of Membership?

## Question

Should a junior family's membership depend on volunteering, either directly or through a fee for not volunteering?

## Current Answer

No. The board tried it from September 2025 with the [[family-volunteer-pledge]] and partly reversed it at the [[board-meeting-2026-02-10]]. Volunteering is now asked for, specifically and personally, and never charged for. The pledge of three sessions a season stays as a voluntary hope on the membership form.

## Why

- It did not fill more slots: the rota fill rate stayed at 81% ([[pledge-review-2026-01-09]]).
- It turned help into a price for some families ([[motivation-crowding-out]]).
- It cost us two families and a lot of Nisha's time.
- It fell hardest on families who work weekends, which [[ravinder-sahota]] warned about in July 2025.

## What Would Reopen It

A season where the rota fill rate falls below 70% despite the [[volunteer-recruitment-drive-2026]], or a change in how the club is structured (for example a paid match-day coordinator funded by a higher fee) that the members vote for at an AGM.
