# Safeguarding Training Tracker

The Clubfold compliance report that tracks each volunteer's safeguarding training and background-check dates. Set up by [[aoife-brennan]] in June 2026 to replace a spreadsheet that had not been updated since 2023.

## Summary

The report lists every volunteer in a "check needed" role with training date, check status and expiry, and flags anyone due within 60 days. It sends reminders to the volunteer and, from November 2026, copies the deputy welfare officer.

## Why It Matters

All counts in [[safeguarding-training-status-october-2026]] and in board reports come from here. It is what makes the three-year rule enforceable ([[how-often-should-volunteers-refresh-safeguarding-training]]).

## Caveats

Only as good as the dates entered. Aoife checks new certificates before marking anyone current.

## Source Location

Clubfold, Volunteers, Compliance, "Training and checks" report. Full access: welfare officer and deputy; status-only view for the chair and volunteer coordinator.
