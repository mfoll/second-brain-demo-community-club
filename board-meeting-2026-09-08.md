# Board Meeting 8 September 2026

Monthly board, Tuesday 8 September 2026. Femi chaired for the first time with me beside him. Nine present.

## Items

- **Co-chair proposal.** [[hamza-qureshi]] tabled his paper proposing co-chairs from the 2027 AGM, himself and [[femi-oyelaran]] ([[hamza-co-chair-proposal-2026-09]]). Femi, chairing, declared an interest and asked me to take the item. Femi said he preferred a single chair with a strong vice-chair and gave his reasons. No vote; the board asked for both models to be written up ([[should-fernhollow-have-co-chairs]]).
- **Incorporation.** [[marek-zielinski]] asked who would be personally on the hook if the club signed a 30-year lease and a £248,000 contract and things went wrong. Nobody knew for certain. Agreed to open a review ([[club-incorporation-review]], [[personal-liability-in-an-unincorporated-club]]).
- **Lanternfield application** to be submitted by 30 September; the council cabinet meets on 17 September on the lease ([[changing-rooms-grant-application]]).
- **Season start:** 161 juniors registered against 168 last season ([[did-the-fee-rise-cost-us-members]]).

## Note

Femi chaired well and kept to time. I spoke twice, which for me is restraint.
