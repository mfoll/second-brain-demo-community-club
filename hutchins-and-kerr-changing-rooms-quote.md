# Hutchins & Kerr Building: Fixed Quote for New Changing Rooms

Fixed-price quote from Hutchins & Kerr Building for building the new changing rooms to Neil Cartwright's tender drawings (revision C, May 2026), received by email from Dale Hutchins on Friday 26 June 2026. Eleven pages.

## Summary

£248,000 in total, including VAT the club cannot recover and provisional sums for fees, fixed until 31 March 2027. After that date the price would be adjusted by a building cost index from the date of the quote. Build programme 22 weeks from start on site.

## Key Details

- Main build (groundworks, block and render, roof, doors and windows, internal fit-out, showers, drainage connections): £214,000.
- Demolition of the 1974 prefabricated block, including licensed removal of the asbestos cement roof sheets as a named subcontract: £9,500.
- New water, electricity and drainage connections: £8,000.
- Provisional sums and contingency: £16,500, covering planning and building control fees, a contract administrator for the build (Neil cannot do this unpaid), and a 5% contingency on the works.
- Exclusions: furniture beyond fixed benches and pegs, any work to the pavilion itself, the club's own legal costs.
- Payment: monthly valuations; 5% retention for 12 months.
- Insurance: contractor's all risks.
- Fixed for acceptance by 31 March 2027. Start date to be agreed at acceptance; Dale prefers a close-season start in early May so the old block can be used until the last home match, finishing by October.

## Why It Matters

This is the figure the grant application is built on. Marek liked that the asbestos removal is a fixed subcontract rather than a provisional sum. The risk is the date: if the grant decision or the lease slips past March 2027, the fixed price ends ([[will-the-hutchins-kerr-quote-hold-until-the-grant-decision]]).
