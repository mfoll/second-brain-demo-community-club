# Communications Audit 2026-02

Hamza Qureshi's audit of how the club communicates, presented to the board on 10 February 2026 and filed on 2 February when he finished it.

## Summary

The club had no single channel. Hamza counted 23 active group chats run by 17 different volunteers, three club email addresses (two of them forwarding to people who had left roles), a dormant social media page, and Clubfold notices that most parents ignored because the same message usually came by chat first.

## Key Details

- Biggest chat: the club-wide group with 230 members, used for everything from lost kit to the fee consultation.
- Typical junior family: in 2 or 3 chats; the survey's maximum was 7 ([[members-survey-2025]]).
- The same fixture change was posted in four places on one Saturday in January, with two different kick-off times.
- Proposals: a monthly newsletter, one broadcast channel per team for match-day information, Clubfold notices for anything official, and a named owner for each channel.

## Why It Matters

The baseline for the [[club-communications-plan]] and the source of the [[one-channel-per-audience]] idea. The superseded 2025 social media plan was dropped after this audit ([[club-social-media-page-plan-2025]]).
