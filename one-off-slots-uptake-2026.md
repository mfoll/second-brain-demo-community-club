# One-Off Slots Uptake 2026

A Clubfold report on one-off volunteer slots from 7 March to 11 October 2026, run by [[nisha-raghavan]] for the October board.

## Key Details

- 212 one-off slots filled by 64 different adults.
- 61 of those slots were filled in March, mostly around the taster morning ([[volunteer-taster-morning-2026-03-07]]).
- 28 adults did one slot only; 21 did two to four; 15 did five or more.
- Of the 15 who did five or more, 3 have since taken a regular role.
- Most filled: tea hut helper (54), pitch setup (41), car park steward (33), tournament helper (31).
- Least filled: line marking (6) and kit sort (4).
- Under-18 helpers are excluded from these figures.

## Why It Matters

Shows one-off slots are widening the pool, which is the main bet of [[role-based-volunteering]]. The conversion rate to regular roles is lower than Nisha hoped, which is why personal asks are the November priority ([[can-the-recruitment-drive-reach-twelve-by-december]]).
