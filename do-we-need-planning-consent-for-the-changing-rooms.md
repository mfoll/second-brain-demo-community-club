# Do we need planning consent for the changing rooms?

## Question

Is a new changing block on the footprint of the old one permitted without a planning application, or do we need full consent?

## Answer

We need full planning consent. Neil checked with the council's planning duty officer in February 2026: a new building of that size, even on an existing footprint, is not covered by any permitted development, and the council as landowner will also want to see it.

## What we decided

- Do not apply before the grant is decided; the fee and drawings cost money we may not need to spend. Neil will have the drawings ready to submit the week the grant is confirmed.
- Expect about eight weeks for a decision, so consent around April or May 2027 if the grant comes in February.
- That timing is one reason the quote's fixed date matters ([[will-the-hutchins-kerr-quote-hold-until-the-grant-decision]]).

Planning fees are in the £16,500 fees and contingency line ([[changing-rooms-cost-breakdown]]).
