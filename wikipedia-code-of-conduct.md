# Code of conduct

## Summary

The English Wikipedia article on codes of conduct. The lead defines a code of conduct simply as a set of rules setting out the norms, responsibilities or proper practices expected of a person or an organisation. The wider article distinguishes codes aimed at members' behaviour from those aimed at an organisation's dealings with outsiders. Last edited 17 October 2026 at the time of the record.

## Why It Matters

[[aoife-brennan]] was rewriting the club's four codes in August 2026 ([[codes-of-conduct-2026]]) and asked whether we needed codes at all when the policy already said it all. Reading the article helped me answer: the policy is for the board and the welfare officers, the codes are the short, plain version people actually read on the touchline. That is why the 2026 codes are a page each and written as "do" and "don't" rather than in policy language.

## Caveats

General; it says nothing about sport or children. Our codes follow the governing bodies' models.
