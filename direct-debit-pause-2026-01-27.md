# Direct Debit Paused 27 January 2026

Finance subgroup ([[claire-whitlow]], [[ravinder-sahota]], me) by video call, Tuesday 27 January 2026.

## Decision

Pause direct debit collection from February. The 38 trial families move to card instalments through Clubfold.

## Why

- The bank's letter of 15 January: community accounts will pay £6 a month plus 40p per direct debit collected from April 2026 ([[bank-community-account-charges-2026]]). For 38 families paying monthly that is about £250 a year.
- Clubfold's card fee is higher per payment but has no fixed charge, and failed card payments are retried automatically. Six direct debits failed in December, and Claire chased each one by hand.

## Disagreement

Ravinder wanted to keep direct debit for families who do not have a card they can rely on. Agreed to pause rather than stop, and to compare banks first ([[is-direct-debit-worth-the-bank-charges]]).
