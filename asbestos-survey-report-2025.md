# Asbestos Survey of the Pavilion and Changing Block

A refurbishment and demolition asbestos survey of the 1974 pavilion and prefabricated changing block, carried out on Tuesday 14 October 2025 by a licensed surveyor Marek found. Cost £680, paid from the facilities budget.

## Summary

- Changing block roof: corrugated asbestos cement sheets, in fair condition, low risk if left undisturbed. Must be removed by a licensed contractor before demolition.
- Changing block wall panels and floor tiles: no asbestos found in samples.
- Pavilion: an asbestos cement flue in the kitchen store, sealed and labelled; nothing in the meeting room or roof void.
- Recommendation: keep the register up to date, label the flue, no work to the changing block roof without a licensed contractor.

## Why It Matters

It settled two things. The 2025 roof repair was right not to touch the changing block ([[pavilion-roof-repair]]), and the old block cannot be kept as a store ([[can-the-old-prefab-be-reused-as-storage]]). It also let Hutchins & Kerr price the removal as a fixed subcontract.

## Caveats

A demolition survey only samples what it can reach; the contractor will re-check once the block is stripped.
