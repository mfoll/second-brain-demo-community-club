# Volunteer Headcount October 2026

A snapshot of who does what, by role group, as of mid-October 2026. Counts only; names and contact details are in Clubfold. "Active" means at least one session in the last eight weeks.

## By Role Group

| Group | Active volunteers |
|---|---|
| Football coaches (head and assistant) | 15 |
| Athletics coaches and officials | 5 |
| Team managers | 6 |
| Club running (rota, fixtures, kit, ground, newsletter, social media, tea hut lead) | 7 |
| Welfare officer and deputy | 2 |
| **Total** | **35** |

Board members are counted in the group where they volunteer, not separately. Of the 35, 22 do something every week in season.

## Changes Since October 2025

- 7 new regular volunteers from the [[volunteer-recruitment-drive-2026]].
- 6 stopped over the 2025-26 season, mostly when their own child moved on.
- Net change +1 (34 to 35). The drive is mostly replacing leavers, which is worth saying plainly to the board.

## Beyond the 35

64 different adults have done at least one one-off slot since March ([[one-off-slots-uptake-2026]]). They are not counted as active volunteers but are where new regulars come from.
