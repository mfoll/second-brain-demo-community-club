# Changing Rooms Timeline

Every date that matters for the new changing rooms, past and planned, in one list. Planned dates are plans, not promises.

## Done

- 22 February 2025: storm damages the pavilion roof ([[pavilion-roof-leak-2025-02-22]]).
- 7 April 2025: board starts [[new-changing-rooms]] after Marek's [[pavilion-condition-survey-2025]].
- 14 October 2025: asbestos survey.
- 12 November 2025: first lease meeting with council estates ([[lease-meeting-2025-11-12]]).
- 5 March 2026: pre-application call with the Lanternfield Trust.
- 12 May 2026: board commits £25,000 of reserves.
- 19 May 2026: design revision C.
- 1 June 2026: appeal launched.
- 26 June 2026: fixed quote of £248,000; 30 June quote chosen.
- 21 July 2026: heads of terms agreed with officers.
- 20 August 2026: Community Chest awards £15,000.
- 17 September 2026: cabinet defers the lease ([[council-cabinet-deferral-2026-09-17]]).
- 30 September 2026: grant application submitted.

## Planned

- 19 November 2026: cabinet, lease item.
- 15 December 2026: trust deadline for tenure evidence.
- January 2027: possible trust site visit.
- February 2027: grant decision.
- 31 March 2027: Hutchins & Kerr fixed price ends unless extended.
- 1 April 2027: new 30-year lease starts, if approved.
- Spring 2027: planning application and consent (about eight weeks).
- Mid-2027: contract signed; reserves and Community Chest money paid in.
- May to October 2028: demolition and build in the close season.
- 2028-29 season: new changing rooms open.

## Why so long

The club cannot be without changing rooms during a season, and nothing can be signed until lease, grant and planning are all in place. See [[what-happens-if-the-lease-is-not-evidenced-by-15-december]].
