# Volunteer Rota 2024-25 Group Chat

> [!warning]
> Archived. The group-chat rota and the pavilion spreadsheet were retired when the Clubfold rota went live on 29 August 2025 ([[clubfold-rota-go-live-2025-08-29]]). Kept only as the baseline for the [[volunteer-rota-overhaul]].

## Summary

How the rota worked before Clubfold: a "Fernhollow Helpers" group chat of about 140 parents and volunteers in a common messaging app, a spreadsheet on my laptop, and a laminated sheet on the pavilion door.

## Key Details

From my count in February 2025 of the previous eight home Saturdays:

- About 60% of slots filled two weeks out, by my rough estimate; most of the rest filled on Friday nights by the same dozen people.
- Requests scrolled away within an hour on busy days.
- Three Saturdays had no tea hut; one under-12s match was nearly called off for want of a linesperson.
- Swaps happened by private message and were rarely reflected on the sheet.

## Why It Matters

The baseline for judging the overhaul: fill rate rose to about 81% two weeks out under Clubfold.

## Source Location

The group chat was closed to new posts on 29 August 2025 and left as read-only for a season, then deleted in August 2026. The spreadsheet is in the shared drive, "Volunteers / Old rota".
