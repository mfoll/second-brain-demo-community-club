# Summer Tournament 2025 Accounts

Closing accounts for the 2025 tournament, from Claire Whitlow, 20 July 2025.

- Income £7,450: entries 44 × £50 = £2,200, tea hut £4,300, raffle £650, stalls £300.
- Costs £1,250: toilets £400, medals £480, first aid £200, referees £170.
- Net £6,200, all to general funds.

Useful as the baseline for 2026: entries rose by £920 with eight more teams and a £10 higher fee, and the tea hut did £1,050 better despite rain. No money was set aside for the changing rooms in 2025 because the appeal did not exist yet.

Source location: club shared drive, Treasurer / Events / 2025 tournament.
