# Women's association football

## Summary

An overview of women's football: the same game under the same Laws of the Game, played professionally in many countries, with about 200 national teams taking part internationally. The body covers history and growth.

## Why It Matters

Leanne used the history section in her pitch to the board in June 2025 when we agreed the [[girls-football-taster-day-2025-06-21]]. What mattered to us was the long story of women's football being held back and then growing fast, which is why girls now arrive expecting a team.
