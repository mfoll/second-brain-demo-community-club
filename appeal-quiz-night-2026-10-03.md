# Appeal Quiz Night, 3 October 2026

Quiz night in the pavilion on Saturday 3 October 2026 for the changing rooms appeal. 18 teams of up to six, £10 a head, raffle and a hot-pot supper.

## Numbers

- Tickets £960, raffle £520, supper £310, less £190 costs: £1,600 net to the appeal.
- About 100 people; the pavilion was full, which raised the fire exit question again (we kept the changing block exit clear, see [[pavilion-fire-risk-assessment-2025]]).

## Notes

- Helen Marsh ran it with four helpers; Hamza was quizmaster.
- Adult football teams came out well: both entered teams and the women's team won.
- Raffle prizes donated by local shops; thank-you letters went out on 6 October.
- Worth repeating in the spring. Next time cap at 16 teams.

Appeal total after the night: £14,350, rising to £14,600 by 20 October with online gifts ([[match-funding-appeal-tracker]]).
