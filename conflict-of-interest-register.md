# Conflict of Interest Register

## Summary

The register of board members' declared interests, required by clause 9 of the [[fernhollow-constitution-2025]] and kept by [[joanna-pryce]] since July 2025. Updated at the start of each board meeting.

## Key Details

Kinds of declaration recorded (summary only):

- A board member whose relative works for a contractor that might quote for club work.
- Board members with children in teams whose coaching or pitch slots are being decided.
- On 8 September 2026, Femi and Hamza declared an interest in the co-chair item and Femi handed the chair to me for it ([[board-meeting-2026-09-08]]).

## Why It Matters

Before the club signs a £248,000 building contract, the register needs to be complete and the people involved in choosing the contractor need to be clear of interests. [[neil-cartwright]]'s pro bono design and his past as a club parent are recorded there.
