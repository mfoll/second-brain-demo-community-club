# Photo and Social Media Guidance 2026

The guidance adopted by the board on 14 July 2026 ([[photo-guidance-adoption-2026-07-14]]), drafted by [[aoife-brennan]] and [[linh-pham]]. Two pages; appendix B of the 2026 policy draft.

## Key Details

1. Club photos of juniors only with recorded consent for that season ([[photo-consent-form-2026]]).
2. No names, ever, with any photo of a junior. Team and age group only.
3. No photos or filming in or near changing rooms or toilets, by anyone.
4. Prefer group and action shots; no close-up of an individual junior without specific consent for that image.
5. Club accounts run by named volunteers only; passwords held by two people.
6. Photos taken on club devices or uploaded to the club folder within a week and deleted from personal phones.
7. Withdrawal of consent: photos removed within a week.
8. Parents' own photos: welcome for their own child; please do not post other children.
9. Coaches do not follow or message junior players on personal social media.

## Why It Matters

Closed gap 1 in [[safeguarding-review-gap-list]]. Linh's per-team no-photo list is how it works in practice.
