# How to Award a Hardship Bursary

How a family gets help with fees, from first mention to the board report. Written with [[ravinder-sahota]] in March 2026 under the [[hardship-bursary-criteria]].

## Guardrails

- Only the membership secretary and one money-handling board member know which families have a bursary.
- Nothing about any family goes into emails to coaches, board papers, minutes, group chats or this vault.
- No evidence of income is asked for.

## Steps

1. **A family mentions money** to a coach, a team manager or anyone at the club. That person says: "There's help with fees; can I pass your email to Ravinder? It stays with him." They pass on only the contact, nothing else.
2. **Ravinder contacts the family** within a week, by phone or in person. A short conversation: which child, what would help. No forms.
3. **Ravinder and the second decider** (currently [[claire-whitlow]]) agree the award by message, the same week if possible.
4. **Ravinder records it in Clubfold** as a fee adjustment under the restricted Bursary code ([[bursary-awards-tracker]]). Half fee plus up to £37.50 for kit or travel.
5. **Ravinder tells the family**, and the coach only that "the fee is sorted".
6. **Board report**, monthly: number of awards, total committed, amount left. Nothing else.

## If the fund runs low

Ravinder tells the chair and treasurer when £300 or less remains. The board decides on any top-up without discussing individual cases.
