# Council Briefing on the Community Asset Review, August 2026

A two-page briefing note issued to councillors and community leaseholders on 26 August 2026 about the council's review of community leases. I only received it on 21 September, after the cabinet deferral, when Graham sent it on.

## Summary

- The council holds about 40 leases of land and buildings to community groups, sports clubs and charities, on very different terms.
- The review will look at rent levels, repair responsibilities, community use clauses and how the council measures community benefit.
- Interim findings to cabinet in November 2026; final report spring 2027.
- Existing leases are not affected until they come up for renewal; "new or extended leases may be considered in the light of the review".

## Why It Matters

This is the reason cabinet deferred our lease ([[council-cabinet-deferral-2026-09-17]]). Nothing in it suggests our heads of terms are out of line: we already have a community use clause and full repair of buildings. The risk is timing, not substance. See [[community-asset-review]].
