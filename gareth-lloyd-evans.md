# Gareth Lloyd-Evans

Volunteer groundsman at Millrace Lane. Has looked after the pitches since about 2017.

## Role

- Mows, marks and repairs the two full-size and three mini pitches through the season, using the club's ride-on mower and line marker.
- Keeps the [[ground-maintenance-calendar]] and the [[pitch-condition-log-2025-26]]; calls pitches off with Martin Goss when they are unplayable.
- Organises the end-of-season renovation (seed, top dressing, rolling) in May and June, with two or three helpers.

## How we work together

Gareth works early, mostly on weekday mornings, and texts rather than emails. He does not come to board meetings and does not want to; Marek speaks for the ground at the board and passes on his reports. When he says a pitch is not fit, we accept it. I try to make sure his work is thanked out loud at the AGM, because it is the most invisible job at the club and he would be very hard to replace ([[volunteer-burnout]]).

## Current threads

- Pitch 2 drainage at the bottom end, which floods after heavy rain. The draft lease leaves the main drains with the council; Gareth's view is that the problem is the field drains, which would be ours ([[should-the-club-take-on-pitch-drainage-under-the-new-lease]]).
- The mower is 11 years old. A replacement is in Claire's list of future capital needs, not this year's budget.
- Wants someone younger to learn the line marking. Ellie Tranter has shown an interest.
