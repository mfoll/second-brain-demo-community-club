# Millrace Lane Ground Overview

A plain description of the ground for anyone new to running it. Written in March 2025 and kept up to date.

## The site

- Two full-size grass pitches. Pitch 1 by the pavilion drains well; pitch 2 at the far end floods at the bottom after heavy rain ([[pitch-condition-log-2025-26]]).
- Three mini pitches on the strip along the lane, used by the under-7s to under-10s.
- The pavilion (1974): meeting room, kitchen with a hatch for the tea hut, a store, and the prefabricated changing block (one shared space, two showers, one WC).
- Car park for about 30 cars; it fills by 09:30 on a busy Saturday.
- A container by the car park for goals, nets and the line marker; the ride-on mower lives in the pavilion store.

## Who uses it when

- Saturday mornings: junior football from 09:00, all pitches in use until about 13:00. Adult men's home matches on Saturday afternoons, women's on Sunday.
- Weekday evenings: training on pitch 1 and the mini pitches until dark (no floodlights).
- Athletics trains at Oakreach School, not here ([[oakreach-track-agreement]]).
