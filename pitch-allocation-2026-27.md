# Pitch Allocation 2026-27

Martin Goss's pitch allocation sheet for 2026-27, published in Clubfold on 28 August 2026 and revised on 3 September for the changing-room rules.

- Training: Monday to Thursday evenings on pitch 1 (floodlit half) and the three mini pitches until mid-October; from November, juniors move indoors or to the Oakreach track infield where agreed.
- Saturday: 11 junior teams, 09:00 to 13:00 (see [[saturday-pitch-timetable-2026-27]]); adult men 14:00 on pitch 1.
- Sunday: adult women and girls' under-14s alternate on pitch 1; under-16s on pitch 2.
- Council pitch hire for 2026-27 is up 12%; the allocation keeps hired hours flat by removing one Friday slot nobody used.

Why it matters: the working document for every coach, and the source for the timetable note.
