# Draymoor Volunteer Centre Good Practice Checklist

A two-page checklist for small groups that involve volunteers, published by the Draymoor Volunteer Centre and given to me by [[celine-dubois]] on 22 January 2026.

## Key Details

The checklist asks whether a group has:

- A written description for each volunteer role, with time commitment.
- A named person new volunteers can go to.
- An induction and a settling-in period.
- A way to say thank you that does not depend on one big event.
- A way for volunteers to step back or change role without awkwardness.
- Out-of-pocket expenses paid promptly.
- Someone checking that no volunteer is doing too much.

We scored ourselves honestly in January: three of seven. By October 2026, six of seven; the thank-you item is still open ([[how-do-we-recognise-volunteers-without-money]]).
