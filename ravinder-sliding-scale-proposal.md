# Sliding Scale Fees Proposal

## Summary

[[ravinder-sahota]]'s two-page proposal for banded junior fees, attached to the fee options paper as option D. Three bands: £120 for families receiving income-related benefits or on a low income, £165 standard, £190 "supporter" rate for those who can pay more. Families choose their band on registration, on trust, with no evidence asked for.

## Key Details

- His argument: 23 families asked for payment plans in 2025-26 ([[payment-plan-requests-2025-26]]); a flat rise hits them hardest; asking for help one family at a time is embarrassing, and choosing a band on a form is not.
- He estimated 25 families at £120, 120 at £165 and 20 at £190, which raises about the same as option B.
- He accepted that the estimate is a guess and that trust-based bands could be abused, but argued that a club should "assume good faith".

## Why It Matters

The board preferred a flat rise with a bursary in March 2026, largely for practical reasons: Clubfold could not handle bands before September, and Claire doubted the take-up guesses. The proposal returns in January 2027 with a season of bursary data ([[should-fees-use-a-sliding-scale]], [[sliding-scale-fees]]).
