# Season Launch Meeting 2026-08-18

Coaches' and team managers' meeting before the 2026-27 season, Tuesday 18 August 2026, pavilion. 24 people, nearly every team represented. Chaired by Donovan Clarke; Hazel, Martin Goss and Aoife Brennan attended.

## Agreed

- Pitch slots for all 13 teams ([[pitch-allocation-2026-27]]). Martin to publish in Clubfold by 28 August.
- Under-7s and under-8s stay in festival format, no league tables. Tom Ashworth made the case; nobody disagreed ([[should-under-7s-play-festivals-not-fixtures]]).
- Every coach to have a current qualification by Christmas; one gap (under-12s).
- Aoife briefed the interim changing-room rules starting 5 September ([[changing-room-supervision-rules]]). The coaches of the under-14s and the adult men's team agreed the 13:00 to 13:45 gap.

## Concerns raised

- Under-16s numbers (Jakub Nowak): 15 players, tight for away games.
- Mini pitch 3 has no light after mid-October; the girls' under-11s need somewhere for winter.
- Several coaches asked for match-day messages to come through one team channel, not three ([[club-communications-plan]]).
