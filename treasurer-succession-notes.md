# Treasurer Succession Notes

Notes from two conversations with [[claire-whitlow]] in April and September 2026 about how to hand on the treasurer's job.

## What the job is now

Bookkeeping in a spreadsheet from bank and Clubfold exports; the [[monthly-finance-check]]; the season budget; year-end accounts for the examiner; grant and match-funding records; bursary sign-off; payments over £500 with a second signatory. Claire estimates five hours a week, more in March.

## Claire's proposal

- Split it. A **bookkeeper** volunteer does monthly entries and reconciliation; the **treasurer** does the budget, reports to the board and signs off.
- Simplify first: one bank account, fewer cash events, Clubfold for everything possible.
- Recruit outside the board: a role description through [[nisha-raghavan]] and the Draymoor Volunteer Centre ([[volunteer-role-descriptions]]).
- A shadow treasurer by the 2027 AGM, taking over by the 2028 AGM at the latest.
- [[farah-ansari]] has offered an hour with the successor.

## What I worry about

If the club incorporates, the reporting gets heavier exactly when a new person takes over ([[club-incorporation-review]]). We should not do both in the same year.
