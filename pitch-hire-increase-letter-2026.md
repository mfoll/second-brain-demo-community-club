# Pitch Hire Increase Letter 2026

## Summary

Letter dated 6 January 2026 from the sport and leisure team at Draymoor Borough Council, signed by [[anita-venkatesh]], received 9 January. It confirms a 12% rise in pitch hire charges for all clubs from 1 September 2026.

## Key Details

- Our charge for Millrace Lane (two full-size pitches, three mini pitches, pavilion use) rises from £10,000 to £11,200 a season.
- Reason given: grounds maintenance contract costs and the council's budget position.
- No change to the lease terms or repairing obligations ([[millrace-lane-lease]]).
- Clubs may pay in two instalments, September and January.

## Why It Matters

This letter started the [[membership-fee-review-2026]]. It is the largest single cost rise in the 2026-27 budget.
