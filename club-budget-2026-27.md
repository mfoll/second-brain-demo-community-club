# Club Budget 2026-27

## Summary

[[claire-whitlow]]'s season budget for September 2026 to August 2027, version 3, circulated on 24 February 2026 with the fee options paper and adopted with option B at the board on 10 March 2026 ([[board-meeting-2026-03-10]]). Turnover about £48,000. It replaced the [[club-budget-2025-26]].

## Why It Matters

This is the spreadsheet behind the fee rise. It shows the 12% pitch hire increase, the bursary line, and how the club plans to rebuild reserves after committing £25,000 to the changing-rooms [[match-funding]]. When anyone asks "why did fees go up", this is the answer, with [[fee-review-options-paper-2026]] for the alternatives.

Other points in the notes tab:

- Reserves: about £31,000 at 31 December 2025, falling to about £6,000 when the £25,000 match-funding commitment is paid at contract signature. The [[club-reserves-policy]] target is three months' running costs, about £10,500.
- Oakreach School track: no charge while the coaching swap holds. A £45 per hour lettings fee for two hours a week in term time would add about £3,150 (about 70 hours a year; [[oakreach-track-agreement]]).
- Fee rise sensitivity: every 10 juniors who do not renew cost £1,650.

## Caveats

- Built on 165 juniors; we have 161, so junior fees will come in about £660 short, before bursaries.
- The budget runs by season; the accounts run by calendar year ([[annual-accounts-2025]]). Comparing them needs care.
- Tournament income is an estimate; 2026 actually netted £8,900, of which £5,000 went to the appeal ([[summer-tournament-2026-debrief]]).
