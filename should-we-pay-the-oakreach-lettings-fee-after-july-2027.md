# Should we pay the Oakreach lettings fee after July 2027?

## Question

If the review with Oakreach School ends with the school still wanting £45 an hour for the Tuesday track slot, should the club pay, negotiate a reduced rate, or move athletics training elsewhere?

## Why It Matters

About £3,150 a year that the budget does not have ([[club-budget-2026-27]]), against a junior athletics squad that depends on a proper track in winter.

## Options

1. Pay the full rate and keep the Wednesday club. Costs about £3,150 a year; roughly £20 a year on every member's fee.
2. Negotiate a community rate (say £20 an hour, about £1,400) plus the Wednesday club.
3. Keep the swap in kind with both sides valued in writing. My preference ([[keep-the-oakreach-swap-in-kind]]).
4. Train on grass at Millrace Lane from October to March. Sheila thinks we would lose athletes.

## Current Answer

None yet. Make the case for option 3 in the January paper; get the board's agreement to a fallback of option 2 before the spring meeting so we are not negotiating against ourselves.
