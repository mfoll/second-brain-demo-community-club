# Photo And Social Media Guidance

Consent-based rules on photographing juniors and posting club content, written as part of the safeguarding review.

## Outcome

Consent-based guidance on photographing juniors and posting club content, adopted by the board on 14 July 2026. Reached.

## Current State

- Completed. Guidance adopted at the July board ([[photo-guidance-adoption-2026-07-14]]) and in force from the 2026-27 season.
- Photo consent is now a yes/no field on every junior registration in Clubfold ([[photo-consent-form-2026]]). For 2026-27, 138 of 161 juniors have consent recorded as yes, 17 as no and 6 not yet answered (treated as no).
- [[linh-pham]] keeps the "no photo" list for each team on her phone and briefs team managers before tournaments.

## Scope and Boundaries

Club photos and club social media accounts, plus guidance to parents about their own photos at matches. Not about individual coaches' private accounts beyond the code of conduct, and not about press photography, which goes through [[hamza-qureshi]] case by case.

## Working Context

The old policy had one paragraph. In practice Linh asked team managers, who asked parents, and nobody wrote anything down. The new guidance ([[photo-and-social-media-guidance-2026]]) sets out:

- Consent at registration, renewable each season, withdrawable any time by message to the welfare officer.
- No names with photos of juniors, ever; team and age group only.
- No photos in or near changing rooms or toilets, by anyone.
- Group and action shots preferred; no close-ups of an individual junior without specific consent.
- Parents may photograph their own child at matches; we ask them not to post other children ([[can-parents-photograph-their-own-children-at-matches]]).

We debated whether to stop posting junior photos altogether. [[aoife-brennan]] was tempted; Linh and [[hamza-qureshi]] argued that match photos are how families and sponsors see the club. The board chose consent plus clear rules.
