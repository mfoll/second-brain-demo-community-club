# Work With The Community

How the club gets on with the people around it: Draymoor Borough Council, Oakreach School, our neighbours on the streets round Millrace Lane, the Residents' Association, the Gazette and the local bodies we lean on.

## Scope

- The council as landlord, pitch provider and funder: the sport and leisure team, the estates team, the ward councillor and cabinet.
- Oakreach School and the track-for-coaching swap.
- Neighbours, parking and the Fernhollow Residents' Association.
- Club communications as a whole sit in [[club-communications-plan]], which Hamza and Donovan's side of the board look after; I keep the relationships here.
- Not here: the lease detail (that is [[pitch-lease-extension]] under [[look-after-the-ground]]) and anything about individual families.

## Current State

- The council relationship is good at officer level and slow at cabinet level. Anita Venkatesh and Graham Sutcliffe both back the lease; the [[community-asset-review]] is what is holding it up.
- Oakreach agreed on 6 October 2026 to keep the free track arrangement until July 2027 while both sides review it ([[oakreach-lettings-meeting-2026-10-06]]). Priya Lal's £45 an hour proposal is still on the table for after that.
- After the July tournament, Imran Sheikh's Residents' Association complained to the council about parking. The council wants a stewarded parking plan for 2027 ([[how-do-we-handle-tournament-parking-in-2027]]).
- Councillor Rhys Penhallow has been a steady ally; he spoke for us at the September cabinet.
