# Board Minutes Archive

## Summary

The approved board and AGM minutes, one folder per year, kept by [[joanna-pryce]] as secretary. Complete from 2011; earlier years are paper in the pavilion office. Each set is a PDF signed by the chair at the following meeting.

## Why It Matters

The minutes are the club's record of decisions. The Event notes in this vault, such as [[board-meeting-2026-03-10]], summarise them for my own use and for handover, but if they ever disagree, the approved minutes win.

## Key Details

- Draft minutes go to the board within a week, approved at the next meeting, then filed.
- Minuted dissent is quoted exactly as the member agreed it ([[minuted-dissent]]).
- Confidential items (bursary decisions, any welfare matter) are minuted only as "confidential item noted", with nothing that identifies anyone. Welfare records are held separately by the welfare officer.
- Edit access: Joanna and me. Board members: view.

## Source Location

Club shared drive, `Governance/Board minutes/<year>/`. Do not copy minutes into the vault; link to them.
