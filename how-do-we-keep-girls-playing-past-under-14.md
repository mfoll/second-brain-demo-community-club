# How do we keep girls playing past under-14?

## Question

When the girls' under-14s age out in 2027 and 2028, where do they play? We have no girls' under-16 team and the adult women's team takes players from 16.

## Why It Matters

Leanne's experience, and the reading in [[wikipedia-youth-sports]], is that the drop-out risk peaks at 13 to 15. If there is no next team, the section loses girls just as they become the volunteers and coaches it needs.

## Current thinking

- A girls' under-16 team needs about 14 players. On current numbers we would have perhaps 8 in 2027-28.
- Options: a joint under-16 team with another club (Northmere FA allows it, per [[vikram-joshi]]); letting 15-year-olds train with the adult women; a young-leaders role so older girls help coach the under-11s, as [[ellie-tranter]] did at that age.
- The adult women's team is willing to have 16-year-olds train with them, subject to the safeguarding policy.

Still open. Next step: Leanne to ask the girls' under-14s what they want, in the spring.
