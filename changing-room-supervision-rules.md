# Changing Room Supervision

Written supervision rules for the shared 1974 changing rooms, and the safeguarding requirements we passed to the new building.

## Outcome

Written supervision rules for the shared 1974 changing rooms in force for the 2026-27 season, and design requirements passed to the new changing rooms.

## Current State

- Interim rules in force since Saturday 5 September 2026 ([[changing-room-interim-rules-start-2026-09-05]], text in [[changing-room-supervision-rules-2026]]).
- Design requirements passed to [[marek-zielinski]] and [[neil-cartwright]] in May 2026 and reflected in the revised design ([[changing-rooms-design-and-quotes]]).
- Seven Saturdays run under the rules so far. Two needed a slot moved because adult kick-off times changed late.
- The rules go into the revised policy as an appendix for adoption on 17 November.

## Scope and Boundaries

How the existing pavilion changing rooms are used and supervised. The new building's design and funding belong to [[new-changing-rooms]]; this project only supplied the safeguarding brief.

## Working Context

The 1974 pavilion has one prefabricated changing block: one shared space, two showers, no separate room for officials and no accessible room. On a typical Saturday the under-14s and under-16s change between 09:00 and 10:00 while the adult men arrive for an 11:00 kick-off and referees need somewhere too. [[aoife-brennan]], [[beatriz-ferreira]] and I watched three Saturdays in May ([[changing-room-observation-2026-05-16]] and [[changing-room-usage-log-saturdays-2026]]): on all three, adults and juniors were in the room at the same time for between 10 and 25 minutes. Nobody had done anything wrong; the building and the fixture times made it happen.

The interim rules:

- Staggered changing slots set by the fixtures secretary, published in Clubfold with the rota.
- No adult changes while juniors are present; adults wait or change at home.
- Two adults outside the door while juniors change, never one adult inside.
- No phones in the changing room, for anyone.
- Officials change in the meeting room, which is locked for the purpose.

[[rebecca-thorne]] at Northmere FA reviewed the rules in June ([[email-from-rebecca-thorne-on-changing-rooms-2026-06]]) and agreed they were proportionate for a building we cannot change quickly.
