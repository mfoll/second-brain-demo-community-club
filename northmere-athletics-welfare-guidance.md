# Northmere Athletics Welfare Guidance

Northmere Athletics' welfare guidance for licensed clubs, 2026 edition, sent by [[orla-keane]] in May 2026 when [[sheila-mortimer]] asked how athletics fits our football-shaped policy.

## Key Details

- Clubs with junior athletes need a welfare officer; one shared with another section of a multi-sport club is acceptable.
- Coaches renew safeguarding training every three years, in line with football.
- Specific guidance on away meets: overnight stays, travel, and who supervises athletes between events.
- Photography at competitions follows the event organiser's rules as well as the club's.
- Training at a third-party venue (for us, the track at Oakreach School) should follow the venue's own safeguarding arrangements as well as the club's.

## Why It Matters

Closed gap 9 in [[safeguarding-review-gap-list]]: the 2022 policy did not mention athletics away meets or the school track. The draft policy now has a short athletics section.
