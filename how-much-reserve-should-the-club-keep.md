# How Much Reserve Should the Club Keep?

After committing £25,000 to the changing rooms, how fast must we rebuild reserves, and is three months' running costs still the right target once we have a new building to maintain?

## What We Know

- Reserves about £31,000 at the end of 2025; about £6,000 once the £25,000 is paid ([[annual-accounts-2025-statement]], [[board-meeting-2026-05-12]]).
- The policy target is three months' costs, about £10,500 ([[club-reserves-policy]]).
- The 2026-27 budget plans a £5,800 surplus ([[club-budget-2026-27]]).
- A new building brings new costs: insurance, cleaning, a sinking fund for repairs. Nobody has priced them yet.

## Current Answer

Open. [[claire-whitlow]] wants to be back at target by the end of 2027-28 and then add a building sinking fund of about £2,000 a year. I agree with the target but worry about raising fees again to get there.
