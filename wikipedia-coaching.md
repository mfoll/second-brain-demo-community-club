# Coaching

## Summary

A general article on coaching as a form of development in which an experienced person supports a learner towards a specific goal through training and guidance, distinguished from mentoring by its focus on specific tasks.

## Why It Matters

I used the coaching-versus-mentoring distinction when writing the [[qualified-coach-per-team]] standard at the start of the [[season-2026-27-plan]]: a team needs a coach for the sessions and can also have a mentor for the new coach. Leanne mentoring Ellie is the second kind.

## Caveats

Mostly about business and life coaching; little on sports coaching. Read on the day the standard was drafted; the page has been edited since. Article last edited 2026-10-23 when checked; licence CC BY-SA 4.0.
