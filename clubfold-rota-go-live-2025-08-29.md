# Clubfold Rota Go-Live 29 August 2025

The Clubfold rota went live for every team on Friday 29 August 2025, ahead of the first weekend of the 2025-26 season. The group-chat rota was closed the same evening with a pinned message pointing to the app ([[volunteer-rota-2024-25-group-chat]]).

## State at Go-Live

- Templates for all 13 football teams, the athletics squad and the fixed match-day roles.
- 143 slots published for September; 112 filled by go-live.
- 96 adults had logged in to the rota at least once.
- [[nisha-raghavan]] and I as the only template editors; team managers can swap slots within their team.

## First Weeks

The first two Saturdays went smoothly. The main problem was swaps: people swapping by private message and not in the app, so the printed sheet was wrong. Nisha sent a short how-to and the problem faded by October.

This closed the [[volunteer-rota-overhaul]]. The setup decisions are written up in [[clubfold-rota-setup-notes]].
