# Volunteer Taster Morning 7 March 2026

The first event of the [[volunteer-recruitment-drive-2026]]: a Saturday morning at Millrace Lane where parents could try a one-off slot alongside an experienced volunteer, with tea and a short talk from [[nisha-raghavan]] about the new [[volunteer-role-descriptions]].

## What Happened

- 23 adults signed up for one-off slots on the morning; 19 turned up.
- Most popular slots: pitch setup (6), tea hut (5), car park steward (4).
- Nisha's ten-minute talk at 11:30 had about 30 people, more than we expected.
- Two people asked about becoming team managers; one is now assistant team manager with the under-13s.
- Linh took photos, following the new consent rules in draft, with no juniors in shot.

## What We Took From It

People were more willing to commit after trying something for two hours with someone friendly beside them. Nisha decided to keep one-off slots open every home Saturday rather than only for special events. By the end of March, 61 one-off slots had been filled ([[one-off-slots-uptake-2026]]).
