# Changing Rooms Design Brief and Revision A (2025)

Neil Cartwright's first brief and sketch plan for the new changing rooms, October 2025. Archived: replaced by [[changing-rooms-design-v2-2026-05]] in May 2026.

## Summary

About 118 square metres: one large junior changing room with four showers, one adult changing room, an accessible WC (not a changing room), a store. Estimated build cost about £227,000.

## Why It Matters

Kept because it shows what we nearly built and why it changed. The brief was written before the safeguarding review and assumed juniors and adults would not change at the same time, which is not how Saturdays work. Aoife's walk-round in April 2026 made that obvious, and the revision cost about £21,000 more ([[changing-rooms-design-revision-2026-05-19]]).
