# Tournament Volunteer Plan 2026

Slots for the 2026 tournament, built by Sam and Nisha and posted on the Clubfold rota on 1 May 2026.

| Role | Saturday | Sunday | Filled |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gate and car park | 6 | 6 | 9 of 12 |
| Tea hut and barbecue | 10 | 10 | 20 of 20 |
| Pitch marshals | 6 | 6 | 12 of 12 |
| Results desk | 2 | 2 | 4 of 4 |
| Lost-child point | 2 | 2 | 4 of 4 |
| Raffle and stalls | 3 | 3 | 6 of 6 |
| Litter and set-down | 3 | 3 | 3 of 6 |
| **Total** | **32** | **32** | **58 of 64** |

Each slot is two to three hours with a one-line description, following Nisha's [[volunteer-role-descriptions]]. 19 people did a one-off slot who had never volunteered before.

The six gaps were all Sunday afternoon car park and litter slots. For 2027: fill car park slots first, offer a barbecue voucher with them, and recruit stewards separately for the [[stewarded-parking-plan]].
