# Joanna Pryce

Club secretary since 2020. Takes the minutes, keeps the correspondence and knows the constitution better than anyone.

## Role

- Minutes of every board meeting and the AGM, filed in the [[board-minutes-archive]]; circulates draft minutes within a week.
- Board papers: collects them and sends them a week before each meeting ([[how-to-run-a-board-meeting]]).
- Club correspondence, including letters from the council and the trust.
- Led the drafting of the 2025 constitution ([[constitution-review-2025]]).

## How we work together

Joanna writes minutes the way they should be written: decisions, reasons, who will do what, and dissent in the member's own terms. When Ravinder asked for his dissent to be minuted in March 2026 she read the wording back to him before the meeting moved on ([[board-meeting-2026-03-10]]). She will tell me plainly when I am about to do something the constitution does not allow.
