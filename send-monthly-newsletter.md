# Send Monthly Newsletter

How to put together and send the monthly club newsletter through Clubfold. Target send date: first Wednesday of the month.

## Steps

1. **Day minus 10.** Check the [[newsletter-content-calendar]]. Ask section leads, the treasurer and the volunteer coordinator for items by day minus 5. Ask Linh for two or three photos.
2. **Day minus 5.** Draft in the Clubfold template: lead item, dates, volunteer role of the month, one section story, board news. Keep it under 600 words.
3. **Checks.** No junior named. Photos only of juniors whose consent flag is set in Clubfold ([[photo-and-social-media-guidance]]). If fees are mentioned, mention the bursary and how to ask in confidence. Anything about the board's decisions matches the draft minutes.
4. **Day minus 2.** Send a test to the chair and one other board member. Fix within a day.
5. **Send** to all member households and adult members. Post a one-line pointer in each team broadcast channel.
6. **Day plus 7.** Note the open rate in the [[club-newsletter-archive]] and anything people replied about.

## Do not

- Copy member contact details out of Clubfold to use another mailing tool.
- Send board business before it has been agreed.
