# Payment Plan Requests 2025-26

## Summary

[[ravinder-sahota]]'s count of families who asked to pay the 2025-26 fees in instalments outside the standard options, taken from Clubfold notes on 20 February 2026. Counts only; the family records stay in Clubfold.

## Key Details

- 23 families asked for a payment plan in 2025-26, against 9 in 2024-25.
- 14 of them had more than one child registered.
- 19 completed payment by February; 4 were still paying.
- Requests clustered in September and January.

## Why It Matters

This was the core of Ravinder's case against a flat fee rise ([[board-meeting-2026-03-10]]) and a reason for keeping [[direct-debit-collection]] alive. It also shaped the bursary criteria ([[hardship-bursary-criteria]]).

## Caveats

A payment plan request is not the same as hardship; some families just prefer to spread the cost. And the rise from 9 to 23 partly reflects that we started telling people they could ask.
