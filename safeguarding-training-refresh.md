# Safeguarding Training Refresh

Getting every volunteer who works with juniors onto current safeguarding training and a current background check, and keeping it that way.

## Outcome

All 35 volunteers in contact with juniors hold current safeguarding training and background checks.

## Current State

- 29 of 35 current on both training and checks as of 15 October 2026.
- Of the six outstanding: three have booked the online course, two have checks in progress, one has not replied to three reminders.
- 18 volunteers attended the in-person workshop run with [[rebecca-thorne]] on 19 September ([[safeguarding-workshop-2026-09-19]]).
- Individual records live only in Clubfold ([[clubfold-volunteer-records]]); this note and [[safeguarding-training-status-october-2026]] hold counts.

## Scope and Boundaries

Training and checks for volunteers aged 18 and over who are in regular contact with juniors. Under-18 helpers do the club's own induction and never count towards supervision ratios ([[should-under-18-helpers-count-in-supervision-ratios]]). Coaching badges are not part of this.

## Working Context

When [[aoife-brennan]] started the review in April, only 22 of 35 were fully current. Most lapses were people whose three-year training had quietly expired; nobody was tracking expiry dates. Aoife moved the tracking into Clubfold, which now flags anyone due in the next 60 days.

The rule from the draft policy, agreed in principle by the board in September: anyone not current by 31 December 2026 steps back from junior sessions until they are. [[what-do-we-do-about-volunteers-not-yet-trained]] covers how we handle that kindly.
