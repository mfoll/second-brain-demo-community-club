# Which channel should parents check first?

## Question

When a parent wants to know whether training is on, where should they look first?

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> The team broadcast channel, for anything about this week's training or match. Clubfold notices for anything club-wide or official (fees, policies, AGM). The newsletter for everything else.

Agreed by the board on 12 May 2026 and written into the channel guide for team managers. The rule that made it work: club-wide news is never posted in team channels except as a one-line pointer.

## Why it was a question

Before the [[club-communications-plan]] the same message could arrive by four routes with different details. Coaches wanted the team channel; Hamza wanted Clubfold to be the single source. We split by scope instead of picking one.
