# Welfare Officer Role Brief

The role descriptions for welfare officer and deputy welfare officer, written by [[aoife-brennan]] when [[beatriz-ferreira]] became deputy in September 2025, and later added to [[volunteer-role-descriptions]].

## Key Details

**Welfare officer**

- First point of contact for any welfare concern; decides on referral.
- Keeps confidential records outside Clubfold and outside any shared drive.
- Maintains the policy and leads its review.
- Checks training and background-check status; reports counts to the board.
- About two to four hours a week, more when a concern arises.

**Deputy welfare officer**

- Covers the welfare officer when away and whenever the welfare officer is involved in a concern.
- Same training as the welfare officer.
- Helps with training, the changing-room rota and girls' and athletics questions.
- About one to two hours a week.

Both: current training and check; not the chair or treasurer; a phone that is answered.

## Why It Matters

Written down for the first time in 2025. The "not the chair" line is deliberate, from my years doing the job: the person who receives concerns should not also be the person who has to keep the club running.
