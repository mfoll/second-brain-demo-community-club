# Safeguarding Workshop 19 September 2026

An in-person safeguarding workshop at the pavilion, Saturday 19 September 2026, 13:00 to 16:00, run by [[rebecca-thorne]] of Northmere Football Association with [[aoife-brennan]].

## What Happened

- 18 volunteers attended, including six whose training had lapsed.
- The session met the Northmere FA requirement for face-to-face training ([[northmere-fa-safeguarding-training-requirements]]), so attendees are current for three years from this date.
- Content: recognising signs of concern, the reporting route ([[report-a-welfare-concern]]), codes of conduct, online contact with juniors, and what to do if a child tells you something.
- Rebecca used made-up scenarios only. Aoife reminded everyone at the start that nothing real should be discussed in the room.

## Results

- Training current went from 23 to 29 of 35 once records were updated in Clubfold ([[safeguarding-training-status-october-2026]]).
- Feedback forms: 16 returned, all rating it useful or very useful. Several asked for the Saturday afternoon timing again.

## Follow-Up

- A second session in January 2027 for new recruits.
- Rebecca's slides are in the safeguarding folder, not circulated by email.
