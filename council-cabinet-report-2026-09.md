# Cabinet Report: Millrace Lane Playing Fields, New Lease

The officer report to cabinet for the meeting of 17 September 2026, published with the agenda on 9 September. Written by Graham Sutcliffe for the head of estates.

## Summary

- Recommends a 30-year lease to the club's trustees on the terms in the heads of terms, from 1 April 2027.
- Notes the club's tenure since 1981, its membership and the Lanternfield application.
- Financial section: the council loses about £10,600 a year of pitch hire and current rent but gains £1,500 rent and saves an estimated £7,000 a year on pitch maintenance and pavilion repairs it would otherwise carry.
- Risks: the club's unincorporated status (mitigated by an assignment clause) and the long term.
- Consultation: ward councillor supportive; no objections received.

## Why It Matters

Officers recommended approval in plain terms, which is why the deferral stung. The report will come back unchanged on 19 November unless the review's interim findings change it. Useful to have when answering the trust ([[pitch-lease-extension]]).
