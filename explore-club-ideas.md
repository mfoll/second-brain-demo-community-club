# Explore Club Ideas

The place for reading and half-formed ideas that have no Project yet: things I or another volunteer have picked up and think the club might do one day. Nothing here is a commitment. When an idea gets an owner, a date and a budget, it moves into a Project under the area that will run it.

## Scope

- Ideas for new activities or ways of running the club, collected as reading, notes and the odd trial.
- Each idea is a cluster Topic with its own Sources and notes. Three are live: [[walking-sports]], [[pavilion-energy]] and [[community-running]].
- Not here: anything with a decision date or money attached. That goes to the board and a proper Project.
