# Clubfold Rota Module

Pointer to the live volunteer rota in Clubfold, the club's membership, payments and rota platform since 2025.

## Summary

The rota module holds rota templates for each team and club role, the published fortnightly rota, slot bookings, swaps and the weekly fill-rate report. It is the single source of truth for who is doing what on a given day. Configuration choices are in [[clubfold-rota-setup-notes]].

## Why It Matters

Everything in [[lead-the-volunteers]] runs through it. The "check needed" flag on slots links the rota to safeguarding status ([[safer-recruitment]]), which is the main reason we chose Clubfold over a spreadsheet.

## Caveats

- Holds volunteers' names and contact details. Do not export lists into email or this vault.
- Template edits limited to the rota admins ([[nisha-raghavan]] and me); see [[who-takes-over-the-clubfold-rota-admin]].

## Source Location

Clubfold, Volunteers, Rota. Admin access: volunteer coordinator and chair. Team managers have swap rights for their team. Last checked 18 October 2026.
