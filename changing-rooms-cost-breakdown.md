# Changing Rooms Cost Breakdown

One page that answers "what does the £248,000 pay for, and who pays it". Figures from the Hutchins & Kerr quote of 26 June 2026 ([[hutchins-and-kerr-changing-rooms-quote]]).

## Costs

| Item | £ |
|---|---|
| Main build: two junior rooms, officials' room, accessible room, lobby, store | 214,000 |
| Demolition of the 1974 block, licensed asbestos removal | 9,500 |
| Water, electricity and drainage connections | 8,000 |
| Provisional sums: planning and building control fees, contract administrator, 5% contingency | 16,500 |
| **Total** | **248,000** |

## Funding

| Source | £ | Status (20 October 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Lanternfield Trust Community Facilities Fund | 185,000 | Applied 30 September; decision February 2027 |
| Club reserves | 25,000 | Committed by the board, 12 May 2026 |
| Draymoor Community Chest | 15,000 | Awarded 20 August 2026 |
| Appeal | 23,000 | £14,600 banked |
| **Total** | **248,000** | |

## Not in the £248,000

- The club's solicitor for the lease (£950 plus VAT, [[club-solicitor-lease-quote-2026-10]]).
- Furniture beyond fixed benches and pegs.
- Running costs once open: about £2,100 a year, plus a £1,000 a year sinking fund from 2028 ([[new-building-maintenance-plan-outline]]).

## Caveats

The 5% contingency is thin for a building with an asbestos roof coming off. If the price is indexed after March 2027, the gap falls on the club ([[should-the-appeal-target-include-a-contingency]]).
