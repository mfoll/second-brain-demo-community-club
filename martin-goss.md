# Martin Goss

Fixtures secretary and a qualified referee who still takes adult games on Sundays.

## Role

- Builds the pitch allocation and Saturday timetable, enters teams with Northmere FA and publishes fixtures in Clubfold.
- Keeps the list of club referees for junior home games ([[referee-availability-2026-27]]).
- Referee coordinator for the summer tournament.

## How we work together

Martin likes a decision before he builds anything, and he hates a timetable being changed after it is published. When Aoife's changing-room rules came in on 5 September he rebuilt the Saturday slots in two evenings, without complaint but with a clear request: no further changes before January.
