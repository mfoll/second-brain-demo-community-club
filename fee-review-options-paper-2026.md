# Fee Review Options Paper 2026

## Summary

Four-page board paper by [[claire-whitlow]] and [[ravinder-sahota]], circulated on 24 February 2026 for the board on 10 March. Sets out four fee options for 2026-27 against the [[club-budget-2026-27]].

## Key Details

| Option | Juniors | Adults | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A, inflation only | £150 | £200 | Covers costs; no reserve rebuild |
| B | £165 | £210 | Covers costs; about £5,800 a season to reserves |
| C | £180 | £220 | Large surplus; judged unfair and unnecessary |
| D, sliding scale | £120 / £165 / £190 | £210 | Depends on take-up of each band; needs income checks |

The paper is honest about what it does not know: how many families would leave at each level, and how many would claim the lowest band under option D. It notes that fees last rose in 2023 ([[fee-history]]).

## Why It Matters

The board chose option B 6-1 ([[board-meeting-2026-03-10]]). Option D is written up in full in [[ravinder-sliding-scale-proposal]] and is due back in January 2027 ([[should-fees-use-a-sliding-scale]]).
