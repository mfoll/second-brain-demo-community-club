# Officer Term Limits

A cap on how long one person may hold a club office in a row. Ours, from the 2025 constitution, is six consecutive years in the same office, followed by at least a year out ([[fernhollow-constitution-2025]]).

## Why we adopted it

Under the 1998 constitution there was no limit, and the club had come to depend on a few people. Colin chaired for eleven years; I was heading for more. The [[constitution-review-consultation-2025]] showed most members wanted a limit, and six years was the most popular length.

## How it works here

- Applies to the four officers (chair, vice-chair, secretary, treasurer), not ordinary board members.
- Consecutive years only; someone can come back after a year off.
- A transitional clause let those in post in June 2025 serve until the 2027 AGM. That is why I step down on 12 June 2027 ([[chair-succession-plan]]).

## What it does not solve

A limit forces a change but does not produce a successor. The treasurer's job shows this most clearly ([[who-will-take-over-as-treasurer]]). It has to go with deliberate shadowing ([[succession-by-shadowing]]). General background in [[wikipedia-term-limit]]. My own view: [[no-one-should-chair-for-more-than-six-years]].
