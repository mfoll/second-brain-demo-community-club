# Solar Panel Quote 2026-03

A quote from a local installer recommended by a club parent, dated 19 March 2026, for a rooftop solar system on the pavilion's south-facing slope.

- System: 6 kW, 14 panels, no battery. Installed price £7,900.
- Estimated generation about 5,200 kWh a year; installer's estimate of saving £1,000 a year if we use most of it on site.
- Condition: the installer would not fit panels on the 2025 roof patch without a structural survey (£450) and recommended re-roofing that section first.

Caveats: one quote only, and the savings assume daytime use. Our heaviest use is evenings and weekends in winter, when panels produce least, so I think the real saving is lower. Not compared with any other installer.
