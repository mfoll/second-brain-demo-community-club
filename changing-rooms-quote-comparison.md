# Changing Rooms Quote Comparison

Marek's spreadsheet comparing the three quotes for the new changing rooms, prepared for the board sub-group meeting on 30 June 2026 ([[changing-rooms-tender-review-2026-06-30]]).

## Summary

| | Hutchins & Kerr | Second local builder | Modular supplier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | £248,000 fixed | £271,500 | £229,000 |
| Fixed until | 31 March 2027 | 3 months | 6 months |
| Asbestos removal | named subcontract | provisional sum £12,000 | excluded |
| Officials' room | yes | yes | no |
| Design life | 60 years | 60 years | 20-25 years |
| Programme | 22 weeks | 26 weeks | 12 weeks on site |

Marek scored each against the trust's assessment headings and his own checklist: price, certainty, design life, fit with the drawings, local references.

## Why It Matters

It is the evidence for the trust's three-quote rule ([[lanternfield-community-facilities-fund]]) and for the choice we made. The modular option was £19,000 cheaper but dropped the officials' room, excluded asbestos removal and has a design life short of the 25-year tenure the trust wants. See [[value-engineering]] for how we thought about cutting cost.

The second builder's names and quote stay in the governed copy only.
