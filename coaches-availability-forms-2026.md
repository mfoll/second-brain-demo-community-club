# Coaches Availability Forms 2026

Summary of the coaches' and team managers' availability forms for 2026-27, returned through Clubfold by 12 June 2026. Collected by Donovan Clarke.

- 26 forms returned from 29 coaches and managers.
- 22 carrying on in the same role; 2 moving up an age group with their team; 2 stopping (one under-12s coach moving away, one under-10s manager).
- Gaps identified: under-12s coach, under-10s team manager, girls' under-14s head coach.
- 5 said they could do more if asked for something specific; 3 named refereeing.

Why it matters: these gaps became the opening tasks of the [[season-2026-27-plan]], which started three days later. All three were filled by August, the under-12s with a coach who still needs his qualification.
