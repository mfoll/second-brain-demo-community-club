# Volunteer Recognition Ideas

A running list for [[how-do-we-recognise-volunteers-without-money]]. Ticked items have been tried.

## Tried

- [x] Annual thank-you evening (November). Reaches regulars, not occasional helpers.
- [x] Long-service mentions for ten years or more.
- [x] Personal thank-you message from Nisha after each person's first one-off slot. Several replied warmly.

## To Try in 2026-27

- [ ] Handwritten card from the team's coach at the end of the season to that team's helpers.
- [ ] Newsletter "thanks this month" line, only for those who say yes ([[club-communications-plan]]).
- [ ] Long-service mentions at the AGM instead of the evening, so members hear them.
- [ ] First refusal on summer tournament places for volunteers' families.
- [ ] Cater the thank-you evening so [[helen-marsh]] is a guest, not the cook. Nominate Helen for the Draymoor Volunteer Centre's annual volunteer awards.
- [ ] A Saturday morning version of the thank-you, with bacon rolls, for people who cannot do Friday nights.

## Rejected

- Discounts on fees for volunteers. Reintroduces money into volunteering by the back door ([[motivation-crowding-out]]).
- Volunteer of the month as a public vote. Popularity contest; see [[idea-volunteer-of-the-month]] in the inbox for the version still being considered.
