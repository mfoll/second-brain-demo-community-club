# End of Season Kit Return

How we get team kit back in May, written up with [[yusuf-demir]] after the 2026 return left us short of shirts for two teams.

## What happened in 2026

Yusuf issued kit to all thirteen football teams in September 2025 and asked for it back by the end of May. By the middle of June he had full sets from nine teams. The rest came back in ones and twos over the summer, and the under-12s and adult women were each four shirts short when the [[kit-order-2026-27|2026-27 kit order]] went in.

## What we will do in May 2027

- Team managers collect the full set at the last match, not the last training session, and hand it to Yusuf in the bag it came in.
- Yusuf ticks it off on the kit sheet the same week and messages any manager who is short.
- Missing shirts after 15 June are ordered from the kit budget, not charged to families.
- One kit swap morning in the pavilion in early July for boots and training tops, run the same way as the [[kit-swap-morning-2025]].

## Why it matters

Shirts are about £18 each with the sponsor's print. Eight missing shirts is most of a bursary.
