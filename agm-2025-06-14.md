# AGM 14 June 2025

Annual general meeting, Saturday 14 June 2025, 10:30, Millrace Lane pavilion. 46 members present (quorum 20).

## What happened

- **New constitution adopted:** 41 for, 3 against, 2 abstentions, comfortably over the two-thirds needed ([[fernhollow-constitution-2025]]). The three against objected to the six-year limit as "pushing out people who still want to help"; Joanna minuted it.
- **Officers elected** for 2025-26, all under the transitional clause: Hazel Pennock (chair), [[femi-oyelaran]] (vice-chair), [[joanna-pryce]] (secretary), [[claire-whitlow]] (treasurer). Five further board members elected, including [[ravinder-sahota]] and [[hamza-qureshi]].
- **2024 accounts** approved ([[farah-ansari]] examiner).
- **Chair's report:** roof repair under way ([[pavilion-roof-repair]]), the Clubfold rota, the girls' section starting, and the first mention to members of a changing-rooms bid.

## What I noticed

Members asked more about the roof and the changing rooms than the constitution. Two parents asked when I would be stepping down; I said honestly that the new rules meant 2027. That was the first time I had said it out loud in public, and it helped.
