# One Channel Per Audience

The communications principle behind the [[club-communications-plan]]: each group of people gets one channel for each kind of message, with one named owner, and the same message is not sent by several routes.

## In practice

| Audience | Channel | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A team's families | Team broadcast channel (match day, training) | Team manager |
| All members | Clubfold notices (official), monthly newsletter | Hamza, for now |
| The public | Club social media | Linh Pham |
| Volunteers | Clubfold rota messages | Nisha Raghavan |

## Why

The 2026 audit found the same fixture change in four places with two kick-off times ([[communications-audit-2026-02]]). One channel per audience means people know where to look and volunteers stop answering the same question repeatedly.

## Limits

It depends on owners. When a team manager is away, their channel goes quiet; each needs a named deputy.
