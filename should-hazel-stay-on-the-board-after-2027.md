# Should I Stay on the Board After 2027?

The constitution would let me stand as an ordinary board member after I stop being chair. Should I?

## Current Answer

> [!success]
> No. I will leave the board at the AGM on 12 June 2027 and stay as a volunteer, available to the new chair for questions for a year if asked. Decided in March 2026 and told to the board in April.

## Why

- A former chair sitting at the table makes it harder for the new chair to lead; people look to the old one.
- Colin did not stay on the board after 2019 and it helped me, even if the handover was short ([[colin-brightwell]]).
- The point of [[officer-term-limits]] is to make room. Staying on as an ordinary member keeps the room full.

I will still run the tea hut rota at the tournament if [[helen-marsh]] will have me.
