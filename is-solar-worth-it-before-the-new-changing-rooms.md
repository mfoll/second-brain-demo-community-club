# Is solar worth it before the new changing rooms?

## Question

Should we put solar panels on the pavilion roof now, or wait until the changing rooms are built?

## Current Answer

Deferred until the grant decision in February 2027. The one quote is £7,900 plus a £450 survey, and the installer wants the 2025 roof patch replaced first. If the changing rooms go ahead, the new block's roof may be the better place for panels and could be designed for them from the start. Marek agrees. Spending £8,000 now, while the [[match-funding-appeal]] still needs money, would also be hard to explain to members.

## Revisit when

The Lanternfield Trust decision is known, or the roof patch fails sooner than expected.
