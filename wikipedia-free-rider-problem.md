# Free-rider problem

## Summary

The English Wikipedia article on the free-rider problem, an economic idea: when people can benefit from a shared resource without paying for it, some will under-pay, and the resource ends up under-provided or worn down. The lead adds that although people tend to cooperate, the presence of free riders can make cooperation decay. Last edited 8 October 2026 at the time of the record.

## Why It Matters

I read it in July 2025 while writing the pledge paper ([[pledge-board-paper-2025-07]]). It framed the problem as I saw it then: every family enjoys match days that a few families run. That framing led us to a charge for not contributing. In hindsight it was the wrong lens for a club. Most non-helping families were not free-riding; they were working weekends, or nobody had asked them for anything specific ([[pledge-lessons-learned]]).

## Caveats

An economics concept about public goods. Treating volunteering purely as a payment problem is exactly what went wrong with the pledge.
