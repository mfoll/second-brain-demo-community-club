# Northmere FA Safeguarding Training Requirements

Northmere FA's page setting out which club roles need which safeguarding training, updated for the 2026-27 season.

## Key Details

- Coaches, team managers and anyone with regular junior contact: the association's safeguarding course, online or face to face, renewed every three years.
- Welfare officers: the welfare officer course plus the standard course.
- A face-to-face session at least once in every two renewals is "strongly encouraged".
- Background checks through the association for the same roles, renewed on the association's schedule.
- Clubs must be able to show compliance at affiliation; the association samples clubs each year.

## Why It Matters

The basis for the club's three-year rule and for the September workshop ([[safeguarding-workshop-2026-09-19]]). It also means the [[safeguarding-training-refresh]] has a real affiliation consequence, not just good practice.

## Caveats

Athletics roles follow Northmere Athletics guidance ([[northmere-athletics-welfare-guidance]]), which is similar but not identical.
