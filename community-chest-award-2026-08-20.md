# Draymoor Community Chest Awards £15,000, 20 August 2026

Letter and email from the Draymoor Community Chest on Thursday 20 August 2026: £15,000 awarded to the club as match funding for the new changing rooms.

## Terms

- Payable once the Lanternfield grant is confirmed and the building contract is signed.
- To be returned if the project does not go ahead by 31 December 2028.
- The council's logo on the site board and a mention in the opening.

## What it means

With the board's £25,000 of reserves agreed on 12 May, the non-appeal part of the [[match-funding]] was complete: £40,000 of £63,000. The appeal target stayed at £23,000 instead of rising to £38,000, which we had planned for in the spring ([[match-funding-plan-2026-03]]).

Anita rang me before the letter arrived. I told the board by email the same evening and Claire added it to the funding statement for the grant ([[changing-rooms-grant-application]]).
