# Wet Weather Schedule

A fallback schedule agreed before an outdoor event, saying exactly what changes if it rains: which pitch closes first, which games shorten and by how much, which matches are dropped, and who makes the call.

We did not have one in 2026. On the Sunday the decisions were made on the spot by Sam and Martin under a gazebo: close pitch 2 after 14:00, play the under-13 and under-14 finals on pitch 1 as 8-minute halves. It worked, but two teams left early because nobody could tell them when their last games would be.

For 2027, Martin is writing it into [[run-tournament-day]]:
- Pitch 2 closes first (lowest ground, by the drainage ditch).
- Group games shorten from 12 to 10 minutes, then 8.
- Plate competitions are dropped before finals are.
- The organiser and fixtures secretary decide together, and the decision goes out by tannoy and the tournament channel at once.
