# Girls-Only Entry Sessions

Free, low-pressure, girls-only sessions as the way in for girls who would not walk into a mixed team's training.

What we have learned from one taster day and a season:

- Girls come with a friend, so invite pairs and small groups.
- Female coaches and players visible from the start matter more than facilities.
- No scores, no sign-up pressure on the day; a follow-up message a week later.
- Under-7s need their own corner, or younger sisters end up watching.

Results: 23 girls came to the [[girls-football-taster-day-2025-06-21]], 14 joined, 12 are still playing. The next one, for under-9s, is planned for April 2027. The same idea has been suggested for adult women returning to football.
