# Succession planning

## Summary

English Wikipedia article (last edited 10 July 2026 when rechecked). It describes succession planning as a deliberate way of passing on leadership roles: identifying the roles that matter most and the skills they need, then finding and developing people who could step into them when they fall vacant. Most of the article is about businesses, including small and family firms handing on ownership and management.

## Why It Matters

I read it the weekend after coffee with Colin ([[coffee-with-colin-2026-01-16]]), before opening the [[chair-succession-plan]] on 19 January 2026. The useful idea was to start from the critical roles rather than from a favoured person. That is why the plan covers the chair and the treasurer, why the chair's job was written down first ([[chair-role-description-2026]]), and why I keep saying the AGM, not me, chooses. It shaped [[succession-by-shadowing]].

## Caveats

Written for companies with paid staff and promotion paths. A volunteer club cannot develop people against their will, and our successors have day jobs.
