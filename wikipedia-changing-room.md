# Changing room

English Wikipedia article, last edited 5 July 2026. Licence CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Summary

A short lead: a changing room (also locker room, changeroom or dressing room) is a room or area set aside for changing clothes, provided in semi-public settings so people can change with some degree of privacy.

## Why It Matters

I looked it up after the first Saturday observation with Aoife in May 2026, half to find the right words for the grant. The phrase about "varying degrees of privacy" stuck with me, because our 1974 block offers very little: one shared space for everyone. It shaped how we wrote the need section: the problem is not comfort, it is that nobody can be given the privacy that age and role require ([[changing-rooms-design-v2-2026-05]], [[new-changing-rooms]]).
