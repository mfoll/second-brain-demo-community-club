# Report a Welfare Concern

How any volunteer, coach, parent or member raises a concern about a child or an adult at the club. This is the process only, taken from the club policy ([[fernhollow-safeguarding-policy]]). Concerns themselves are never recorded in this vault.

## When to Use

Whenever something you see, hear or are told makes you worried about a child's or a young person's welfare, or about how an adult is behaving towards juniors, at the club or connected to it. You do not need to be sure. It is the welfare officer's job to judge, not yours.

## Steps

1. **If a child is in immediate danger, call the emergency services first.** Then tell the welfare officer.
2. **If a child tells you something:** listen, stay calm, do not ask leading questions, do not promise to keep it secret. Tell them you will pass it to someone who can help.
3. **Tell the welfare officer the same day,** in person or by phone: [[aoife-brennan]], or the deputy [[beatriz-ferreira]] if Aoife is away or the concern involves her. Their numbers are on the pavilion noticeboard and in Clubfold.
4. **Write down what you saw or heard,** in the child's words where possible, with date and time, on the club's concern form. Give it to the welfare officer. Keep no copy.
5. **Do not discuss it** with anyone else, including other volunteers, the board, the family, or the group chat, unless the welfare officer asks you to.
6. **The welfare officer decides next steps,** which may include advice from [[rebecca-thorne]] at Northmere FA, Northmere Athletics, or statutory services.

## Guardrails

- Never investigate yourself.
- Never put details in email, messages, Clubfold notes, board papers or this vault.
- The chair is told only what the chair needs to know, by the welfare officer.
- If the concern is about the welfare officer, go to the deputy; if about both, to Northmere FA directly.

## Verification

Every volunteer hears this route at induction ([[onboard-a-new-volunteer]]) and at refresher training. The short version is on a card in each first-aid kit.
