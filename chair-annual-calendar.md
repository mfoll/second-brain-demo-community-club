# Chair's Annual Calendar

The year as it falls for the chair. Board meetings are the second Tuesday of every month except August.

| Month | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| January | Budget first draft and reserves review ([[club-reserves-policy]]); accounts year-end starts |
| February | Fee options for the next season; Lanternfield or other grant rounds open |
| March | Fees set; accounts to the examiner |
| April | Volunteer recognition; AGM date confirmed |
| May | Examiner signs; AGM papers out at least 21 days ahead |
| June | AGM (second Saturday); registrations open; tournament final push |
| July | Summer tournament; affiliation due 31 July ([[northmere-fa-affiliation-requirements-2026]]) |
| August | No board; pitch handover from the council; coaches' meeting |
| September | Season starts; skills and roles check; Oakreach after-school club restarts |
| October | Membership numbers; bursary report; Christmas fundraising ideas |
| November | Safeguarding training check; budget brief to the treasurer |
| December | Short board; thank-you notes; plan next year's AGM |

Adjust each year; the 2027 AGM is on 12 June ([[agm-2027-planning-notes]]).
