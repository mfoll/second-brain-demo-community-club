# Volunteer Recruitment Drive

Nisha Raghavan's role-based approach to finding new volunteers, which replaced the compulsory family pledge in February 2026.

## Outcome

Twelve new regular volunteers recruited through role descriptions and one-off slots by 31 December 2026.

## Current State

- Seven new regular volunteers so far (October 2026), against a target of twelve.
- 27 role descriptions published in Clubfold; 11 one-off slot types.
- One-off slots filled 212 times since March, by 64 different adults. See [[one-off-slots-uptake-2026]].
- Three of the seven new regulars started with one-off slots, which is the route we hoped for.
- Biggest gaps: a second Clubfold rota admin, a deputy fixtures secretary and two team managers for the under-13s.

## Scope and Boundaries

Recruiting and inducting adult volunteers. Safeguarding training and checks for new starters run through [[safeguarding-training-refresh]] and [[safer-recruitment]]; nobody starts with juniors until both are done. Coaching qualifications belong to [[season-2026-27-plan]].

## Working Context

Nisha's argument in her January paper ([[role-based-recruitment-proposal-2026-01]]) was that "can you help?" gets no answer, while "can you run the tea hut for the under-10s home games, about 2 hours, six Saturdays before Christmas?" often does. Each role description says what the job is, how long it takes, who you report to, whether you need a check, and what happens if you cannot do a week. One-off slots are single sessions, bookable in Clubfold, with no commitment beyond the day.

We used the [[volunteer-taster-morning-2026-03-07]], the [[volunteer-centre-fair-2026-04-25]] with [[celine-dubois]], the [[club-communications-plan]] newsletter and the summer tournament to recruit. Progress month by month is in [[recruitment-drive-progress]].
