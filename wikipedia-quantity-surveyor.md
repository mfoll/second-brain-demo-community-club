# Quantity surveyor

English Wikipedia article, last edited 11 October 2026. Licence CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Summary

The article describes a quantity surveyor as a construction professional with expert knowledge of building costs and contracts, and notes that the qualified title and the body that confers it differ between countries.

## Why It Matters

Femi asked in June 2026 whether we should pay a quantity surveyor to check the three quotes before choosing. Marek did the comparison himself, from 30 years of school building work ([[changing-rooms-quote-comparison]]), and the board accepted that for the grant. But the contract administrator allowance in the £16,500 provisional sums is there partly because we will want cost control by a professional during the build ([[changing-rooms-cost-breakdown]]).

## Caveats

Whether to appoint one is a decision for the board once the grant is known.
