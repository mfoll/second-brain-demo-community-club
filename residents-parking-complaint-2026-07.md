# Residents' Association Letter on Tournament Parking, July 2026

Letter from the Fernhollow Residents' Association to the council's sport and leisure team, copied to the club, dated 6 July 2026 and received by me on 8 July.

## Summary

- On Saturday 4 July visiting cars parked on both sides of Millrace Lane and the two side streets, on verges and across two driveways.
- One resident could not get out for a hospital appointment and had to be helped by a neighbour.
- Sunday was better because of the rain and fewer spectators.
- The Association asks the council to require a parking plan for any future event of this size, and asks the club to talk to residents beforehand.

## Why It Matters

Fair and specific. It led to the meeting on 22 July ([[residents-association-meeting-2026-07-22]]) and to the council's condition for 2027 ([[council-event-conditions-letter-2026-08]]). The tournament itself is Sam's and is written up in [[summer-tournament-2026-debrief]].
