# Northmere FA Guidance on Changing Facilities for Grassroots Clubs

A 12-page guidance note from Northmere Football Association on changing rooms for grassroots clubs, sent by Vikram Joshi in April 2026 when we told him about the safeguarding review.

## Summary

- Recommends separate changing for juniors and adults, and for girls and boys where teams train or play at the same time.
- Recommends a separate officials' room with its own shower, especially where officials may be young referees.
- Minimum sizes per player for benches and shower provision.
- Supervision: adults should not be in junior changing rooms except where needed, and then in pairs; no phones or cameras in changing rooms.
- Accessible changing should be a separate room, not just an accessible WC.

## Why It Matters

It gave Neil and Aoife an outside reference for the May 2026 redesign ([[changing-rooms-design-revision-2026-05-19]]), and the trust likes to see a governing body's standards cited. The supervision points also fed into [[changing-room-supervision-rules]].
