# Will the Hutchins & Kerr quote hold until the grant decision?

## Question

The £248,000 fixed price runs to 31 March 2027. The trust decides in February 2027 if all goes well. Will the price still hold when we can actually sign, and what if it does not?

## Why It Matters

The whole funding package is built on £248,000. Any increase falls on the club, which has already committed most of its reserves.

## What we know

- Dale has said informally he could hold the price "a few months" past March, with indexation after that.
- Signing needs the lease, the grant offer letter and the planning consent. Planning cannot realistically be granted before April 2027 ([[do-we-need-planning-consent-for-the-changing-rooms]]).
- So even on the best timetable we sign in May or June 2027, after the fixed date.

## Current Answer

Ask now, in writing, for the fixed price to be held to 30 June 2027, offering a letter of intent subject to funding. Marek is drafting it. If Dale will only offer indexation, put a contingency on the appeal ([[should-the-appeal-target-include-a-contingency]]).
