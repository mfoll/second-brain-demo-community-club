# Letters of Support for the Changing Rooms

Five letters of support gathered in September 2026 and attached to the Lanternfield application as one PDF.

## Summary

- Councillor Rhys Penhallow, ward councillor for Fernhollow: the club's role in the neighbourhood and the council's intention on the lease.
- Northmere Football Association, club development officer: growth of the girls' section and the association's facilities guidance ([[northmere-fa-changing-room-guidance]]).
- Oakreach School, headteacher: the after-school athletics club and community use.
- Fernhollow Residents' Association, chair: support for the building, with a line about working together on parking.
- Draymoor Borough Council sport and leisure team: pitch use figures and the Community Chest award.

## Why It Matters

The trust scores community backing. Joanna drafted suggested points for each writer but asked them to use their own words. The Residents' Association letter came only a few weeks after the parking complaint ([[residents-association-meeting-2026-07-22]]), which says something good about how that conversation went. No letter names any junior or family.
