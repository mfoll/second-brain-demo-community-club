# The Chair Should Not Run the Rota

## Stance

The chair should not be the person who fills gaps on the volunteer rota. When the chair quietly covers, the board never sees the gap, the load grows invisibly, and the next chair inherits a job nobody knew existed.

## Scope

Fernhollow, and I suspect most small clubs. A chair should still help out on a Saturday like anyone else; the point is not to be the backstop.

## Why I Think This

- For years I filled tea hut gaps, marked lines and published the rota when Nisha was away. The board saw a full rota and assumed all was well.
- [[colin-brightwell]] did the same before me, and I inherited about six jobs on top of chairing in 2019.
- The [[chair-succession-plan]] only works if the job being handed over is the chair's job, not chair plus odd jobs.

## What Would Change My Mind

If the club shrank to the point where there genuinely was nobody else. We are not there; 64 people have done one-off slots this year.

## Implications

- I now report rota gaps to the board as gaps.
- A second Clubfold admin who is not me ([[who-takes-over-the-clubfold-rota-admin]]).
- My handover list for the next chair separates chair duties from the odd jobs I have collected, so each can be reassigned.
