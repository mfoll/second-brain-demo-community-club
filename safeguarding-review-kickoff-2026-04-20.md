# Safeguarding Review Kickoff 20 April 2026

First working session of the [[safeguarding-policy-review]], Monday 20 April 2026, in the pavilion meeting room. Present: [[aoife-brennan]] (lead), [[beatriz-ferreira]], [[linh-pham]] for the photo strand, and me.

## What We Agreed

- Work from the terms of reference the board approved in April ([[safeguarding-review-terms-of-reference-2026]]).
- Keep the 2022 structure ([[fernhollow-safeguarding-policy]]) and the Northmere FA template, and fix the gaps rather than start again.
- Three strands that could finish at different times: photos and social media (Linh and Aoife), training and checks (Aoife and Beatriz), and changing rooms and supervision (Aoife, Beatriz and me).
- Aoife to produce a gap list by mid-May ([[safeguarding-review-gap-list]]).
- Target: revised policy to the board in the autumn.

## Ground Rules

Aoife set these at the start and I wrote them on the flipchart:

- We talk about policy, buildings and processes. We do not discuss any past or current concern, even without names.
- Anyone who has a concern raised by the discussion takes it to Aoife privately afterwards.
- Documents stay in the safeguarding folder on the shared drive, not in email attachments.

## Afterwards

The photo strand moved fastest and became [[photo-and-social-media-guidance]]. The changing-room strand started with Saturday observations in May ([[changing-room-observation-2026-05-16]]).
