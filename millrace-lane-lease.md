# Millrace Lane Lease and Draft Heads of Terms

The club's current lease of Millrace Lane playing fields from Draymoor Borough Council, and the draft heads of terms for a new 30-year lease agreed with the council's estates team in July 2026. Both are summarised here; the signed originals are in the club's deed box held by the secretary, and scans are in the shared drive under Facilities/Lease.

## Summary

The current lease runs from 1 April 2006 to 31 March 2031. It covers the pavilion and its car park; the pitches are used under a separate pitch hire arrangement renewed each season. The draft heads of terms would replace both with a single 30-year lease from 1 April 2027, giving the club the 25 years' tenure the Lanternfield Trust requires.

## Key Details

Current lease (2006 to 2031):

- Term: 25 years, ending 31 March 2031. No right to renew.
- Demise: pavilion, changing block, car park and the grass strip between them. The pitches themselves are not in the lease.
- Rent: £850 a year, unchanged since 2016.
- Repairs: the club repairs the inside of the pavilion; the council repairs structure and roof "where reasonably required". In practice the council has not paid for a roof repair since 2009, which is why the club paid for the 2025 [[pavilion-roof-repair]] itself.
- Use: sport and community use; alcohol only under temporary event notices.
- Pitch hire: charged each season under the council's sports pitch tariff. For 2026-27 this rose 12% ([[pitch-hire-schedule-2026-27]]).

Draft heads of terms (agreed with officers 21 July 2026, subject to cabinet):

- Term: 30 years, 1 April 2027 to 31 March 2057, with the 2006 lease surrendered at the same time.
- Demise: pavilion, changing block, car park and both full-size and three mini pitches.
- Rent: £1,500 a year, reviewed every five years; pitch hire charges end once the lease starts, replaced by the rent and the club's maintenance obligations.
- Repairs: club takes full repair of the pavilion and any new building, and routine pitch maintenance; council keeps the main surface water drains and boundary trees ([[repairing-obligations]]).
- Community use clause: pitches available to schools and community groups at agreed times.
- Consent to a funder's restriction on the title for 25 years.
- Break: council may end the lease if the club stops using the site for sport for two consecutive seasons.
- Assignment to an incorporated successor body permitted with consent ([[club-incorporation-review]]).

## Why It Matters

Everything in [[new-changing-rooms]] depends on this. The current lease gives less than five years, so the grant cannot be offered on it. The new lease also moves the pitches from hire into the lease, which on Claire's numbers saves the club money from 2027 even with full repairs, but puts maintenance risk on us ([[should-the-club-take-on-pitch-drainage-under-the-new-lease]]).

Cabinet deferred approval on 17 September 2026 ([[council-cabinet-deferral-2026-09-17]]).

## Caveats

- Heads of terms are not binding. The final lease wording has not yet been checked by a solicitor.
- The council's [[community-asset-review]] may revisit rent levels for all community leases.
