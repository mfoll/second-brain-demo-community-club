# Council Letter on Conditions for Future Events, August 2026

Letter from Anita Venkatesh, sport and leisure, dated 11 August 2026, following the Residents' Association complaint.

## Summary

- Any event at Millrace Lane expecting more than 30 visiting teams will need the council's agreement at least three months ahead.
- Condition for the 2027 tournament: a stewarded parking plan, including overflow parking off the lane, at least six marshals in high-visibility vests at peak times, and a letter to residents on the affected streets two weeks before.
- The council will not close the lane.
- The letter thanks the club for its quick response and the meeting on 22 July.

## Why It Matters

Without this plan there is no 2027 tournament, and without the tournament the appeal and general funds lose their biggest earner ([[summer-tournament-2026-debrief]]). The plan is open in [[how-do-we-handle-tournament-parking-in-2027]].
