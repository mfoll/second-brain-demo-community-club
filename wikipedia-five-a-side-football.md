# Five-a-side football

## Summary

Describes five-a-side football: a form of small-sided football with five players per team, a smaller pitch and goals and shorter games, played indoors or outdoors. Related forms include futsal and six- and seven-a-side.

## Why It Matters

Background for the [[season-launch-meeting-2026-08-18]], where Tom Ashworth argued for keeping our under-7s and under-8s in 5-a-side festivals ([[mini-football-festival]]). Fewer players means more touches of the ball per child, which is the whole point at that age.

## Caveats

The article is mostly about adult and commercial five-a-side. Junior formats and pitch sizes come from the [[northmere-fa-league-handbook-2026-27]]. Article last edited 2026-10-11 when checked; licence CC BY-SA 4.0.
