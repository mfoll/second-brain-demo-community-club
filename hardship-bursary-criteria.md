# Hardship Bursary Criteria 2026-27

## Summary

One-page scheme agreed on Monday 16 March 2026 by [[ravinder-sahota]], [[claire-whitlow]] and me, under the board's decision of 10 March ([[board-meeting-2026-03-10]]).

## Key Details

- **Who:** any junior member's family who tells us the fee is a problem. No evidence asked for; a short conversation with Ravinder.
- **What:** £120 per child for 2026-27: half the £165 fee (£82.50) plus £37.50 towards kit or away travel.
- **Fund:** £2,400, enough for 20 awards. £930 from 2025-26 pledge opt-outs, £1,470 from the season budget.
- **Decided by:** Ravinder and one other board member who handles money (currently Claire). Nobody else is told.
- **Recorded:** as a fee adjustment in Clubfold under a restricted code. Board reports give counts and totals only.
- **Coaches:** told only that "the fee is sorted". No coach is ever asked to judge need.
- **Review:** after one season, in January 2027.

## Why It Matters

The criteria are deliberately light. The aim is that asking costs a family less than the fee does. See [[hardship-bursary-notes-2026]] for how it has worked so far.
