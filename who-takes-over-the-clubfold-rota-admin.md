# Who Takes Over the Clubfold Rota Admin?

## Question

Who becomes the second, and eventually the main, Clubfold rota administrator, so the rota does not depend on [[nisha-raghavan]] and me?

## Why It Matters

Only Nisha and I can edit rota templates and publish the weekly rota. If Nisha is ill or away, I do it, which is exactly the kind of gap-filling I am trying to stop ([[the-chair-should-not-run-the-rota]]). When I step down in June 2027 the rota would have one admin.

## What We Know

- The job is about two hours a week in season, mostly on Sunday evenings, plus swaps during the week ([[volunteer-role-descriptions]]).
- It needs someone comfortable with Clubfold and with chasing people politely.
- It does not need a background check, because the role has no contact with juniors, but the admin can see volunteers' contact details, so it needs someone the board trusts.
- Two possible people from the recruitment drive have said "maybe after Christmas".

## Current Answer

None yet. Advertised as a role in October 2026. Target: someone shadowing Nisha by Christmas and publishing alone by March 2027.
