# Oakreach School Partnership

Keep free use of the Oakreach School track for our athletics squad, in return for club coaches running the school's after-school athletics club, and put the arrangement on a footing that lasts beyond July 2027.

## Outcome

Free track use on Tuesday evenings in exchange for club coaches running a Wednesday after-school athletics club, renewed beyond July 2027.

## Current State

- The swap has run since September 2025 ([[oakreach-track-agreement]]): our junior athletics squad and masters group use the six-lane track free on Tuesdays 18:00 to 20:00; Kwabena Osei and another club coach run a free Wednesday after-school athletics club for about 25 pupils in term time.
- In September 2026 the school's new business manager, Priya Lal, proposed charging the club the standard lettings rate of £45 an hour ([[oakreach-lettings-proposal-2026-09]]). For two hours a week in term time, about 70 hours a year, that would be about £3,150.
- On Tuesday 6 October 2026 the head, Adaeze Okafor, agreed to honour the free arrangement until July 2027 while both sides review it ([[oakreach-lettings-meeting-2026-10-06]]).
- Luke Garvey, head of PE, is firmly on our side. Priya is doing her job: the school's lettings income is under pressure and she was not there when the swap was agreed.

## Scope and Boundaries

The relationship with the school and the written agreement. The coaching itself, session plans and which coaches go on Wednesdays belong to the athletics section ([[run-the-season]], Sheila and Kwabena). No pupil names or registers in the vault: Kwabena keeps the after-school register on the school's own system, and we only note counts ([[oakreach-after-school-club-summary]]).

## Working Context

Before 2025 the athletics squad trained on grass at Millrace Lane and hired the Oakreach track for occasional Saturday sessions at the lettings rate. Sheila, Luke and I worked out the swap in summer 2025: the school wanted an after-school athletics club it could not staff; we wanted a track. Nobody wrote down what each hour was worth, which is now the problem. See [[oakreach-partnership-history]].

My argument for the review, which I put to Adaeze on 6 October:

- Two qualified coaches for an hour and a half a week, with planning, is worth more than two hours of an empty track on a Tuesday evening.
- About 25 pupils a term take part, and four Oakreach pupils have joined the club squad since (that count includes no names).
- Our coaches hold current safeguarding training and background checks through Clubfold, which saves the school its own vetting.

Priya's counter-argument is fair: the track's floodlights and caretaker cost the school real money on a Tuesday, and other lettings pay. My preference is to keep the swap in kind and put a value on both sides in writing ([[keep-the-oakreach-swap-in-kind]]). A fallback would be a reduced community rate. See [[joint-use-arrangement]] and [[should-we-pay-the-oakreach-lettings-fee-after-july-2027]].
