# What the Chair Actually Does

My handover notes for whoever chairs after June 2027. The formal version is the [[chair-role-description-2026]]; this is the honest one. I add to it as things come up.

## Every week

- Read the club email and the board group chat; answer what only the chair can answer, pass on the rest. Most things are not the chair's to answer.
- Sunday evening call with the vice-chair about the week ahead.
- Turn up somewhere on a Saturday. People raise things with you at the touchline that never reach an email. Listen, write it down, do not decide there.

## Every month

- Agenda with [[joanna-pryce]] by the Monday two weeks before the board; papers out a week before ([[how-to-run-a-board-meeting]]).
- Chair the board. Keep to time: 21:30 is the target, 22:00 is the limit.
- Check in with the welfare officer, [[aoife-brennan]]. You do not need to know details; you need to know she is supported and has what she needs.
- Read [[claire-whitlow]]'s monthly finance note ([[monthly-finance-check]]).

## Every year

See [[chair-annual-calendar]].

## The outside world

- The council: [[anita-venkatesh]] for pitches and bookings, [[graham-sutcliffe]] for the lease. Copy in [[rhys-penhallow]] on anything that might go to cabinet.
- The Lanternfield Trust: [[esther-lowell]]. From January 2027 this is Femi's.
- Oakreach School: the head, [[adaeze-okafor]], for anything about the agreement; [[luke-garvey]] for the day to day.
- Northmere FA and Northmere Athletics: affiliation each July ([[northmere-fa-affiliation-requirements-2026]]).

## Things nobody writes down

- The pavilion boiler timer resets after a power cut. [[marek-zielinski]] knows.
- When a parent complains, ring them back within two days even if you have no answer yet.
- If two board members fall out, speak to each separately before the next meeting, not at it.
- Thank people by name, in the newsletter and in person. It costs nothing and it keeps volunteers.
- You will be asked about safeguarding concerns. Never take details yourself; point people to the welfare officer and the policy, every time.
