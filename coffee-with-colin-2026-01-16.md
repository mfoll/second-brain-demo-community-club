# Coffee With Colin 16 January 2026

Friday 16 January 2026, with [[colin-brightwell]], at the café by Fernhollow library.

I asked him what he would do differently about handing over. His answers, roughly:

- Start a year earlier than feels necessary. He gave me three weeks in 2019 and I spent the first season ringing him.
- Write down the things that are not in any document: who at the council answers the phone, which parents will help if asked directly, what the pavilion boiler does in January.
- Let the successor make decisions while you are still there, so their first mistakes are small ones.
- Do not choose your successor. Make it possible for several people to stand.

I opened the [[chair-succession-plan]] the following Monday. He followed up by email a few weeks later ([[colin-brightwell-email-2026-02]]).
