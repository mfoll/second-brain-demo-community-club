# NIMBY

English Wikipedia article, last edited 15 October 2026. Licence CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Summary

The article explains "not in my back yard", or NIMBY, as a phrase for residents' opposition to new developments near them and their support for strict land-use rules. It notes the implied criticism: that such residents object only because the development is close, and would accept it elsewhere.

## Why It Matters

After the tournament parking letter in July 2026 ([[residents-parking-complaint-2026-07]]), someone on the board called the residents "nimbys". I looked the word up and did not like what I found: it is a dismissive label, and it did not fit. The Residents' Association did not object to the tournament or the club, only to being blocked in without warning, and it later backed the changing rooms grant ([[letters-of-support-changing-rooms]]). I asked the board not to use the word in minutes or messages.

## Caveats

Read as a reminder about language, not as an analysis of our neighbours.
