# Board Meeting 10 March 2026

Monthly board meeting, Tuesday 10 March 2026, 19:30, pavilion meeting room. The meeting that set 2026-27 fees.

## At a Glance

> [!note]
> Fees for 2026-27 agreed by 6 votes to 1: juniors £140 to £165, adults £190 to £210. Ravinder Sahota voted against and his dissent was minuted at his request. The board agreed in principle a confidential hardship bursary as the compromise, set up six days later.

## Attendance

Present: Hazel Pennock (chair), [[femi-oyelaran]], [[ravinder-sahota]], [[claire-whitlow]], [[joanna-pryce]] (minutes), [[marek-zielinski]], [[hamza-qureshi]], [[donovan-clarke]]. Attending ex officio without a vote: [[aoife-brennan]]. Apologies: [[sheila-mortimer]]. Eight board members present, quorum met. As chair I do not vote except to break a tie, so seven voted.

## Fees for 2026-27

Papers: [[fee-review-options-paper-2026]], [[club-budget-2026-27]], [[ravinder-sliding-scale-proposal]], [[pitch-hire-increase-letter-2026]].

Claire presented the budget. Pitch hire rises 12%, from £10,000 to £11,200. Reserves of about £31,000 are expected to fall to about £6,000 once £25,000 goes to [[match-funding]]. Option B (juniors £165, adults £210) covers rising costs and puts about £5,800 a season back towards reserves.

Ravinder presented the sliding scale: three junior bands of £120, £165 and £190. He pointed out that 23 families asked for payment plans in 2025-26 ([[payment-plan-requests-2025-26]]) and said a £25 rise on top of last year's £30 pledge opt-out would be the last straw for some of them.

Discussion, as minuted:

- Femi: the trust will ask what the club's own members are putting in; a fee that covers costs and builds reserves is part of that answer.
- Donovan: supports option B, but coaches must know how to point families to help, quietly.
- Claire: a sliding scale needs household income checks the club cannot do fairly or keep private, and Clubfold cannot be set up for bands before September.
- Hamza: the fee should be explained to members as running costs first, building second.
- Aoife (no vote): asked that any help be arranged without coaches knowing which children receive it.
- Ravinder: accepted the practical points on timing, not the principle.

**Vote on option B: 6 for, 1 against (Ravinder Sahota).**

Ravinder asked for his dissent to be minuted. Agreed wording: "R. Sahota voted against. He considers a flat rise unfair to lower-income families and believes a sliding scale is the better long-term approach. He asks that the board revisit it after one season." ([[minuted-dissent]])

## The bursary compromise

Agreed in principle without a vote: a confidential hardship bursary for 2026-27, absorbing the £930 of pledge opt-out money already moved on 10 February ([[board-meeting-2026-02-10]]), topped up from the budget. Ravinder offered to run it. Claire, Ravinder and I to agree criteria within a week. Set up on 16 March as the [[hardship-bursary-fund]] with £2,400 ([[hardship-bursary-criteria]]).
