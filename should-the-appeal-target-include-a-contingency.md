# Should the appeal target include a contingency?

## Question

Should we raise the appeal target above £23,000 to cover a possible price rise or overrun, or keep it at £23,000 and deal with any gap if it comes?

## Why It Matters

The board has said it will not take more from reserves, which will be about £6,000 after the match-funding commitment. The quote includes the contractor's own contingency, and our £16,500 fees and contingency line is modest ([[changing-rooms-cost-breakdown]]). If the price is indexed after March 2027, we could need £7,000 to £12,000 more.

## Views so far

- Claire: keep the published target at £23,000 so people see an end, but quietly aim for £27,000 and say so if we get there.
- Ravinder: no second ask of members this season after the fee rise.
- Marek: get the quote extended first and the question may go away ([[will-the-hutchins-kerr-quote-hold-until-the-grant-decision]]).

## Current Answer

Leaning towards Claire's approach. Decide at the board on 8 December once we know about the lease and the quote.
