# Leanne Fairbairn

Coach of the girls' under-11s and the person who started the girls' section in June 2025. Played adult women's football for the club for years before coaching.

## Role

- Head coach of the girls' under-11s, Northmere FA qualified.
- Unofficial lead of the girls' section: recruitment, taster days, the link with the adult women's team.

## How we work together

Leanne came to me in spring 2025 with a plan already written: a taster day, one team, one season, then review. I mostly cleared obstacles (pitch slot, kit from [[yusuf-demir]], a board line in the budget). She prefers decisions quickly and in writing.
