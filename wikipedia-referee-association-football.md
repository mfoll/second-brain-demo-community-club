# Referee (association football)

## Summary

Explains the role of the referee in association football: the official who interprets and enforces the Laws of the Game, has final say on facts connected with play, starts and stops the match and disciplines players and coaches.

## Why It Matters

Martin added it to the referee recruitment pack for parents who are thinking about the Northmere FA course, alongside his [[referee-availability-2026-27]] list. It is a plain description of what the job involves, which helps people decide.

## Caveats

Nothing about youth refereeing or how to protect young referees from touchline abuse, which is our real recruitment problem. Article last edited 2026-10-10 when checked; licence CC BY-SA 4.0.
