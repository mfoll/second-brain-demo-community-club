# Council Cabinet Defers the Millrace Lane Lease, 17 September 2026

Draymoor Borough Council's cabinet did not approve the 30-year lease of Millrace Lane on Thursday 17 September 2026. It deferred the item to its meeting on 19 November 2026, pending the council's community asset review.

## At a Glance

> [!warning]
> The Lanternfield Trust needs evidence of 25 years' tenure by 15 December 2026. If cabinet defers again on 19 November, there is less than four weeks of slack before the application rolls to round 1 of 2027 and the Hutchins & Kerr fixed price runs out first.

## What happened

- Graham Sutcliffe rang me at 9:15 that morning to say the item would be deferred. The officer report recommending approval was unchanged; the decision was political, not about the club.
- At the meeting, the cabinet member for resources said the council had begun a review of all leases of council land to community groups in August 2026 and that cabinet should not approve new leases over 10 years until it reported. Three other community leases on the agenda were deferred with ours.
- Councillor Rhys Penhallow spoke for the club. He set out the grant deadline and asked for a firm date. Cabinet agreed to bring our item back on 19 November "subject to the review's interim findings".
- Marek and I watched from the public gallery. Nobody from the club spoke; members of the public cannot speak at cabinet without notice.

## What it means

- The grant went in on 30 September anyway, with the deferral explained openly ([[changing-rooms-grant-submission-2026-09-30]]). We judged that waiting for round 1 of 2027 would risk the quote and lose six months.
- I told Esther Lowell at the trust the next morning. On 9 October she gave us until 15 December to evidence the lease ([[lanternfield-tenure-deadline-email-2026-10-09]]).
- Graham's reading is that the review is about rent levels and community use clauses, both of which our heads of terms already cover ([[millrace-lane-lease]], [[community-asset-review]]).
