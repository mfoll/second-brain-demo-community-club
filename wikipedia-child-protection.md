# Child protection

## Summary

The English Wikipedia article on child protection. The lead describes it as protecting children from violence, exploitation, abuse, abandonment and neglect, and lists its parts: recognising signs of possible harm, responding to allegations or suspicions, supporting children and families, and holding to account those who cause harm. Last edited 10 August 2026 at the time of the record.

## Why It Matters

[[aoife-brennan]] suggested the review group read the lead before the first working session ([[safeguarding-review-kickoff-2026-04-20]]), so that everyone, including volunteers new to safeguarding, had the same broad picture. For the club it is a reminder of where our job stops: we recognise and respond through [[report-a-welfare-concern]]; investigation and intervention belong to the welfare officer and the statutory services.

## Caveats

Written at the level of states and agencies, not sports clubs. Practice varies by country, and the club follows its governing bodies' guidance ([[northmere-fa-safeguarding-template]]) rather than anything general.
