# Girls Under-14s First League Match 2026-09-13

The girls' under-14s played their first Northmere FA league match on Sunday 13 September 2026, at home on pitch 1. Lost 2-3. Fourteen girls in the squad, twelve available.

Why it matters: until this season the older girls played in the girls' under-11s squad or not at all. The team formed in August from girls who had aged out of the under-11s plus five new players. Ellie Tranter assisted. Leanne came to watch and said the second half was the best football she had seen from them.

Two practical notes: the team has no permanent head coach beyond this season (see [[girls-football-section]]), and Sunday home games mean sharing the changing room with the adult women's team, which the interim [[changing-room-supervision-rules]] handle with staggered times.
