# Donor recognition wall

English Wikipedia article, last edited 13 August 2020. Licence CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Summary

The article describes a donor recognition wall as a display, usually wall-mounted in a central spot in a hospital, university, museum, library, place of worship or other nonprofit, listing the people and companies who gave to a capital campaign or another major fundraising effort. It gives two purposes: to honour major contributors and to encourage others to give.

## Why It Matters

Helen found this when she was working out the name-a-peg idea in June 2026. Our version is smaller and more personal: a plate over a coat peg instead of a wall of names, and £25 rather than large gifts ([[name-a-peg-scheme]]). The point about recognition encouraging others held true; peg sales picked up once the first list of team names went up on the pavilion noticeboard.

## Caveats

Background on a common practice, not advice. We do not list donors' names without their agreement.
