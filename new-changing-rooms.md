# New Changing Rooms

Replace the 1974 prefabricated changing block at Millrace Lane with proper changing rooms: two junior rooms, an officials' room and an accessible room, at a build cost of about £248,000, open for the 2028-29 season.

## Outcome

New changing rooms open at Millrace Lane for the 2028-29 season, paid for by a Lanternfield Trust grant of £185,000 and £63,000 of [[match-funding]], on a lease long enough to satisfy the trust. The old prefab is removed safely and the site is left tidy.

## Current State

- Design and a fixed quote of £248,000 from Hutchins & Kerr Building are done ([[changing-rooms-design-and-quotes]], closed 26 June 2026).
- The Lanternfield application for £185,000 went in on 30 September 2026 ([[changing-rooms-grant-submission-2026-09-30]]). Decision expected February 2027.
- The lease is the risk. Cabinet deferred the 30-year lease on 17 September to 19 November ([[council-cabinet-deferral-2026-09-17]]); the trust needs evidence by 15 December 2026 or the application rolls to the next round.
- Match funding: £25,000 reserves (board, 12 May 2026), £15,000 Draymoor Community Chest (awarded 20 August 2026), £23,000 appeal at £14,600 on 20 October.
- Interim supervision rules in the old block have applied since 5 September 2026 ([[changing-room-supervision-rules]]).

## Working Context

The old block is one shared space with two showers and no separate room for officials or for anyone who needs step-free access. Adult and junior sessions overlap on Saturday mornings, so for years we have relied on goodwill and timing. The safeguarding review in spring 2026 put that in writing, and Neil redrew the plan in May to give juniors their own two rooms and referees their own room. That revision is also the strongest part of the grant case ([[separate-junior-changing-is-worth-the-extra-cost]]).

The money only works because everything lands in the right order: lease, then grant decision, then quote still valid, then contract in mid-2027 and build in the close season from May to October 2028. If the lease slips, the grant slips; if the grant slips past March 2027, the quote's fixed price ends and indexation starts ([[will-the-hutchins-kerr-quote-hold-until-the-grant-decision]]). See [[changing-rooms-timeline]] for the dates in one place.

The cost breakdown is in [[changing-rooms-cost-breakdown]]. The short version is £214,000 build, £9,500 demolition and asbestos removal, £8,000 utilities and £16,500 fees and contingency.
