# Femi Oyelaran

Vice-chair of the board since the 2023 AGM and coach of the under-14s. The most likely next chair.

## Role

- Vice-chair: chairs board meetings when I am away, and since 1 September 2026 shadows me as part of the [[chair-succession-plan]] ([[femi-shadowing-starts-2026-09-01]]).
- Under-14s head coach, which keeps him close to the junior families and the coaches.
- Voted for the 2026 fee rise; argued at the time that the club could not ask the trust for £185,000 without showing its own members would contribute ([[board-meeting-2026-03-10]]).

## How we work together

We talk most weeks, usually on the phone on Sunday evening after fixtures. He likes a written brief before a difficult call and prefers to decide in the room rather than by message. Since September he chairs the meeting while I sit beside him and say as little as I can manage.

## Current threads

- The co-chair question. Femi prefers a single chair with a strong vice-chair, because he thinks two chairs blur who decides and who the council rings. He has been fair about Hamza's proposal and said so in the minutes ([[should-fernhollow-have-co-chairs]]).
- Taking the lead on the Lanternfield Trust relationship from January 2027.
- Worried, reasonably, about doing both the chair and the under-14s; he has said he would hand the team to an assistant coach if elected.
