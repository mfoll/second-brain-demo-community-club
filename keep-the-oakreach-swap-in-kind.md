# Keep the Oakreach swap in kind

## Stance

The track arrangement with Oakreach should stay a swap of coaching for track time, with no money changing hands, but with both sides valued in writing so nobody can mistake it for a free letting.

## Why I Think This

- At a sessional coaching rate, 99 coach hours a year are worth at least as much as about 70 hours of track time at £45 ([[oakreach-after-school-club-summary]]).
- Money would change the relationship. Once we pay, the after-school club becomes a service we could withdraw, and the school would have to find and vet coaches itself.
- The arrangement brings pupils into the club, which is good for both of us.

## What Would Change My Mind

If the school's floodlight and caretaker costs for our slot are genuinely more than the value it places on the after-school club, a reduced community rate would be fairer than insisting on a pure swap. Also if our coaches cannot keep up the Wednesday sessions ([[how-many-coaching-hours-can-we-give-oakreach]]).

## Implications

The January 2027 paper leads with numbers on both sides. The board should agree a fallback rate before we negotiate ([[should-we-pay-the-oakreach-lettings-fee-after-july-2027]]).
