# Constitution Review Consultation Summary

## Summary

[[joanna-pryce]]'s four-page summary of members' responses to the draft constitution, from the evening on 25 March 2025 and 27 written responses up to 11 April. Circulated to the board on 18 April 2025.

## Key Details

- **Term limits:** 31 responses supported a limit; of those, 19 preferred six years, 8 three years, 4 ten. Six years was adopted, with a transitional clause so the officers did not all leave in 2025.
- **Welfare officer without a vote:** supported once explained.
- **Junior voice:** 11 responses asked for it; a youth forum was preferred to a board seat because no junior could be a board member legally responsible for decisions.
- **Quorum of five:** no objections.
- **Co-chairs:** raised by two members; included as an option needing AGM approval.
- **Electronic voting at general meetings:** split; left out.

## Why It Matters

The record of why the 2025 constitution says what it does ([[fernhollow-constitution-2025]]), and that co-chairs were thought about long before Hamza's proposal.
