# Facility management

English Wikipedia article, last edited 5 October 2026. Licence CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Summary

The article defines facility management as the coordinated management of an organisation's buildings, sites and workplaces, together with the support services that keep them working, safe and effective for the people who use them.

## Why It Matters

Marek pointed me to it when we were writing the maintenance section of the grant in September 2026. The word that mattered was "coordinated": at the moment the pavilion is looked after by whoever notices a problem. A new building with showers, an accessible room and a 25-year funder's charge needs someone to own it ([[who-maintains-the-new-changing-rooms]], [[new-building-maintenance-plan-outline]]).

## Caveats

Written for organisations with paid staff; ours will be a small volunteer team and a paid cleaner.
