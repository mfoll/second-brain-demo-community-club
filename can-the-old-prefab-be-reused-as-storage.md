# Can the old prefab be reused as storage?

## Question

Rather than demolish the 1974 changing block, could we keep it as a store for goals, nets and kit once the new rooms open?

## Why it came up

Yusuf asked in September 2025: kit storage is always short, and demolition costs money.

## Answer

No. The asbestos survey of 14 October 2025 found asbestos cement roof sheets ([[asbestos-survey-report-2025]]). Keeping the block means managing that roof for years, and it sits on the footprint the new building needs. The new design includes a store for goals and nets instead ([[changing-rooms-design-v2-2026-05]]). Demolition with licensed asbestos removal is £9,500 in the quote.

Answered at the October 2025 board; Yusuf agreed once he saw the store on the plan.
