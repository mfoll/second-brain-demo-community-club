# First Lease Meeting with Council Estates, 12 November 2025

Marek and I met Graham Sutcliffe at the council offices on Wednesday 12 November 2025 to ask for a lease long enough for a capital grant.

## What we asked

- A new lease of at least 30 years, so a funder's 25 years would still be met if the grant took two years to land.
- The pitches brought into the lease, rather than hired season by season.

## What Graham said

- The council is in principle happy to grant longer leases to established community groups; Millrace Lane is a good candidate.
- Anything over 10 years goes to cabinet, which means a report and about three months.
- The council would expect the club to take on full repair of all buildings and the pitches, including drainage.
- He asked about the club's legal status; an unincorporated club would sign through holding trustees.

## Our reaction

Length was not a problem; repairs were. Marek pointed out the drainage on pitch 2 and asked for time to cost it. We agreed to come back in the new year with Gareth's log and a proposal. That became eight months of heads of terms ([[lease-negotiation-notes]], [[repairing-obligations]]).
