# How Often Should Volunteers Refresh Safeguarding Training?

## Question

The 2022 policy required safeguarding training but said nothing about refreshing it. How often should volunteers renew, and what happens if they lapse?

## Current Answer

Every three years, matching the Northmere FA requirement ([[northmere-fa-safeguarding-training-requirements]]) and Northmere Athletics guidance ([[northmere-athletics-welfare-guidance]]). Background checks follow the governing bodies' renewal rules. Clubfold reminds the volunteer and the welfare officers 60 days before expiry. Anyone not current steps back from junior sessions until they are; they can still do roles without junior contact.

Written into the draft policy ([[safeguarding-policy-draft-2026-09]]) and agreed in principle by the board in September 2026.

## Why

When the review started, 13 of 35 volunteers were not fully current, almost all because three-year training had expired with nobody noticing. The rule is less about the training content than about having a date someone checks.
