# LED lamp

## Summary

Explains LED lamps: lights using light-emitting diodes, much more efficient than incandescent and fluorescent lamps, and lasting several times longer.

## Why It Matters

Marek sent it round after the [[pavilion-energy-audit-visit-2026-02-26]] to make the case for replacing the changing-room fluorescent tubes. It supports the cheapest item on the [[pavilion-energy-ideas-list]] and raises the bigger question of LED floodlight heads, since floodlights are about a third of our electricity.

## Caveats

Efficiency figures are for the best lamps; ours will be ordinary ones. Floodlight replacement needs specialist advice, not this article. Article last edited 2026-08-11 when checked; licence CC BY-SA 4.0.
