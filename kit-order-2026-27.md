# Kit Order 2026-27

Yusuf Demir's kit order for the 2026-27 season, placed 30 July 2026.

- New match kits for the girls' under-14s and the under-10s; replacement shirts for three other teams.
- 18 new size-4 and size-3 balls, two sets of pop-up goals for the mini pitches, cones and bibs.
- Total £2,640: £1,500 covered by the Coyle Motors kit sponsorship, the rest from the season budget.
- Delivered 24 August, in time for the launch meeting.

Why it matters: the girls' under-14s could not have entered the league in their own kit without it. Yusuf keeps the full order and size list.
