# How to Run a Board Meeting

The monthly board cycle as we run it since 2023. For the chair and secretary; written so a new chair can follow it from the first meeting.

## Inputs

- Date: second Tuesday of the month, 19:30, pavilion meeting room (video link for anyone who cannot come). No meeting in August.
- Standing agenda: apologies and declarations of interest; minutes and actions; finance; welfare (the welfare officer's report, never with names); sections; projects; any other business.

## Steps

1. **Two weeks before (Monday):** chair and secretary agree the agenda. Anyone wanting a decision tells the secretary now.
2. **One week before (Tuesday):** secretary sends the agenda and all papers. A decision without a paper waits a month ([[board-papers-a-week-before-meetings]]).
3. **On the night:** check quorum (five voting members; the welfare officer does not count). Background on why quorum matters: [[wikipedia-quorum]].
4. Take declarations of interest and record them ([[conflict-of-interest-register]]). Anyone conflicted leaves the room for the item; if it is the chair, the vice-chair takes it.
5. For each decision: paper presented, questions, discussion, a clear proposal, vote by show of hands. The chair votes only to break a tie.
6. If a member asks for dissent to be minuted, the secretary writes the wording and reads it back before moving on ([[minuted-dissent]]).
7. Confidential items (bursaries, welfare) are taken last, minuted only as "confidential item noted".
8. Finish by 22:00. Anything not reached goes first next month.

## After

- Secretary sends draft minutes within a week; to any dissenting member first.
- Actions go in the draft minutes with a name and a date.
- Decisions that changed something go in the [[board-decision-log]].
- Approved minutes are filed in the [[board-minutes-archive]].

## Urgent decisions

Between meetings, the chair may put a decision to the board by email. It needs replies from a quorum and a majority, and is reported at the next meeting.
