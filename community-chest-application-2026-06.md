# Draymoor Community Chest Application, June 2026

The club's application to the Draymoor Community Chest, the council's small-grants scheme, for £15,000 towards match funding for the new changing rooms. Submitted 15 June 2026.

## Summary

- Asked for £15,000, the scheme's maximum for capital projects.
- Short form: project summary, community benefit, other funding, timetable.
- Made clear that the money is match funding and would be returned if the Lanternfield grant is not awarded.
- Anita Venkatesh read a draft and suggested leading with the girls' section and the accessible room.

## Why It Matters

This was the second leg of the [[match-funding]], and it came through on 20 August 2026 ([[community-chest-award-2026-08-20]]). Without it the appeal target would have been £38,000, which nobody on the board thought we could raise ([[match-funding-plan-2026-03]]).
