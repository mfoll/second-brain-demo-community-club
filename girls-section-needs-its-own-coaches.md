# Girls Section Needs Its Own Coaches

## Stance

The girls' section will only last if it grows its own coaches, ideally women and former players, rather than borrowing parents from boys' teams for a season at a time.

## Why I Think This

- Leanne's taster day worked partly because the coaches were women from the adult team ([[girls-football-taster-day-2025-06-21]]).
- The girls' under-14s head coach is a parent who has agreed to one season. When he steps back, the team needs someone who is staying.
- Older girls who help coach younger ones are the coaches of 2030. [[ellie-tranter]] is the proof: a former junior now assisting and on a course.

## What Would Change My Mind

If a borrowed coach stayed three seasons and the girls kept coming back, I would care less who the coach is and more that they stay.

## Implications

Spend the Northmere FA funded places on women and young leaders first. Ask the adult women's team every season.
