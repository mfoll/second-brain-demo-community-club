# Track and field

English Wikipedia article, last edited 11 October 2026. Licence CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Summary

The article describes track and field as a sport of running, jumping and throwing contests, named after the running track and the grass field where the events take place. It places track and field within the wider sport of athletics, alongside road running, cross country and race walking.

## Why It Matters

I read it in June 2025 while Sheila, Luke and I were working out the Oakreach swap, because I did not know enough about athletics to argue for it. It made the case simply: the sport is built around a track, and a squad that trains on a football pitch is missing half of it. That is what the Tuesday slot gives our juniors ([[oakreach-track-agreement]], [[school-partnership]]).
