# Kwabena Osei

Athletics coach (sprints) and the club coach who runs the Wednesday after-school athletics club at Oakreach School. Qualified through Northmere Athletics coach education.

## Role

- Coaches sprints with the junior squad on Tuesday evenings at the Oakreach track.
- Runs the free Wednesday after-school club for about 25 Oakreach pupils in term time, with one other club coach. This is our side of the track swap ([[oakreach-track-agreement]]).

## How we work together

Kwabena is calm, punctual and keeps a careful register. He is the reason the school values the arrangement; Luke Garvey has said as much. He has also said, fairly, that two hours every Wednesday is a lot to give for free if the school starts charging us for Tuesdays.
