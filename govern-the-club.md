# Govern The Club

How Fernhollow Sports and Youth Club is run: the board of nine, its monthly meetings and decisions, the constitution, the AGM, and my own succession as chair. It has no end date. Somebody always has to answer for the board, and from June 2027 it will not be me.

## Scope

- The board: meetings on the second Tuesday of each month at 19:30 in the pavilion meeting room, quorum five, papers, minutes and the decision record ([[how-to-run-a-board-meeting]], [[board-minutes-archive]]).
- The constitution and anything that changes it ([[fernhollow-constitution-2025]], [[constitution-review-2025]]).
- The AGM each June, officer elections and the members' voice in between.
- Board composition, skills and succession: [[board-skills-audit]], [[chair-succession-plan]], [[who-will-take-over-as-treasurer]].
- The club's legal form ([[club-incorporation-review]]).
- Not here: money decisions sit under [[manage-club-money]] even when the board takes them; safeguarding sits under [[keep-children-safe]]; the changing-rooms build under [[look-after-the-ground]]. The board meeting Events link out to them.

## Working Notes

I write these as handover notes, the way I did on the ward: what is true now, what is waiting, who to ring. Board disagreements go in as the minutes record them, both sides' reasons and no personal remarks. Board papers go out a week ahead ([[board-papers-a-week-before-meetings]]); that habit has done more for our decisions than anything in the constitution.
