# Pitch Condition Log 2025-26

Gareth's season log of pitch inspections and call-offs, a shared spreadsheet with one row per Saturday and Sunday.

## Summary

- 33 match weekends logged from September 2025 to May 2026.
- Matches called off for waterlogging: 14 on pitch 2, 4 on pitch 1, none on the mini pitches.
- Pitch 2 bottom end under standing water after more than about 15 mm of rain in a day; it drains slowly over two to three days.
- Goalmouths renovated in May; pitch 2 needed extra top dressing.

## Why It Matters

Fourteen lost fixtures on one pitch is a lot of disappointed juniors and rearranged fixtures for Martin. The log is the evidence we used with Graham Sutcliffe to keep the main drains with the council in the new lease ([[repairing-obligations]], [[should-the-club-take-on-pitch-drainage-under-the-new-lease]]).
