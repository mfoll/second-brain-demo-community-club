# Should Under-18 Helpers Count in Supervision Ratios?

## Question

Several 16- and 17-year-old athletes and players help with younger groups. Do they count as one of the adults in supervision ratios or the [[two-adult-rule]]?

## Current Answer

No. Under-18 helpers are welcome, do the club's own induction and follow the codes of conduct, but they never count towards ratios, never act as the second adult, and are never left in charge of a group. Volunteers aged 18 and over who hold current training and a check, such as [[ellie-tranter]] at 19, count as adults like anyone else.

Written into the draft policy ([[safeguarding-policy-draft-2026-09]]) and confirmed by [[rebecca-thorne]] as consistent with the [[northmere-fa-safeguarding-template]].

## Why

Young helpers are themselves under the club's duty of care. Asking a 16-year-old to be the second adult outside a changing room puts them in a position they should not be in.

## Practical Effect

The Saturday changing-room rota ([[run-the-saturday-changing-room-rota]]) needed two more adult door slots a week, which [[nisha-raghavan]] filled from one-off volunteers.
