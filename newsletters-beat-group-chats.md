# Newsletters Beat Group Chats

## Stance

For anything that is not urgent, a monthly newsletter is better than a group chat. Chats are fine for "training is off tonight" and terrible for everything else.

## Why I Think This

- A chat message is gone in an hour; a newsletter can be found again.
- The April newsletter explained the fee rise properly, with the bursary, and drew thanks. The same explanation in the old club-wide chat in 2025 drew 60 replies and an argument.
- Chats put volunteers on call all evening. On a ward we had handover at fixed times for good reason. A newsletter is a handover ([[volunteer-burnout]]).
- 58% open rate is better than I expected, and better than the share of people who read past the first screen of a busy chat.

## What Would Change My Mind

Open rates falling below 40%, or evidence that families who do not read email are missing important news. Some families do not use email much; for them the team channel carries a one-line pointer.

## Implications

Keep chats for match-day logistics only. Anything official goes in Clubfold notices and the newsletter.
