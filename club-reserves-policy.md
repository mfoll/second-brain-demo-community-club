# Club Reserves Policy

## Summary

One-page policy adopted by the board on 14 April 2026, drafted by [[claire-whitlow]] after the examiner's recommendation ([[independent-examiner-report-2025]]).

## Key Details

- **Target:** unrestricted reserves equal to three months' running costs, about £10,500 on the 2026-27 budget.
- **Purpose:** to cover a bad season (lost tournament, a council price rise), urgent repairs to the pavilion, and cash flow in July and August before fees arrive.
- **Designated funds:** the board may designate reserves for a capital project by resolution, but must then plan how to return to target.
- **Review:** each January with the budget.

## Why It Matters

A month after adoption the board designated £25,000 for the changing rooms ([[board-meeting-2026-05-12]]), which takes reserves to about £6,000, below target. The policy allowed it, and also required a rebuild plan, which is the open question in [[how-much-reserve-should-the-club-keep]].
