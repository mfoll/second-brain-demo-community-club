# Clubfold Payment Settings

## Summary

[[claire-whitlow]]'s note of how fees are collected through Clubfold, written when the club switched on instalments in September 2025 and updated in March 2026 for the new fees.

## Key Details

- Payment options for families: one card payment in September, or card instalments (three or ten).
- Card fee charged to the club: 1.9% plus 20p per payment. On a £165 fee in ten instalments that is about £5.10 per child.
- Failed card payments are retried after three and seven days, then flagged to the treasurer.
- Direct debit option hidden since February 2026 ([[direct-debit-pause-2026-01-27]]).
- Bursary adjustments use a restricted fee code ([[bursary-awards-tracker]]).
- Payouts to the club bank account weekly, on Mondays.

## Why It Matters

The comparison point for [[is-direct-debit-worth-the-bank-charges]], and what the next treasurer needs to know first.
