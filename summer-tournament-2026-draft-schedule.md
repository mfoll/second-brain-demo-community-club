# Summer Tournament 2026 Draft Schedule

First draft of the tournament schedule from the [[tournament-planning-meeting-2026-03-24]], before entries closed. Superseded by the final schedule Sam published on 20 June 2026; kept for the reasoning. See [[summer-tournament-2026]].

- Planned for 56 teams in groups of four; reduced to 52 when the pitch plan showed under-10 games would fall below 10 minutes.
- Saturday 09:00 to 16:00, Sunday 09:00 to 17:00. The final schedule finished Sunday at 16:00 to leave time to clear the car park.
- No wet-weather alternative. That gap is now covered by the [[wet-weather-schedule]].
