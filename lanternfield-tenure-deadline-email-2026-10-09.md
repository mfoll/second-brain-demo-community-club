# Email from Esther Lowell on Tenure Evidence, 9 October 2026

Esther Lowell's reply to my email of 18 September telling the trust about the cabinet deferral.

## Summary

- The application is valid and will be assessed in round 2.
- The trust cannot make an offer without evidence of at least 25 years' tenure. It will accept either a signed lease or a cabinet resolution committing the council to grant one, with the lease signed before the first payment.
- The club has until Tuesday 15 December 2026 to provide that evidence. If it is not received, the application will be carried forward to round 1 of 2027 (decisions July 2027) without needing to be resubmitted, though the trust may ask for updated figures.
- She thanked us for telling her early.

## Why It Matters

This sets the real deadline for [[pitch-lease-extension]]. Cabinet meets on 19 November, which leaves about four weeks. Rolling to 2027 is not fatal but would push the decision past the Hutchins & Kerr fixed-price date of 31 March 2027 ([[what-happens-if-the-lease-is-not-evidenced-by-15-december]]).
