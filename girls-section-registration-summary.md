# Girls Section Registration Summary

Aggregate counts for the girls' section from Clubfold, 15 October 2026. No individual details.

- Girls' under-11s: 17 registered (ages 8 to 11).
- Girls' under-14s: 14 registered (ages 11 to 14).
- Total 31. Of these, 9 joined after the [[members-survey-2025]] identified families with interested daughters, 12 came from the [[girls-football-taster-day-2025-06-21]], and 10 by word of mouth.
- Retention from the first season: 12 of the 14 founding girls are still playing.
- Bursaries: some girls' section families receive help from the hardship bursary; numbers are reported only in the bursary totals.

Why it matters: the evidence that the section is growing and holding on to players, and the base line for [[how-do-we-keep-girls-playing-past-under-14]].
