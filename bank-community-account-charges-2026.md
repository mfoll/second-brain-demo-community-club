# Bank Community Account Charges 2026

## Summary

Letter from the club's bank, dated 12 January 2026, received 15 January, announcing charges on community accounts from 1 April 2026.

## Key Details

- Monthly account fee £6, from April 2026 (previously free).
- 40p per direct debit collected; 30p per electronic payment out over 30 a month.
- Cash deposits over £1,000 a month: 70p per £100.

For the club: about £72 a year in account fees, about £180 a year for 38 families on monthly direct debit, and some cash charges in tournament months. About £250 to £300 a year in total.
