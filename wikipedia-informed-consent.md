# Informed consent

## Summary

The English Wikipedia article on informed consent. The lead presents it as an applied ethics principle: a person needs enough information and understanding before deciding to accept a risk, including the benefits, the alternatives and the right to refuse. It notes that the principle reaches beyond medical treatment, for example to research, to disclosing someone's personal information and to taking part in high-risk recreation. Last edited 14 October 2026 at the time of the record.

## Why It Matters

I sent the link to [[linh-pham]] and [[aoife-brennan]] in May 2026 while they were drafting the photo guidance. Informed consent was part of my working life for 38 years, and the same elements carried over well to photos of juniors: say exactly where an image may appear, make "no" as easy as "yes", ask again each season and let people change their minds. Those four points became [[consent-for-photography]] and the wording of the Clubfold question ([[photo-consent-form-2026]]).

## Caveats

The article is mainly about health care and research. Photo consent in a club is a much lighter matter, given by parents on behalf of children; the parallel is ours.
