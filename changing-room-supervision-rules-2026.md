# Changing Room Supervision Rules 2026

The interim rules for the shared 1974 changing rooms at Millrace Lane, agreed by [[aoife-brennan]], [[marek-zielinski]] and me in August 2026, noted by the board, and in force from 5 September 2026. Appendix C of the 2026 policy draft.

## The Rules

1. **Staggered slots.** Each junior team has a 20-minute changing slot before and after its game, published with the rota.
2. **No adult changes while juniors are present.** Adults wait in the corridor, or arrive changed.
3. **Two adults outside the door** during junior slots. Never one adult inside with juniors. If a child needs help, both adults go in or the door is propped open.
4. **No phones in the changing room**, for anyone, at any time.
5. **Officials change in the meeting room**, locked for the purpose.
6. **Juniors keep their slot** if games overrun; adults wait.
7. **If only one door adult is available**, the junior team changes at home or after the adults have gone.

## Why It Matters

The building cannot separate adults from juniors ([[changing-room-observation-2026-05-16]]). These rules make the [[two-adult-rule]] workable until the [[new-changing-rooms]] open. [[rebecca-thorne]] judged them proportionate ([[email-from-rebecca-thorne-on-changing-rooms-2026-06]]).

## Caveats

Interim. They depend on fixture times and on finding four door adults a Saturday.
