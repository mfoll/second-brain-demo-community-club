# Accessible toilet

English Wikipedia article, last edited 7 October 2026. Licence CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Summary

The article explains that accessible toilets are designed for people with physical disabilities and are also useful to people with reduced mobility. It lists measures such as a higher bowl, more space, grab bars to help transfer, and extra room for a carer, and notes that some countries set accessibility requirements for public toilets.

## Why It Matters

Our first design had an accessible WC and nothing more ([[changing-rooms-design-brief-2025]]). When we read this in May 2026, before Neil's revision, the point about room for a carer made us ask who would actually use the room: a masters athlete after a hip operation, a visiting player in a wheelchair, a parent helping a child with a disability. A WC alone does not let any of them change. Revision C has a full accessible changing room with a level shower ([[changing-rooms-design-v2-2026-05]], [[inclusion-and-access]]).

## Caveats

Dimensions and fittings follow the architect and building regulations, not this article.
