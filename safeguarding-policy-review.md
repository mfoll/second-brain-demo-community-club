# Safeguarding Policy Review

Replacing the club's 2022 safeguarding policy with a revised policy that matches how the club actually runs now: more girls' teams, a young adult volunteer coaching, a membership platform, social media, and a changing-room building that does not separate adults from juniors.

## Outcome

The 2022 safeguarding policy replaced by a revised policy adopted by the board on 17 November 2026 and briefed to all 35 volunteers.

## Current State

- Draft version 2 circulated by [[aoife-brennan]] on 28 September 2026 ([[safeguarding-policy-draft-2026-09]]). Board comments close on 3 November.
- [[rebecca-thorne]] at Northmere FA has reviewed the draft against the [[northmere-fa-safeguarding-template]]; two minor changes requested on reporting timescales.
- Three child projects: photo guidance done, training and changing-room rules still running.
- Adoption is scheduled for the board meeting on 17 November 2026; briefing sessions pencilled for 21 and 25 November.

## Scope and Boundaries

Policy, roles, the reporting route, codes of conduct, training expectations, photos and online, changing rooms, travel and away fixtures, and the link to the new building. **No cases.** Anything that looks like a concern goes straight to Aoife through [[report-a-welfare-concern]] and never into these notes, minutes or the review documents.

## Working Context

Aoife started the review on 20 April 2026 ([[safeguarding-review-kickoff-2026-04-20]]) with terms of reference agreed by the board ([[safeguarding-review-terms-of-reference-2026]]). Her gap list ([[safeguarding-review-gap-list]]) found eleven gaps. The biggest were:

1. Photos and social media were covered by one paragraph written before the club had an Instagram account.
2. Training expectations did not say how often refreshers are needed or what happens if someone lapses.
3. The policy assumed separate changing for adults and juniors, which the 1974 pavilion cannot give. On Saturdays adult and junior sessions overlap ([[changing-room-observation-2026-05-16]]).
4. Nothing on volunteers aged 18-21 who were juniors here until recently, such as [[ellie-tranter]].

The changing-room finding fed straight into the new building: the design was revised in May 2026 to give two separate junior rooms and an officials' room ([[changing-room-design-requirements]], [[changing-rooms-design-and-quotes]]), and the grant application cites it as a safeguarding benefit ([[changing-rooms-grant-submission-2026-09-30]]).
