# Colin Brightwell

Chair of the club from 2008 to 2019, and my informal mentor since I took over. Still comes to the summer tournament and most home games of the adult men's team.

## How we work together

We meet for coffee two or three times a year, or I ring him when I am stuck. He handed over to me in about three weeks in 2019, and we both say now that it was not long enough. He knows the history: why the 1998 constitution said what it did, who at the council remembers the club, how the 1974 pavilion was paid for.

- His view on co-chairs: he is sceptical, but says the club is bigger than when he chaired and the paperwork has trebled.
