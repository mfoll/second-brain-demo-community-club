# Pledge Board Paper July 2025

The two-page paper I wrote with [[donovan-clarke]] proposing the family volunteer pledge, circulated on 8 July 2025 for the board on 15 July.

## Summary

The paper argued that the rota was failing because the same 20 or so families did all the volunteering, and that a clear expectation of every family would spread the load. It proposed three sessions per junior family per season, a £30 opt-out added to fees, and a review in January.

## Key Details

- Problem evidence: three Saturdays without a tea hut in spring 2025; about 140 slots a month; an estimated 20 families doing most of them.
- Arithmetic: about 150 families times three sessions is 450 sessions a season, against roughly 1,000 slots, "enough to change the culture".
- Opt-out justified as covering the cost of buying in help, though no costing was given.
- Risks listed: resentment, administration. Not listed: families working weekends, informal help drying up.

## Why It Matters

Worth reading alongside [[pledge-lessons-learned]]. The risks we did not list are the ones that came true.
