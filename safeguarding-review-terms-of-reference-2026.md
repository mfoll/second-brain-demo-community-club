# Safeguarding Review Terms of Reference 2026

One-page terms of reference for the safeguarding policy review, drafted by [[aoife-brennan]] and approved by the board by email on 14 April 2026.

## Key Details

- **Purpose:** bring the 2022 policy up to date with how the club runs now, and with the Northmere FA template 2025.2.
- **Lead:** the welfare officer. Members: deputy welfare officer, chair, photography volunteer for the photo strand, facilities lead consulted on changing rooms.
- **Scope:** policy, roles, reporting, safer recruitment, codes of conduct, supervision, changing rooms, photos and online, training, athletics.
- **Out of scope:** any individual case, past or present.
- **Outputs:** a revised policy for board adoption by the end of 2026; separate guidance documents where useful.
- **Consultation:** coaches and team managers on drafts; Northmere FA and Northmere Athletics on the final draft.
- **Records:** all documents in the safeguarding folder on the shared drive.

## Why It Matters

Sets the boundaries of the [[safeguarding-policy-review]], including the explicit exclusion of cases that this vault follows too.
