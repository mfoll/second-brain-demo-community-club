# How do we handle tournament parking in 2027?

## Question

What stewarded parking plan will satisfy the council and the residents for the 2027 summer tournament, and can we staff it?

## Why It Matters

The council has made a parking plan a condition of the 2027 tournament ([[council-event-conditions-letter-2026-08]]). The tournament netted £8,900 in 2026, £5,000 of it for the appeal ([[summer-tournament-2026-debrief]]).

## Ideas so far

- Overflow parking at Oakreach School (would need the school's agreement, awkward while the lettings review is on) or the church car park Imran mentioned.
- Staggered arrival times by age group, Saturday for the younger groups and Sunday for the older ones.
- Six marshals at peak times, which means recruiting from visiting clubs as well as our own.
- A drop-off zone and a letter to residents two weeks ahead.
- Capping entries at 44 teams instead of 52.

## Current Answer

None yet. What a stewarded plan involves is in [[stewarded-parking-plan]]; Sam and I will draft ours in November and share it with Imran before Christmas. Capping entries is the fallback if we cannot find marshals.
