# Asbestos

English Wikipedia article, last edited 21 October 2026. Licence CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Summary

The article introduces asbestos as a group of naturally occurring fibrous silicate minerals, used for thousands of years to make flexible fire-resistant products such as fireproof fabric, and states plainly that it is toxic.

## Why It Matters

Marek sent the link round the board the day after the survey found asbestos cement sheets on the changing block roof in October 2025 ([[asbestos-survey-report-2025]]), because two members wanted to strip the roof themselves to save money. It ended that idea. It is also why the old block cannot become a store ([[can-the-old-prefab-be-reused-as-storage]]) and why removal is a named, licensed subcontract in the quote ([[hutchins-and-kerr-changing-rooms-quote]]).
