# Occupational burnout

## Summary

The English Wikipedia article on occupational burnout. The lead describes it as a work-related state arising from chronic workplace stress that has not been managed, with three recognised features: exhaustion or depleted energy, growing mental distance or cynicism about the work, and a reduced sense of effectiveness. Last edited 22 October 2026 at the time of the record.

## Why It Matters

I looked it up in December 2025 after Nisha's hours estimate showed seven people doing more than half the volunteer hours ([[volunteer-hours-estimate-2025]]). The three features matched what I had seen on wards for years and what I was starting to see in a few volunteers: tiredness, short replies, and "I don't know why I bother". It gave me words for [[volunteer-burnout]], though volunteering is not employment and I am careful not to diagnose anyone.

## Caveats

The article is about paid work. It says nothing about volunteers or clubs; the link to our situation is my own.
