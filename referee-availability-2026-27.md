# Referee Availability 2026-27

Martin Goss's list of club referees for junior home games, 1 September 2026.

- 7 qualified referees available for under-12 and younger home games: 3 most Saturdays, 4 occasional.
- 2 parents on the Northmere FA referee course this autumn; one 16-year-old former player interested for 2027.
- Saturday demand: up to 6 home games needing a club referee on a busy morning. On three Saturdays before half-term a coach refereed his own team's game.

Why it matters: the reason we cannot add more Saturday home slots, and a recurring gap in the [[season-2026-27-plan]].
