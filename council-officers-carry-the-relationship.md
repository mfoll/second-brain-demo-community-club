# Council officers carry the relationship, cabinet carries the timetable

## Stance

Our real relationship with the council is with two or three officers. They get things right and they are on our side within the rules. But officers cannot control when cabinet decides, and cabinet does not plan round a club's grant deadlines.

## Scope

Draymoor Borough Council and decisions that need cabinet (leases over 10 years, big grants). Day-to-day matters such as pitch hire and events are officer decisions and run fine.

## Why I Think This

- Graham and Anita delivered everything they promised: heads of terms, a supportive report, the Community Chest advice.
- Cabinet deferred on 17 September for a reason that had nothing to do with us ([[council-cabinet-deferral-2026-09-17]]).
- Rhys's intervention got a date; nothing an officer could have done would have.

## What Would Change My Mind

If the lease goes through smoothly on 19 November, I will think the deferral was a one-off.

## Implications

- Build in at least one cabinet cycle of slack for any decision we need.
- Keep the ward councillor informed early, not just when we need help.
- Never quote officers publicly in a way that puts them in a difficult position.
