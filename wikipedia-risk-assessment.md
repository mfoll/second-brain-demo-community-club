# Risk assessment

## Summary

Describes risk assessment as a process of identifying hazards that could harm people, assets or the environment, judging how likely they are and how bad the consequences would be, and deciding what actions reduce them.

## Why It Matters

Sam and I used the structure (hazard, likelihood, consequence, control) for the [[tournament-risk-assessment-2026]]. The gaps the debrief found, parking and wet weather, were hazards we had not listed at all, which is the step the article puts first.

## Caveats
