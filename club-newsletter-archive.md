# Club Newsletter Archive

Pointer to every issue of the monthly club newsletter, kept in Clubfold's communications module with open-rate statistics.

Issues so far: April to October 2026, seven in all. Open rates: 64%, 59%, 57%, 61%, 52% (August), 56%, 58%. August is always low. Clicks are mostly on fixtures and the volunteer role of the month.

The newsletter is sent to all member households and adult members. Issues are not posted publicly because they sometimes mention team news that only members should see. Planning for future issues is in [[newsletter-content-calendar]]; how to send one is in [[send-monthly-newsletter]].
