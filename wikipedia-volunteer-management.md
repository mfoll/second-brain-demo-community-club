# Volunteer management

## Summary

The English Wikipedia article on volunteer management, which it also calls volunteer engagement, coordination or administration. The lead defines it as a systematic process of working with and through volunteers to meet an organisation's aims, and the article treats recruitment, role design, induction, supervision, recognition and retention as parts of one cycle rather than separate jobs. Last edited 31 August 2025 at the time of the record.

## Why It Matters

[[nisha-raghavan]] sent me the link in January 2026 when she was writing her options paper ([[role-based-recruitment-proposal-2026-01]]). What I took from it was the idea of a cycle: we had spent years on recruitment appeals and almost nothing on role design or keeping people. That framing is behind [[role-based-volunteering]] and the induction steps in [[onboard-a-new-volunteer]].

## Caveats

General and written mainly with larger organisations and paid volunteer managers in mind. Nothing in it is specific to sports clubs.
