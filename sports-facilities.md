# Sports Facilities

Retrieval surface for anything about the physical places we play and train: Millrace Lane, the pavilion, the changing rooms, the Oakreach track, and what we have read about how other grassroots clubs build and run them.

## Threads worth knowing

- Facilities and safeguarding are tied together here. The 1974 block is one shared space, so supervision rules ([[changing-room-supervision-rules]]) carry the load until the new rooms exist. The design revision in May 2026 was driven by that.
- Facilities and access: the accessible room in the new design is the first step-free changing space the club will have had. See [[inclusion-and-access]].
- Facilities and money: every building decision sits on [[match-funding]] and on the lease ([[security-of-tenure]]).
