# Club Governance

How a members' club makes and records decisions: the board, officers, the constitution, the AGM, quorum, conflicts of interest, dissent and succession. This Topic gathers reading and ideas that cut across Projects; the work itself lives under [[govern-the-club]].

## Why it matters to us

We are nine volunteers running a £48,000 club that is about to sign a 30-year lease and a building contract. None of us trained in this. Good governance for us is mostly plain habits: papers a week early, decisions written down with the reasons, disagreements minuted fairly, and nobody holding a job so long the club cannot manage without them.
