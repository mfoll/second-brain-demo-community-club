# Summer Tournament 2026 Debrief

Debrief of the 2026 summer tournament, Tuesday 21 July 2026, 19:30 in the pavilion meeting room. Present: Sam Okonjo (organiser), Hazel Pennock, Helen Marsh (catering), Martin Goss (referees), Nisha Raghavan (volunteer rota), Claire Whitlow (treasurer, for the money item), Linh Pham. Apologies: Marek Zielinski.

## At a Glance

52 teams over two days, no serious injuries, a wet Sunday, one formal complaint about parking, and £8,900 net. £5,000 goes to the changing rooms appeal as agreed in May. The council now wants a stewarded parking plan before it will agree a 2027 date.

## What happened

- **Entries.** 52 teams from under-7 to under-14 (44 in 2025), 21 from outside Draymoor. We capped at 52 in March and turned away six late entries.
- **Saturday 4 July.** Under-7 to under-10 festivals on the three mini pitches and pitch 2 split in half. Dry and warm. Busiest parking of the weekend: cars on the verges of Millrace Lane and Weir Close from about 09:30.
- **Sunday 5 July.** Under-11 to under-14 competition. Heavy rain from about 11:00. Pitch 2 closed after 14:00 because of standing water by the goalmouths; the under-13 and under-14 finals were played on pitch 1 as 8-minute halves. Two teams left before their last group games. The pavilion could not shelter everyone; the gazebos from the tea hut helped.
- **First aid.** Booked cover both days; 11 minor treatments, nobody sent to hospital. Details stay with the first aid provider's log.
- **Volunteers.** 58 of 64 slots filled through the Clubfold rota. The gaps were all Sunday afternoon car park and litter slots, the ones that mattered most.

## Money

Income £10,310: entries 52 × £60 = £3,120, tea hut and barbecue £5,350, raffle and tombola £1,240, stall pitches £600. Costs £1,410: portable toilets £420, medals and trophies £510, first aid cover £240, referees' expenses £240. Net £8,900. Claire confirmed the figures from [[summer-tournament-2026-budget]].

Decision: £5,000 to the [[match-funding-appeal]] as the board agreed on 12 May, to be transferred when Claire closes the accounts on 24 July; £3,900 to general funds. Sunday tea hut takings were about £700 below Saturday's because of the rain.

## Parking complaint

Imran Sheikh, chair of the Fernhollow Residents' Association, wrote to the council on 6 July about verge parking and a blocked driveway on Weir Close on the Saturday ([[residents-parking-complaint-2026-07]]). Sam had not seen the complaint before it reached the council. We agreed it was fair: we had two volunteers at the gate and no plan beyond that.

## Lessons

1. Parking needs a written plan with stewards, an overflow arrangement and a message to visiting teams in advance. Sam to draft ([[stewarded-parking-plan]]).
2. Sunday needs a wet-weather schedule agreed beforehand: which games shorten, which pitch closes first, who decides.
3. The last car park slots go unfilled because they are the least appealing. Next year, pair them with a free barbecue voucher and fill them first.
4. 52 teams is the most the site can take; more would mean less play per team and worse parking ([[tournament-should-stay-community-sized]]).
5. Photos went well under the draft photo guidance; Linh had no consent problems.

## Actions

- Hazel: reply to the council and the residents' association, acknowledging the complaint, this week.
- Martin: write a wet-weather fixture rule into the tournament procedure ([[run-tournament-day]]).
- Claire: close the accounts by 24 July.
