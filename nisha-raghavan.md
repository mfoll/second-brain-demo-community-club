# Nisha Raghavan

Volunteer coordinator since September 2024. Designed the role-based recruitment that replaced the family pledge, and runs the Clubfold rota day to day.

## Role

- Publishes the weekly rota in Clubfold every Sunday and handles swaps.
- Wrote the [[volunteer-role-descriptions]] and the January 2026 paper that persuaded the board ([[role-based-recruitment-proposal-2026-01]]).
- Leads the [[volunteer-recruitment-drive-2026]].
- Not a board member; attends board meetings for volunteer items.

## How We Work Together

Nisha works in HR for a logistics firm and thinks in roles, hours and handovers. She is the reason the volunteer side is organised at all. She is honest when something is not working: it was her January numbers that ended the compulsory pledge, even though she had been the one chasing families for it.

We speak most Sunday evenings when the rota goes out. She prefers a quick message to a meeting. I try not to add to her load; she does about six hours a week for the club, and I have asked her to tell me if that grows.

## Current Threads

- Seven of twelve new regular volunteers recruited ([[recruitment-drive-progress]]).
- Training a second rota admin ([[who-takes-over-the-clubfold-rota-admin]]).
- Planning the November thank-you evening with [[helen-marsh]].

## Background With the Club

Nisha first helped as a one-off tea hut volunteer in 2023 when her son joined the under-9s, and took on the coordinator role when the previous holder stepped down in summer 2024. She inherited the group-chat rota and was the first to say it could not go on. The [[volunteer-rota-overhaul]] was largely her design; I chaired the decisions and wrote the board papers.
