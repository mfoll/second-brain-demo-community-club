# Direct Debit Collection

Letting families pay fees in monthly instalments by direct debit instead of one payment in September.

## Outcome

Monthly fee instalments by direct debit; paused on 27 January 2026 until the bank's community account charges are compared.

## Current State

- Paused since 27 January 2026 ([[direct-debit-pause-2026-01-27]]).
- 38 families signed up for the trial in autumn 2025. They were moved back to card instalments through Clubfold, which costs more per payment but has no monthly charge.
- The bank announced charges on community accounts from April 2026: £6 a month plus 40p per direct debit collected ([[bank-community-account-charges-2026]]).

## Resume Condition

Resume when [[claire-whitlow]] has compared at least two other community accounts and the cost per instalment is below Clubfold's card fee. Due before registrations open in June 2027.

## Working Context

Instalments matter because of the 23 families who asked for payment plans in 2025-26 ([[payment-plan-requests-2025-26]]). [[ravinder-sahota]] sees monthly payment as part of keeping the club affordable, so he wants this back. Claire's worry is the work of chasing failed collections, which fell on her during the trial (six failed in December alone).
