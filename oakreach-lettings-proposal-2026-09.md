# Oakreach Lettings Proposal, 22 September 2026

Email from Priya Lal, business manager at Oakreach School, to me and Sheila Mortimer.

## Summary

- The school is reviewing all lettings as part of its budget plan for 2026-27.
- The club's Tuesday track use appears in the lettings diary at no charge. The standard community lettings rate for the track with floodlights is £45 an hour.
- Proposal: charge the standard rate from January 2027; the Wednesday after-school club to continue as now.
- Invitation to discuss.

## Why It Matters

This is what put the swap at risk. At about 70 hours a year it would cost about £3,150, which the 2026-27 budget does not have ([[club-budget-2026-27]]). The email did not mention the after-school club's value, which is the gap our reply addressed. It led to the meeting on 6 October ([[oakreach-lettings-meeting-2026-10-06]]).
