# Separate junior changing is worth the extra cost

## Stance

> [!info]
> The extra £21,000 for two junior rooms, an officials' room and a proper accessible room is the best money in the whole project. If we had to cut, I would cut almost anything else first.

## Scope

The new changing rooms at Millrace Lane. I am not saying every club needs this layout; a club with no adult teams might not.

## Why I Think This

- Thirty-eight years of nursing taught me that you design out the situations you do not want to manage. Rules on a door work only while there are enough people to stand by the door.
- Our interim rules ([[changing-room-supervision-rules]]) need two adults free every Saturday morning. Some weeks we struggle to find them.
- The girls' section is growing (31 players in October 2026) and girls deserve their own space at the same time as the boys.
- Young referees changing in the car park is not acceptable, and losing referees costs the whole league.
- The trust scores safeguarding and access as reasons for a project ([[lanternfield-pre-application-call-2026-03-05]]). The revision made the application stronger, not just dearer.

## What Would Change My Mind

Evidence that the extra rooms would lose us the grant on value for money, or a funding gap so large the building would not happen at all. Neither has happened.

## Implications

Defend the layout in any value-engineering discussion ([[value-engineering]]). If costs rise, cut finishes and fittings first.
