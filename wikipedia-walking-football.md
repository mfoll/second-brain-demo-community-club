# Walking football

## Summary

A short article on walking football, a slower form of association football created in 2011 and now played by many teams in several countries. The lead is brief; the body describes the basic idea that players must walk rather than run, with reduced contact.

## Why It Matters

I read it before the [[walking-football-trial-session-2026-05-20]] so Donovan and I could explain the format without inventing rules. It confirmed the three things we told people on the night: no running, no contact, ball kept low. It informed [[should-we-start-walking-football]].

## Caveats

Very thin on how clubs actually organise sessions or insure them. Our questions about pitch slots and leaders are not answered here. Article last edited 2026-10-14 when checked; licence CC BY-SA 4.0.
