# Membership Fee Review

Setting fees for the 2026-27 season after the council raised pitch hire by 12% and the club committed to building match funding for the changing rooms.

## Outcome

2026-27 fees set by board vote on 10 March 2026: juniors £140 to £165, adults £190 to £210. Done.

## Current State

- Completed. The board voted 6-1 on 10 March 2026 ([[board-meeting-2026-03-10]]). [[ravinder-sahota]] voted against and asked for his dissent to be minuted.
- The compromise was the confidential [[hardship-bursary-fund]], set up on 16 March 2026.
- Junior membership fell from 168 to 161 for 2026-27. We do not yet know how much of that is the fee ([[did-the-fee-rise-cost-us-members]]).
- Ravinder's sliding scale is not dead; it comes back in January 2027 ([[should-fees-use-a-sliding-scale]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

Annual fees only. Match subs, kit and tournament entry stayed as they were. The bursary became its own project.

## Working Context

The pressures, from [[club-budget-2026-27]] and [[pitch-hire-increase-letter-2026]]: pitch hire up from £10,000 to £11,200; affiliation and referee costs up; reserves about to drop by £25,000 for [[match-funding]]. Fees had last risen in 2023 ([[fee-history]]).

[[claire-whitlow]] and Ravinder wrote the options paper together ([[fee-review-options-paper-2026]]):

- A: inflation only (juniors £150, adults £200). Covers costs, rebuilds nothing.
- B: juniors £165, adults £210. Covers costs and puts about £5,800 a season back towards reserves. Chosen.
- C: juniors £180. Rejected by everyone.
- D: Ravinder's sliding scale, three junior bands of £120, £165 and £190 by household circumstances ([[ravinder-sliding-scale-proposal]]).

Ravinder's argument: 23 families asked for payment plans in 2025-26 ([[payment-plan-requests-2025-26]]), so a flat rise would push some children out. The majority's argument: a sliding scale needs us to assess household income, which a volunteer club cannot do fairly or privately, and it was too late to build for September. The bursary was the middle way, and Ravinder agreed to run it while still arguing for the scale.

The [[members-survey-2025]] showed fees were the second concern after communications, which we took seriously.
