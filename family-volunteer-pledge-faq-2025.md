# Family Volunteer Pledge FAQ 2025

> [!warning]
> Archived. This FAQ described the compulsory pledge and £30 opt-out, which the board scrapped on 10 February 2026. It was replaced by the letter in [[letter-to-families-on-the-pledge-2026-02]] and the role descriptions in [[volunteer-role-descriptions]].

## Summary

The FAQ sent to all junior families on 11 August 2025 with the 2025-26 registration link, after the [[pledge-vote-2025-07-15]]. Written by me and checked by [[nisha-raghavan]].

## Key Details

- Every junior family asked to cover three volunteer sessions in the 2025-26 season. One family with two children still owed three sessions, not six.
- Sessions bookable in the new Clubfold rota.
- Families who could not volunteer could pay £30, added to fees at registration.
- Coaches, team managers and board members counted as having met the pledge; athletics-only families exempt.
- Money from opt-outs to go to general funds "for match-day costs".
- Review promised in January 2026.

## Why It Matters

Kept as a record of what families were actually told, for anyone reading [[pledge-lessons-learned]] or reconsidering a similar scheme ([[should-volunteering-be-a-condition-of-membership]]).
