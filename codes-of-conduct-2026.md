# Codes of Conduct 2026

The club's four codes of conduct (coaches and volunteers, parents and spectators, junior players, adult players), revised as appendix A of the 2026 policy draft.

## Key Details

- **Coaches and volunteers:** put welfare first; no one-to-one private messages with juniors; no lifts for a single junior who is not your own child unless the parent arranged it; no alcohol at junior events; follow the reporting route ([[report-a-welfare-concern]]).
- **Parents and spectators:** positive touchline behaviour; no abuse of referees; photos of your own child only, and do not post other children ([[can-parents-photograph-their-own-children-at-matches]]).
- **Junior players:** respect teammates, opponents and officials; tell a coach or the welfare officer if something feels wrong.
- **Adult players:** wait outside while juniors change; no phones in changing rooms.

What changed from 2022: the messaging rule, the adult players' code (new), and the photo line.

## Why It Matters

The codes are what volunteers and parents actually read. They carry the [[two-adult-rule]] and the changing-room rules into everyday language.
