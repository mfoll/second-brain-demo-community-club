# Matching funds

## Summary

English Wikipedia article (last edited 18 August 2026 when rechecked). Matching funds are payments made in proportion to money available from other sources, usually in charitable or public-good settings. The lead notes that cost sharing, in-kind and matching contributions are often used interchangeably but are different kinds of support.

## Why It Matters

Read when the Lanternfield Trust's 75% cap made it plain we needed £63,000 of our own ([[match-funding]]). The distinction between cash match and in-kind contributions mattered: we checked with the trust and confirmed that volunteer time would not count for this fund, so the £63,000 had to be money, which is why the board committed reserves on 12 May 2026 ([[board-meeting-2026-05-12]]).

## Caveats

General background only; each funder sets its own rules, and ours are in [[lanternfield-community-facilities-fund]].
