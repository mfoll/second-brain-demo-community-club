# Pitch Lease Extension

Replace the Millrace Lane lease, which ends on 31 March 2031, with a 30-year lease so the Lanternfield Trust's 25-year security of tenure rule is met.

## Outcome

A 30-year lease of Millrace Lane approved by council cabinet, giving the 25 years' tenure the Lanternfield Trust requires.

## Current State

- Heads of terms for a 30-year lease were agreed with Graham Sutcliffe's estates team in July 2026 ([[millrace-lane-lease]]): 1 April 2027 to 31 March 2057, rent £1,500 a year reviewed every five years, the club to repair the pavilion and any new building, the council to keep the pitches' main drains.
- Leases over 10 years need cabinet. Cabinet was due to approve it on Thursday 17 September 2026 and deferred it to 19 November pending its [[community-asset-review]] ([[council-cabinet-deferral-2026-09-17]]).
- The trust's deadline for evidence is 15 December 2026 ([[lanternfield-tenure-deadline-email-2026-10-09]]).
- Graham says the report is unchanged and officers still recommend approval. Rhys Penhallow is pressing for it to be first on the November agenda.

## Scope and Boundaries

The lease and the council's decision on it. Pitch hire charges are part of the conversation but are billed separately each season ([[pitch-hire-schedule-2026-27]]). Incorporation (whether the club should sign as a charitable company rather than through trustees) is a separate question in [[club-incorporation-review]], though the lease may force the timing.

## Working Context

When we started in November 2025, I thought this would be paperwork. The council already wanted community groups on longer leases, and the club has been at Millrace Lane since 1981. The first meeting with Graham on 12 November 2025 ([[lease-meeting-2025-11-12]]) went well; the sticking points were repairs and drainage, not the length.

Repairs: the council's first draft made us responsible for everything, including pitch drainage. Gareth and Marek costed relaying the drains on pitch 2 alone at well over £20,000. We agreed in July 2026 that the club repairs buildings and does routine pitch work, and the council keeps the main drains ([[repairing-obligations]], [[should-the-club-take-on-pitch-drainage-under-the-new-lease]]).

Signing: as an unincorporated association, the lease would be signed by holding trustees. Claire and Femi are uneasy about personal liability over 30 years. That is now part of [[club-incorporation-review]], but I do not want it to delay the cabinet decision; the council has said it can grant the lease to trustees and accept an assignment later.

The cabinet deferral was not about us. The council started a review of every community lease on its land in August 2026 and cabinet did not want to approve new long leases mid-review. Officers have since told us the review is about rent levels and community use clauses, and our heads of terms already have a community use clause. My notes from each conversation are in [[lease-negotiation-notes]].
