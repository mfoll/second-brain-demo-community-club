# Pavilion Roof Repair

Stop the pavilion roof leaking into the meeting room and kitchen, for as little as we could responsibly spend while the bigger changing rooms question was open.

## Outcome

Leaking pavilion roof patched for £6,800 from reserves, buying about five years. Reached on 18 July 2025.

## Current State

- Completed. The felt over the meeting room and kitchen was replaced and the flashings round the old chimney stack redone. The prefab changing block roof was not touched because it contains asbestos cement sheets and is due to come down anyway ([[asbestos-survey-report-2025]]).
- No leaks reported since, including through the wet winter of 2025-26.
- Marek checks the roof each October from the ladder and after any named storm. Next proper look due in 2029 or 2030.

## Scope and Boundaries

Meeting room and kitchen roof only. Not the changing block, not the guttering on the car park side (Gareth cleared it in May 2025 as routine), not the electrics that got wet (checked by an electrician under a separate £240 job).

## Working Context

The roof went badly in the storm on 22 February 2025 ([[pavilion-roof-leak-2025-02-22]]). Water came through the meeting room ceiling over the board table, and we lost a box of old minutes and two trophy plinths. The board met in the kitchen in March.

Marek got three quotes ([[pavilion-roof-quotes-2025]]): £6,800 to replace the felt and flashings over the two rooms, £14,200 for a new warm roof over the whole building including the prefab, and £3,100 for patching only, with no guarantee. The board chose the middle way on 11 March 2025: a proper repair to the part we will keep, nothing on the part we plan to replace. The £14,200 option would have spent money on a block we were already hoping to demolish, and the patch would have bought one winter at most.

It came from reserves, which Claire noted at the time brought them down to about £31,000. That figure matters later, because £25,000 of it is now committed to [[match-funding]].

Work was booked for the close season so the pavilion could stay open for junior matches. The roofer started on 7 July and finished on 18 July 2025 ([[roof-repair-completed-2025-07-18]]). Building control was not needed for a like-for-like repair.
