# How Do We Recognise Volunteers Without Money?

## Question

How can a club with £48,000 turnover and every spare pound going to match funding thank volunteers in a way that actually helps them stay, including the occasional helpers who never come to the thank-you evening?

## What We Know

- The [[volunteer-thank-you-evening-2025-11-21]] reached about two thirds of regulars and almost none of the occasional helpers. Two people said they dislike public thanks.
- The exit conversations ([[volunteer-exit-conversations-2025]]) suggest people leave when they feel taken for granted more than when they feel unthanked in public.
- Our budget for recognition is £450 a year, set by [[claire-whitlow]], and I do not want to grow it while we are raising the appeal.

## Current Thinking

A mix of small, personal and specific: a handwritten note from the team's coach at the end of the season, a mention in the newsletter for people who are happy with that, long-service mentions at the AGM rather than the evening, and first refusal on tournament places for volunteers' families. The list I keep adding to is [[volunteer-recognition-ideas]].

## What Would Answer It

A season of trying three or four of these, and asking in the end-of-season form whether volunteers felt appreciated. I would like an answer by the 2027 AGM so it is in the handover to the next chair.
