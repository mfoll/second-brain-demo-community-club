# Photovoltaic system

## Summary

Describes a photovoltaic (solar power) system: solar panels converting sunlight to electricity, an inverter turning direct current into alternating current, plus mounting and cabling. Large systems may track the sun.

## Why It Matters

Read the day after the [[solar-panel-quote-2026-03]] arrived so I could understand what the installer was selling: 14 panels, an inverter, no battery. It made clear why our evening and winter use matters, since panels produce in daylight. Fed into [[is-solar-worth-it-before-the-new-changing-rooms]].
