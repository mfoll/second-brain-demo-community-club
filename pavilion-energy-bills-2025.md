# Pavilion Energy Bills 2025

Claire Whitlow's summary of the pavilion's 2025 energy bills, pulled from the accounts on 16 February 2026 at my request.

- Electricity: about 14,800 kWh, £2,900. Peaks in October to March from floodlights on the half pitch and the water heater.
- Gas (meeting-room and kitchen heating): £1,100.
- Total about £4,000, up from £3,100 in 2023.
- Floodlights alone are roughly a third of the electricity, estimated from the separate meter on the lighting circuit.

Why it matters: the number any energy idea has to beat. A £600 LED change that saves 10% of electricity pays back in about two years; solar has to be judged against a much longer horizon ([[is-solar-worth-it-before-the-new-changing-rooms]]).

Source location: Treasurer / Utilities in the club shared drive.
