# Aoife Brennan

Club welfare officer and board member since the 2019 AGM, when she took the role over from me. Leads the [[safeguarding-policy-review]].

## Role

- Welfare officer: the named person for any concern about a child or an adult at the club. The confidential route in [[report-a-welfare-concern]] goes to her, or to [[beatriz-ferreira]] as deputy.
- Board member with the safeguarding brief.
- Author of the 2026 draft policy ([[safeguarding-policy-draft-2026-09]]) and the gap list behind it ([[safeguarding-review-gap-list]]).
- Holds Northmere FA welfare officer training, renewed in 2025.

## How We Work Together

Aoife is a primary school teacher, careful and direct. Because I was welfare officer before her, I make a point of not second-guessing her calls; she tells me what the chair needs to know and nothing more, which is right. We meet for half an hour before each board meeting. She writes in short numbered points and prefers comments on a draft to a long email.

She keeps her own confidential records outside Clubfold and outside this vault, as the policy requires. I never ask for detail and nothing about any concern is written here.

## Current Threads

- Revised policy for board adoption on 17 November 2026.
- Chasing the six volunteers not yet trained ([[safeguarding-training-refresh]]).
- Reviewing the interim [[changing-room-supervision-rules]] after half term.
- Asked whether she would stay on as welfare officer beyond the 2027 AGM: yes for one more year, then she wants Beatriz to take over.

## Background With the Club

Aoife came to the club as a parent in 2014 and helped with the under-8s before training as deputy to me in 2017. When I became chair at the 2019 AGM she took over as welfare officer, and she joined the board at the same time. She ran the club's switch to the 2022 policy and has handled the welfare side of two seasons of growth in the girls' section.
