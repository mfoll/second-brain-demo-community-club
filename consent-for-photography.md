# Consent for Photography

The principle that the club photographs and publishes images of a junior only with a parent's recorded, informed, renewable and withdrawable consent.

## What Each Word Means for Us

- **Recorded:** a yes/no field in Clubfold at registration, not a verbal "that's fine" to a coach ([[photo-consent-form-2026]]).
- **Informed:** the form says exactly where photos may appear (club website, newsletter, club social media, local news through the club) and that names are never used.
- **Renewable:** asked every season, because circumstances change.
- **Withdrawable:** any time, by a message to the welfare officer; photos already posted are taken down within a week.

No answer counts as no.

## Why It Matters

Some families have reasons, which they never need to explain, why their child must not appear online. A team manager needs to know quietly before kick-off, which is why [[linh-pham]] keeps a per-team "no photo" list. The general idea of informed consent ([[wikipedia-informed-consent]]) comes from medicine, which I knew well from nursing; the club version is lighter but rests on the same respect for the person's choice.

## Limits

It covers the club's own photos. Parents' photos at matches are covered by a request, not consent ([[can-parents-photograph-their-own-children-at-matches]]).
