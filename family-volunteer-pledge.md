# Family Volunteer Pledge

A rule that every junior family covers three volunteer sessions a season or pays a £30 opt-out. Superseded by [[volunteer-recruitment-drive-2026]] after the board scrapped the opt-out on 10 February 2026.

## Outcome

Every junior family covers three volunteer sessions a season or pays a £30 opt-out. Not reached; the project was replaced before the end of its first season.

## Current State

- Superseded on 10 February 2026 at the [[board-meeting-2026-02-10]].
- The pledge survives only as a voluntary ask on the membership form: "we hope every family can help three times a season".
- The £930 of opt-out money already collected was moved to the [[hardship-bursary-fund]].
- Successor: [[volunteer-recruitment-drive-2026]], built on Nisha's [[volunteer-role-descriptions]].

> [!info]
> Short answer to "did we drop the pledge?": partly. We dropped the £30 opt-out fee and the compulsion in February 2026, kept the three-sessions pledge as a voluntary request, and moved the money already collected to the hardship bursary.

## Scope and Boundaries

Junior families only; adult members were never asked. Coaches and team managers were counted as having met the pledge.

## Working Context

The board agreed the pledge on 15 July 2025 ([[pledge-vote-2025-07-15]]) after a summer where three Saturday fixtures went ahead with no tea hut and one under-12s match was nearly called off for want of a linesman. The vote was 7-2. The idea was simple: about 150 junior families, three sessions each, would cover the gaps several times over.

What happened, from the January review presented to the board on 13 January 2026 ([[board-meeting-2026-01-13]]; Nisha's review in [[pledge-review-2026-01-09]], data in [[pledge-review-data-january-2026]]):

- Only 44% of families had done at least one session by mid-January.
- 31 families paid the £30 opt-out, £930 in total.
- Two families left the club and said the pledge was part of the reason.
- Coaches reported that some parents who had paid felt they had "bought out" and stopped helping informally, which they had done before. See [[motivation-crowding-out]].
- Chasing sessions took Nisha about four hours a week.

The board discussed three options on 10 February 2026: keep it and enforce harder, drop it entirely, or drop the fee and keep a voluntary ask with better-defined roles. The third won. [[pledge-lessons-learned]] is my own write-up.
