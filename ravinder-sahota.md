# Ravinder Sahota

Membership secretary and board member since 2021. Runs the confidential [[hardship-bursary-fund]].

## Role

- Membership secretary: registrations, renewals and membership records in Clubfold.
- Decides bursary awards with [[claire-whitlow]]; the only person who sees which families receive one.
- Sat on the constitution working group in 2025 ([[constitution-review-2025]]).

## The fee vote

At the board on 10 March 2026 Ravinder voted against raising junior fees from £140 to £165 and asked for his dissent to be minuted ([[board-meeting-2026-03-10]], [[minuted-dissent]]). His reasons, as he gave them: 23 families had asked for payment plans in 2025-26 ([[payment-plan-requests-2025-26]]), and a flat rise falls hardest on them. He proposed a sliding scale instead ([[ravinder-sliding-scale-proposal]]).

He then agreed to run the bursary that came out of the compromise, and has run it carefully: 14 awards and £1,680 by October 2026. He still argues for a sliding scale and will bring it back in January 2027 ([[should-fees-use-a-sliding-scale]]).

## How we work together

Ravinder is precise and does his homework; when he disagrees he brings numbers. I value that he lost the vote and then made the compromise work. He prefers email to calls and wants papers in good time.
