# Annual general meeting

## Summary

English Wikipedia article (last edited 12 October 2026 when rechecked). Its lead section is a single sentence: an annual general meeting is a meeting of an organisation's general membership.

## Why It Matters

I looked it up in April 2026 while planning the 2026 AGM, mainly to check whether there was anything we routinely leave out. There was not much to learn from so short a lead, but it was a useful reminder of whose meeting it is: the members', not the board's. That is why the 2026 papers included a plain fees explainer ([[agm-2026-papers]]) and why the 2027 AGM will hear the co-chair question as a members' decision ([[agm-2027-planning-notes]]).

## Caveats

Very thin as a source. Our own rules on notice, quorum and voting are in the [[fernhollow-constitution-2025]].
