# Lead The Volunteers

Everything the club does depends on about 35 people giving up evenings and Saturday mornings. This area covers how we find them, how we organise them, how we thank them and how we stop the same handful from carrying everything. It has no end date: there will always be a rota to fill and someone new to welcome.

## Scope

- The volunteer rota (Clubfold since August 2025), match-day and training cover, tea hut, kit, line marking.
- Recruitment, role descriptions, one-off slots and induction of new volunteers.
- Recognition: the thank-you evening, long-service mentions, small gestures.
- Watching for burnout and spreading the load.
- Not here: safeguarding training and background checks for volunteers, which sit under [[keep-children-safe]]; coaching qualifications and team allocations, which sit under [[run-the-season]].
