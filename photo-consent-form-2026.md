# Photo Consent Form 2026

The photo consent question added to junior registration in Clubfold on 15 July 2026, and the aggregate answers for 2026-27 as of 1 October 2026.

## The Question

"Do you consent to the club taking and publishing photos or video in which your child may appear, on the club website, newsletter, club social media and local news supplied by the club? Names are never published. You can change your answer at any time by messaging the welfare officer." Yes / No.

## Aggregate Answers

| Answer | Juniors |
|---|---|
| Yes | 138 |
| No | 17 |
| Not answered (treated as no) | 6 |
| **Total** | **161** |

## Why It Matters

The per-team no-photo lists come from this field. Team managers see only "photo: no" against a player in their own team, never a reason.

## Source Location

Clubfold, Members, registration field "Photo consent 2026-27". Report access: welfare officers and the photography volunteer.
